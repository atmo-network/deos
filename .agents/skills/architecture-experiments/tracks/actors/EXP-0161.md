## Decision Question

Which single physical design carries current-state Actor semantics — process ownership, discovery, residence, rounds, wake acknowledgment, lifecycle and resource ownership — without legacy snapshot or Ready-queue authority?

## Context and Candidates

- `Current architecture`: The released 0.7.26 engine moved whole-cell frames through a ticket FIFO with Opening snapshots, latches, successor tickets and waiting-primary copies, coupling unrelated lifetimes.
- `Hypothesis`: One stable Actor-generation process with generation-bound memberships, one Live/Pending ring and the retained C32 deadline substrate carries every supported state with bounded structural work and no legacy authority.
- `Materiality`: Each choice must remove a named structural cost or hazard (whole-population scan, predecessor discovery, duplicate residence, lost wakeup, unowned cleanup) without semantic change; no throughput benefit is claimed.
- `Controlled / changed variables`: Semantics fixed by the Actors specification; one candidate per decision family, each with one smallest falsifier. Candidate sets were frozen and exhausted before selection.

## Workloads and Criteria

- `Evidence class`: Design-level structural analysis, finite interleaving matrices and native regressions; no production benchmark.
- `Acceptance criteria`: Every supported state has exactly one semantic owner and at most one residence; every transition has a bounded plan; every hazard has an invariant and a finite falsifier.
- `Rejection criteria`: Any required population, predecessor or deadline-range scan; duplicate or zero residence; lost acknowledgment; custody mutation by scheduling.

## Measurements

Structural maxima of the composed design; these are encoded-size bounds, not trie or production-cost measurements.

| Physical owner | Selected content and bound |
| --- | --- |
| `Process[ActorId]` | One current generation, accepted plan, typed status, sparse run/current basis, residence discriminator, Q1 and reverse handles; carriers hold no authoritative process copy |
| `ServiceHeader` | Global Live/Pending count, persistent next-encounter cursor and immutable block-round identity |
| `ServiceNode[ActorId]` | Generation, previous/next `ActorRef`, kind, eligibility and considered markers; 41-byte reference payload plus round widths |
| Deadline substrate | `Option<ActorRef>` is 17 bytes; one C32 slot vector is 544 bytes plus prefix/page metadata; paged min-heaps index every nonempty Block/Tick/review key without range scan |
| Park ownership | Process-owned generation/plan certificate plus bounded exact-source pages and reverse handles; incomplete domains also own timed review |
| `ActorRef` | `(actor_id: u64, generation: u64)`, 16 fixed SCALE bytes |

| Structural operation | Bounded plan |
| --- | --- |
| Retained Live Step | Update process/run/Q1 and advance to stored next; allocate/delete no node or successor ticket |
| Admission/unlink | Insert before cursor with neighbor patches; removal reads the reverse handle, patches at most two neighbors and repairs the cursor |
| Residence movement | Stage destination, linearize once, release source; full destination preserves source; deadline movement touches one C32 page plus a bounded heap path |
| Wake | Traverse a fixed revision/page target, hand off durable Pending, acknowledge only the covered revision |
| Close | Revoke, exact-unlink, reclaim synchronously within bounded close; custody untouched |

## Result

Every supported current-state transition has one semantic owner, at most one residence and a bounded structural plan. Per-Step Ready dequeue, successor ticket allocation, Ready-page growth, waiting-primary copies and latch rearm are structurally eliminated. Asynchronous cleanup manifests and debt were designed but the shipped implementation closes synchronously within one bounded close, so O11 no longer describes current architecture.

## Interpretation

The design removes structural hazards, not measured cost: the eliminated work is replaced by ring, reverse-handle and deadline maintenance whose frequency and total cost only the frozen workloads in [EXP-0162](./EXP-0162.md) and generated owners can establish.

## Decision

Accepted as the current Actors physical baseline. Decision scope: physical architecture only. Outcome class: research result. Finite stopping basis: every necessary choice has one selected mechanism and one smallest falsifier, no falsifier is observed, and no alternative can add evidence before realization.

### Pull-Model Amendment (2026-09-26)

The pull-model cut (commit `5d33ddf`) removed push-reactive observation Triggers, Oracle-to-Actors ingress, shared-feed fanout, Crossing indexes and observation-gated Park. Observations are read only by fresh-only Step predicates at the check or Attempt.

- `O2`: Discovery sources are now Manual and certified AddressEvent lookup, AtTime/Cadenced deadlines, and parked-balance revisions. For parked balance, only a configured asset's checked absolute net movement from the fixed final parking anchor reaching `max(authored minimum, 100 × asset minimum)` qualifies; subthreshold changes stay parked and create no check.
- `O3`: Predicate applicability alone never returns a parked-balance Actor; a qualifying signal creates at most one check while not busy, and completion either Parks with a new anchor or returns under round rules.
- `O4`: The parked-balance episode is the only live Park producer. Its certificate also binds watched assets, the fixed anchor, direction and inclusive threshold; a negative rearm keeps the anchor. Native/fungibles adapters publish coalesced revisions and timed review covers other routes. The unreachable non-episode negative reasons are `Actors/Pull-Model Residue`.
- `O8`: A negative parked-balance check may acknowledge the covered revision but never replaces the anchor.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: A bounded, contradiction-free physical design for current-state semantics under the governing specification.
- `Does not establish`: Production Weight soundness, fairness under load, throughput, or net benefit over the released engine.
- `Invalidation triggers`: A change to residence, round, acknowledgment, lifecycle or custody semantics; a new discovery source; a reachable scan or duplicate residence.

## Next Gradient
