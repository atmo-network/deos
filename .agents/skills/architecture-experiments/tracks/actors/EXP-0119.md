## Decision Question

Does the converged final `0.7.26` tree reproduce the sealed Checkpoint A end-to-end results across the frozen representative workload set — the rank-1 temporal drain workload, the full schedule campaigns, the funded crossing cohort, retry/lifecycle, tombstone pressure, heavy market and liquidity effects, and the production identity replay — at the unchanged production Wasm/Weight/metadata identities, so that Phase 2 closes with no adopted optimization and no cross-workload regression, or does any confirmed difference remain?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `BACKLOG.md` P10.3 owns the final end-to-end comparison and its report fields; P10.2 owns the binding reassessment; `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` owns two-axis reporting and comparative classification; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` owns the FIFO/Q1, causal, cutoff, lifecycle and custody semantics the fixtures assert.
- `Invariant`: The comparison changes no semantic, admission, reservation, FIFO/Q1/cutoff or artifact rule; Checkpoint A and final `0.7.26` are the same measured objects on the artifact-bearing surface, and no claim extends beyond the measured workload, population and horizon.
- `Reopen trigger`: A confirmation row failing, a production artifact identity moving from the P10.2-confirmed binding (`BACKLOG.md` P10.2), or a changed fixture, toolchain, runtime constant or source identity.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: Phase 2 measured the complete Actor resource anatomy on Checkpoint A (P7.2–P7.10; [workload ledgers](./experiments.md#p73-frequency-weighted-resource-ledgers-0726-phase-2), [resource synthesis](./experiments.md#p710-resource-anatomy-synthesis-0726-phase-2)), ranked the bottlenecks (P8; [single-redesign freeze](./experiments.md#p84-single-redesign-freeze-0726-phase-2)), tested the frozen single-redesign set and rejected every candidate ([EXP-0117](./EXP-0117.md), [EXP-0118](./EXP-0118.md)), converged the losing bridge and diagnostics back to the pre-campaign code state (P10.1), and confirmed the production binding on the converged tree with a bit-exact Wasm rebuild (P10.2).
- `Reason`: The P10.3 charter requires one final comparison on the converged tree: repeat the workload whose bottleneck selected the redesign, rerun the fixed representative regression set needed to establish no cross-workload regression, compare Checkpoint A with final `0.7.26`, and retain `v0.7.25` as historical context where comparable.
- `Hypothesis`: Because the artifact-bearing tree is byte-identical to Checkpoint A and the production Wasm rebuilt bit-exactly at P10.2, every frozen workload reproduces its recorded Checkpoint A values on the final tree with zero cross-workload regression, and Phase 2 closes as a research result with no adopted optimization.
- `Materiality`: Declared by `BACKLOG.md` P10.3 before the runs: the comparison decides whether the no-optimization disposition holds on the final tree; a single failed fixture, moved identity or non-reproduced recorded value would reopen the disposition.

## Result

All ten confirmation rows are green on the final tree at the unchanged production identity. F1 reproduces the recorded rank-1 reading exactly (1,223 blocks, 10,005 Cycles, the same step histogram, 9,997 occurrences, head 10,005 / tail 14,997). The C phase-attribution fixture reproduces its nine per-block rows exactly in both arms with the prepared cutoff assertion. E reclaims every declared prefix exactly, B completes the same 48-member cohort at the same horizons and spans, D and its declared fixture reproduce rollback and retry semantics, F2 reproduces the 12-of-100 ProofSize-bound frontier, the full A/C campaigns reproduce 1,694 and 132 committed Cycles, and the production reference block replays through the exact Wasm with a verified storage proof.

## Interpretation

The comparison is an identity confirmation, and the measured evidence is consistent with that reading at every level: the artifact-bearing surface is byte-identical to Checkpoint A, the source identity recorded by every confirmation run equals the P7.3 source identity, the production Wasm rebuilt bit-exactly, and every deterministic value, counter, proof total and assertion reproduced its recorded Checkpoint A owner. The only recorded difference is a sub-`0.001 %` transcription drift in two derived prose totals of the matched C fixture whose per-block rows, counts and proof totals agree exactly; it is classified as a recorded-total transcription scope, not a behavioral difference, and the verified fresh sums are used. Nothing in the comparison indicates a cross-workload regression or an unmeasured improvement: no production Weight, execution path, semantic contract or artifact identity moved during Phase 2, so the Phase-2 outcome is a research result and the rank-1 bottleneck plus the EXP-0117 under-coverage finding remain the starting evidence for any future scope decision.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: That the converged final `0.7.26` tree reproduces the recorded Checkpoint A values across the frozen representative workload set at the unchanged production Wasm/Weight/metadata identity; that all ten confirmation rows succeed under their owning identity guards; and that no cross-workload regression exists on the measured set.
- `Does not establish`: Herd-scale or steady-state behavior beyond the declared fixtures; any wall-clock or host-independent latency claim; the conditional or unmeasured matrix rows; the final release-tree assurance (P10.6) or public-truth reconciliation (P10.5).

- `Invalidation triggers`: A changed fixture, population, horizon, success semantics, runtime constant, toolchain, Weight, Wasm or metadata identity; a failing confirmation row; or a public-truth reconciliation finding a retained claim without an evidence owner.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: When a bounded optimization campaign closes without an adopted change, the strongest possible final comparison is an identity confirmation on the frozen set — the measurement work then has value as proof that the anatomy, ranking and rejection evidence still hold on the release tree, not as a performance result.
