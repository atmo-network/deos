## Result

The accepted campaign results compose into one adopted efficiency improvement with explicitly stated new costs and retained research results. On the funded User ObservationCrossing→Transfer workload, the retained pair-owner fallback switches the Crossing admission mode at the shared materialization grant: the steady three-block materialization cycle rises `12 → 16` at unchanged cycle ProofSize with a runtime-bound premium per additional candidate ([EXP-0110](./EXP-0110.md)), and at 48-member witness scale the same cohort completes earlier — completion horizon `12` against the released `14`, completion tail `12` against `14`, mean `9.46 → 8.67` — with exactly-once materialization, `S = A = 48`, zero censoring and zero faults, and the complete declared regression set green ([EXP-0115](./EXP-0115.md)).

The classification of every composed result is:

- `Resource-efficiency record`: the retained pair-owner fallback as the single production admission path ([EXP-0110](./EXP-0110.md), converged by [EXP-0111](./EXP-0111.md)) — fewer charged Control resources over the fixed window for the same completed funded-user work under unchanged guarantees.
- `Observed service improvement`: the funded User cohort completion horizon and latency improvement ([EXP-0115](./EXP-0115.md)); the applicable population is the declared 48-member witness, not a herd or steady-state guarantee.
- `Explicit tradeoffs`: a runtime-bound premium per additional admitted candidate with settled per-candidate cost still below the released per-candidate cost ([EXP-0110](./EXP-0110.md)); deeper maximum queue span `22 → 29` as the mechanism of earlier placement ([EXP-0115](./EXP-0115.md)); a retained rather than regenerated production binding, with the pinned generated Weight kept after two inadmissible full generations ([EXP-0112](./EXP-0112.md)); and an added honest owner of `1,467,000 / 0` charged once per unit (about 0.2% of the branch owner) as a resource-soundness correction ([EXP-0114](./EXP-0114.md)).
- `Useful negative findings`: the executed materialization rate follows the shared family rotation with a per-block ceiling of `MaxCrossingTransitionsPerBlock`, so the retired `10,000`/`100` horizon is policy-bound rather than materialization-bound ([EXP-0103](./EXP-0103.md)); the component counter is not the operative stop — reservation-fit against the rotated family grant decides each capped phase ([EXP-0104](./EXP-0104.md)); the finite candidate set stops at the first material candidate, leaving the count-parameterized owner and the demand-aware family minima unadmitted ([EXP-0105](./EXP-0105.md)); and the binding's ownership completeness was a real bounded gap before its correction ([EXP-0113](./EXP-0113.md)).
- `Non-dominated alternatives retained`: the count-parameterized admitted-batch owner and the demand-aware family-minimum reservation remain preserved, unadmitted evidence in [EXP-0105](./EXP-0105.md); a new candidate requires a new explicit scope decision and is not activated by this disposition.
- `Progress ownership`: the retained progress belongs to a component-level admission path (`select_crossing_admission` placed-batch fallback) and materializes through the integrated funded-User workload; the admission domain is ObservationCrossing materialization only; lifecycle create/update/close cost and repeated-use behavior are unchanged; the tooling-bound results are the Benchmark Reassessment Protocol application and the test-side accepted-identity rebinding.

No other workload is averaged into this frontier: the detector-scale pressure profiles, schedule and geometry controls, liquidity and Burn witnesses, and contention campaigns keep their own scopes in their child records and are not scored as throughput.

## Interpretation

The campaign's binding dimension was ProofSize at the shared materialization grant: the generated pair costs the same as the scalar single, so the fallback switched the admission mode without taking additional guaranteed capacity, and the runtime-bound premium per extra candidate is the bounded new cost. The composed frontier is therefore workload-qualified — an end-to-end improvement for the dense same-threshold funded User Crossing cohort at the declared witness scale on the retained binding — not a universal percentage, block-time or Actor-throughput claim.

The retained result is Pareto-relevant on its measured domain: more admitted candidates per lean-phase decision, an earlier completion wavefront and lower charged Control totals, against a bounded per-candidate premium and a deeper Ready queue span under the same fairness rule. The P3 research results explain why the gain is bounded: the per-block ceiling is the frozen shared-family grant and the operative stop is reservation fit, so no per-candidate optimization can raise the mean beyond the counter cap. The binding correction adds honesty rather than capacity: it charges a previously unowned, storage-free segment once per unit while metadata and every other per-owner identity stay unchanged.

The negative findings close the campaign's own questions rather than opening new ones: the retired `10,000`/`100` horizon stays closed, the count-parameterized owner and demand-aware minima remain available only under a new explicit scope decision, and the released baseline keeps its sealed reference authority. Public-truth reconciliation (P6.2) and final-tree assurance (P6.3) remain outside this record.

## Decision

Accepted. The finite `0.7.26` campaign is terminated by this disposition — outcome class **efficiency improvement** for the retained pair-owner fallback on the funded User ObservationCrossing→Transfer workload, with the explicit tradeoffs and retained research results recorded above. Finite stopping basis: the declared EXP-0105 materiality is met and the named obligations are satisfied — the candidate set stopped at its first material candidate, the end-to-end comparison improved the completion horizon with the complete declared regression set green, and the production binding is bound and owner-complete. The campaign is terminal for `0.7.26`: no new optimization is activated, and a further candidate requires a new explicit scope decision.

The retained changes justify the `0.7.26` checkpoint release. This record decides the disposition only; publication remains the P6.3 gate, and P6.2 owns reconciliation of package and root architecture and bilingual Wiki claims to the measured form. No tag, push or release action is taken here.
