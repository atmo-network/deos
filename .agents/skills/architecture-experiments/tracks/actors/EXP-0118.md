## Decision Question

Does the [P9.4-selected](./experiments.md#p94-one-campaign-stop--next-candidate-decision-0726-phase-2) class-C deadline-order representation — a bounded multi-way replacement of the binary min-heap cursor behind the same drain interface — cut the qualified deep-cursor retained unit to at most `15_892` proof with coverage held, authorizing Reading 2, or must candidate 5 be rejected under the frozen stop condition and the frozen six-candidate set close as a research result?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `BACKLOG.md` P9.5 owns the deadline-order falsifier and P10.1 the convergence of losing candidate code; the [P9.4 One-Campaign Stop](./experiments.md#p94-one-campaign-stop--next-candidate-decision-0726-phase-2) section owns the selected candidate's seven declared fields, absolute coverage-held targets and stop condition; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` owns the temporal drain semantics; `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` owns component-wise admission and reservation-before-mutation.
- `Invariant`: exactly one due occurrence recognized and materialized once; strict deadline order; FIFO substitution; Q1; the causal floor, captured cutoff, atomicity, rollback, custody, continuation and fee ownership unchanged; the funded close/fault reservation stays funded; boundedness (`MaxActiveActors`, page bounds, cursor length) and `check_integrity` hold.
- `Reopen trigger`: a finding that the representation change requires changing semantics, ordering, admission authority or another protected guarantee.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: the cursor is a binary min-heap over 32-slot pages (`WakeupCursorPages`, `ActorWaitingCursorIndices`, insert/remove sifts, height bound 14 at `MaxActiveActors = 10_000`); EXP-0117 attributed the `38_506`-proof deep excess (`47_296 − 8_790`, `+33` reads / `+21` writes) to roughly 13 heap levels for the cadence-rearm insert and its latch-disarm.
- `Reason`: P9.4 selected candidate 5 because its owner is the cursor heap maintenance that creates the deep excess; its saving envelope (up to `38_506`) contains the required first cut (`31_404`), and the node encoding decides how much of the envelope is realised.
- `Hypothesis`: a 32-ary min-heap cursor (fanout = `WakeupPageSize`, height at most 3 at `MaxActiveActors = 10_000`) with explicit slot-granular node updates behind the same drain interface cuts the qualified deep retained unit to at most `15_892` proof while preserving every protected guarantee.
- `Materiality`: the frozen P9.4 absolute coverage-held targets — Retain at most `15_892` / `14_548` / `13_096` / `12_307`; Remove at most `60_891`; the primary gate is the deep Retain estimate at most `15_892` (δ at least `518` against the recorded composed `16_410`).

## Result

- W1: the shallow retained control charges `8_790` proof (`2_850` measured) — bit-identical to EXP-0117, confirming the encoding change does not touch depth-1 drain work.
- W2: the deep retained unit charges `16_579` proof (`5_689` measured; 35 r / 20 w) against the binary `47_296` — a `30_717`-proof cut (`64.9%` of the unit, `79.77%` of the `38_506` deep excess), but `687` proof above the frozen `15_892` target and `169` proof above the recorded composed `16_410`.
- W3: the deep Tick remove unit charges `28_238` proof (`7_448` measured; 47 r / 28 w) against the binary `82_856` — a `54_618`-proof cut (`65.9%`) and `32_653` proof below the frozen `60_891` target.
- Both deep legs passed their internal drain-sequence assertions (one scanned entry, one ready entry, occupancy/length/pointer postconditions) on every run.

## Interpretation

The multi-way node encoding realises most of the P9.4 saving envelope but not the first coverage-held cut. The binary carrier paid roughly 13 heap levels per rearm insert and latch-disarm; the 32-ary carrier pays at most three levels with a bounded child scan, removing `79.77%` of the measured deep excess. The frozen model's smallest conversion class requires the qualified retained unit at `15_892` proof or below (δ at least `518` against `16_410`); the measured unit is `16_579`, so δ is `−169` and no refusal class converts. Because the frozen conversion is joint, the strong Remove-side result (`+36_070`) does not substitute for the primary gate.

The `687`-proof miss is close enough to tempt a design iteration — a different fanout or a page-aligned child encoding could plausibly recover it — but the falsifier froze one prototype and one measurement cycle with no design iteration, and a follow-on encoding is a new candidate requiring a new explicit scope decision. The result therefore stands as measured: the class-C deadline-order representation is a real but insufficient lever at its declared single encoding.

The result also re-prices the deep paths under the bridge: the recorded composed Retain owner over-covers the multi-way path by `169` and the composed Remove owner over-covers by `36_070`. That is a property of the rejected candidate, not of the shipped baseline; the shipped binary carrier retains EXP-0117's under-coverage finding.

## Decision

Rejected. Outcome class: **research result** — the declared candidate is rejected under the predeclared stop condition and the frozen six-candidate set is exhausted; no performance record is claimed. Finite stopping basis: the P9.4 stop condition — the qualified deep retained estimate (`16_579`) is above `15_892`, so Reading 2 is not authorized; candidates 1 and 5 are rejected by measurement, candidates 2–4 are consumed by recorded bounds, and candidate 6 stays gated behind an explicit trade declaration, so the campaign closes with the explicit no-optimization disposition. No production Weight, composition, execution path or artifact identity changes; `77eaf529` remains the released baseline and P10.1 removes the losing bridge code.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: the deterministic proof identities and read/write sets of the three qualified legs on the bridge Wasm; the realised cut of the binary deep excess (`30_717` retained, `54_618` remove); the rejection of candidate 5 under the predeclared stop condition; the exhaustion of the frozen six-candidate set and the campaign's no-optimization disposition.
- `Does not establish`: any production Weight replacement; any F1 re-run measurement; that a different fanout, page alignment or ordered directory cannot clear the gate (only that the declared one-prototype 32-ary encoding did not); the full-cursor exhaustion cost under the multi-way carrier; the User fee path or AtTime variants; any global optimum.
- `Known limitations`: System cadenced actors only; the retained deep leg needs one free cursor slot; the fixture installs only the pages the operations can touch; RefTime magnitude is two-run host-conditioned; the adapted carrier-geometry unit test witnesses one upward repair — the deepest reachable within the mock's `1_024`-actor limit — instead of the binary four-level path; the fixture and test adaptations are part of the removable bridge.
- `Invalidation triggers`: a changed drain composition, cursor geometry, page size, fanout, anchor state, fixture convention, toolchain or F1 baseline; a regenerated production binding that moves the recorded composed units.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: cursor height was the dominant contributor (`13` levels → `3`); with it removed, the residual deep cost is dominated by fixed drain work, the bounded two-page child scan and owner-index churn, so any successor must target a child-scan-free ordering rather than another level-count reduction.
