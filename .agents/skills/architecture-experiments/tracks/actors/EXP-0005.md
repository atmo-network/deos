## Decision Question

Which single-owner representation minimizes per-block current-Step control proof and mutation cost without duplicating Step semantics or weakening lifecycle authority?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` → `§3.3`, `§3.5`, `§5.1`, `§6.1`, `§8.2-§8.3`, `§9.3`, and `§13.1`.
- `Invariant`: Preserve strict FIFO, Q1, N+1 eligibility, atomic Step effects, durable prefixes, transactional replacement/cancel/close, and one canonical semantic Step owner.
- `Reopen trigger`: Any candidate requiring stale execution caches, duplicate Step truth, relaxed update invalidation, or a changed queue order reopens Specification and is not an optimization candidate.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: ActorHot, Contract head/tail, split Run head/payload, admission/activation authority, and FIFO ticket are separately proven during service.
- `Reason`: Equal-thirds 10,000-Actor profiles remain Actor-machine constrained at roughly 14.7 committed distinct Actors per saturated block before authority experiments.
- `Hypothesis`: Co-locating frequently co-read authority or transferring current-Step ownership into a commitment-bound RunFrame can materially reduce RefTime/ProofSize per Q1 commit.
- `Materiality`: At least 20% lower complete control Weight or at least 20% more distinct Actors per saturated block, with no larger binding state-hold/lifecycle regression.

## Statistical / Variance Notes

Focused probes use matched candidate Wasm and 50/20 generation. Integrated A2 uses deterministic equal-thirds W1-W3 profiles. A1 and A3 stop before integration because optimistic feasibility or semantic ownership already fails the declared gate.

## Result

A2 was the strongest focused candidate, but complete generated Weight disproves material production impact. Integrated equal-thirds W1/W2/W3 all improve only from block 679 to 658, about 3.2% throughput, below the declared 20% threshold. Zero failures and retained user dispatch establish semantic viability but do not justify production churn; the integrated candidate must be reverted.

A1 combined-owner measurement confirms a local structural win, but equal-thirds production geometry bounds its possible complete-path effect far below materiality. At about 14.7 committed Actors/block, each committed Actor consumes roughly `20.8B / 45.9K` of the saturated Control envelope. Even granting the full isolated read+write/time saving and the full structural proof reduction on every commit yields below 1% RefTime and about 5.4% proof, while RunHead-only access already regresses 10.6% time and 14.1% measured proof. An integrated storage rewrite cannot clear the 20% gate.

A3 has no conforming physical layout. A reusable Contract must retain its authored Step for future Cycles. Materializing that Step in a persistent RunFrame duplicates semantic execution authority, forbidden by §3.5 and §13.1. Moving it destructively from the Contract requires bounded restoration on completion/cancel/close and breaks ordinary one-Step Opening without removing its Contract read. A3 is therefore dominated for W1/W5 and non-conforming for W2/W3.

## Interpretation

A2 is rejected as sub-material, reverted to the separate owner baseline, and retained as evidence only. A1 is rejected before integration because an optimistic composition bound is still far below the declared gate and its RunHead-only path regresses. A3 is rejected because every persistent form duplicates Step truth and every destructive form breaks reusable Contract ownership. The separate A0 authority geometry remains the local Pareto baseline.

## Decision

Retain A0. No candidate materially improves `CommittedStepsPerBlock`, `DistinctActorsProgressedPerBlock`, `ActorMachineCostPerCommittedStep`, or component-wise `UsefulActionWeight / ActorMachineWeight` without a larger proof/lifecycle tradeoff or semantic-authority violation. Close EXP-0005 and move the next independent gradient to EXP-0021 Contract ceiling.
