## Decision Question

Can one local same-semantics vacancy authority remove the maximum reachable C32 page scan while retaining the selected deadline carrier, exact reverse handles, bounded saturation, and transactional source preservation?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §2.3; `template/pallets/actors/docs/architecture.en.md` Deadline/Weight ownership; EXP-0137 Route P.
- `Invariant`: One deadline bucket remains a linked set of nonempty C32 pages under the existing Block/Tick index. Planning selects one exact free slot without mutation; insert, remove, move, refusal, and rollback preserve every source obligation and generation-bound handle.
- `Reopen trigger`: The complete generated insertion/removal/outer-owner ledger exceeds the release resource contract, the vacancy list changes supported deadline semantics, or executable invariant evidence finds an unreachable vacancy or stale link.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Observed trigger`: The implemented linear planner can legally traverse all `ceil(10,000 / 32) = 313` nonempty pages. Its generated maximum ProofSize exceeds the current Actor-control budget before insertion or any surrounding branch runs.
- `Single causal owner`: `plan_deadline_destination`; no other carrier owner or alternative structure is admitted by this record.
- `Hypothesis`: A header vacancy head plus page-local vacancy predecessor/successor handles reduces planning to one header and one C32 page read. Full-page insertion unlinks one vacancy node, removal from a full page links one node, and empty-page removal unlinks both physical and vacancy memberships atomically.
- `Materiality`: The baseline owner alone cannot fit the release budget. The candidate must fit with enough margin for its complete surrounding owner; targeted success is necessary but not sufficient for final acceptance.

## Result

The measured Candidate B reduced maximum destination planning from 314 reads to two and fit the release Actor-control budget with large margin. Post-measurement mutation tests reproduced a truncated-page indexing panic and a missing vacancy head being treated as a full bucket. Shared header validation, bounded page refusal and checked C32 access correct those implementation defects without changing the candidate. Their executed overhead and all complete surrounding owners require fresh generated reconciliation.

`deadline_vacancy_membership_survives_all_page_removal_orders_and_refills` proves exact physical/vacancy membership through all six three-page removal orders, full/nonfull/empty transitions and generation replacement. The move and neighbor-refusal tests bind rollback to complete storage-root equality, including refusal after an earlier neighbor write. The maximum-population test covers both page and member saturation. These are behavioral witnesses of the same vacancy-maintenance invariant, not production capacity measurements.

## Interpretation

The failure was not deadline ordering or C32 capacity; it was absence of direct vacancy authority. Adding one bounded intrusive vacancy membership makes population geometry irrelevant to destination discovery while keeping exact page and Actor handles. This is the finite local page-metadata refinement allowed by EXP-0137, not a second deadline carrier.
