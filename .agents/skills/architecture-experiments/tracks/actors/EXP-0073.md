## Decision Question

Does the finite W5 lifecycle witness distinguish committed progress from failed attempts and terminal control outcomes while preserving its committed prefix on the retained production binding?

## Governing Specification

- `Owners`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` (lifecycle, retry, completion, custody and prefix durability); `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` (complete Pipeline admission and Action accounting); `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` (evidence scope).
- `Invariant`: Q1, committed-Step durability, atomic effects, custody-neutral close and distinct Trigger/Pipeline/Action owners remain unchanged.
- `Reopen trigger`: A concrete semantic violation must be classified, never ratified as a faster lifecycle design.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: Frozen C1 under the accepted EXP-0095 production binding.
- `Hypothesis`: W5's lifecycle timeline reconciles attempts, commits, completion and close without treating suspension or admission refusal as continuously runnable service.
- `Materiality`: One duplicate/compensated prefix Transfer, wrong terminal classification or timeline/state mismatch falsifies this bounded witness.

## Result

The source reports five successful Transfers, two failed middle-Step attempts, three completed Cycles, one failed Cycle and exact retained-prefix custody after retry exhaustion.

## Interpretation

The finite timeline distinguishes committed service, unsuccessful attempts and control-only termination. It does not certify general retry fairness or funded User Opening coverage. Historical “closes” refers to this bounded reporting gap only.

## Decision

Accepted for its bounded current-production claim. The EXP-0095 refresh preserves every lifecycle, terminal and custody outcome while rebinding the quantitative Control/effect and proof values to the current generated Weight and exact Wasm. This does not accept sustained retry fairness, funded User Opening or complete lifecycle coverage.
