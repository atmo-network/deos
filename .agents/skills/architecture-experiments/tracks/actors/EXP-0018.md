## Decision Question

Can one bounded same-block Fresh Step-0 opportunity reduce Trigger-to-first-Step latency relative to T+1 while preserving Q1, deterministic economic ordering, one causal generation per block, bounded block work, strict continuation liveness, and no repeatable Fresh priority?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` → `1.4 Block-Paced Actor Cycles`, `1.5 Causal Readiness`, `4.1 Execution Model`, and `5.1 FIFO and Temporal Readiness`; `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` → `6.1 Control Open and Execution Cutoff`; `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` → `3.4 Fresh Step-0 Timing` and `4.4 Mixed-Length Shared-Trigger Step 0`.
- `Invariant`: Q1; at most one committed Step/Actor/block; one shared Trigger instance defines causal discovery independently of `0..=MaxContractSteps`; bounded Fresh opportunity; actor-produced causes cannot execute in the source block; deterministic order; class/Task/fee/Weight-neutral continuation service; upfront Trigger/Pipeline Machine charge plus current-Action effect fee; no dynamic re-cohorting after first reaction; no unreachable-tail physical tax.
- `Reopen trigger`: Reopen only if measured production evidence proves that protocol-fixed next-block eligibility fails a required economic contract and a bounded alternative preserves FIFO, Q1, non-recursion, and available-Weight honesty.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: Q1 execution, C6 optional inline Step 0, compact causal materialization, loaded-state reuse, typed cause phase/source block, and one protocol-fixed current-block prepass cutoff are implemented. Readiness observed in N cannot execute before N+1; later execution remains governed by FIFO and available `on_idle` Weight. The rejected timing configuration and candidate branches are physically absent.
- `Reason`: First economic reaction is more latency-sensitive than later continuation, but a controlled T+1 versus T0 comparison requires both timing architectures. The implementation must first converge to the specified T+1 prepass cutoff and source provenance; otherwise the experiment would mislabel existing same-block behavior as its baseline.
- `Hypothesis`: A small deterministic Fresh grant over one predeclared external/pre-Actor causal generation can materially reduce first-Step latency without starving Running Actors; unused Fresh/continuation grants remain work-conserving. The null is that phase/cutoff overhead, herd ProofSize, economic-order complexity, or continuation pressure makes T+1 preferable.
- `Materiality`: T0 must materially improve Trigger-to-first-Step latency or staleness under low/normal pressure, preserve a nonzero continuation floor and bounded p99/max service gap under sustained Fresh pressure, fit both Weight dimensions, and add no recursive generation or scheduler ranking.

## Controls

- `Controlled variables`: Q1, C6 Chunk4 with optional inline Step 0, byte-identical nonempty Step-0 semantics/resources, actor/population order, shared Trigger instance/revision, Action fixtures, User/System mix, funded per-length activation quotes, resource policy, Running backlog, deterministic seeds, runtime/toolchain/backend, block demand, and generated Weight method. Mixed-length profiles vary zero-Step absence or unreachable suffixes. Observation provenance has one bounded owner per revision: signed `Oracle::publish` is `ExternalPhase`, public/internal `publish_from` fails closed to `Deferred`, dirty-feed state retains separate latest and active-fanout provenance, and every queued Crossing transition carries its own provenance. Each of those revision owners must also retain the exact cause block: phase without source time cannot distinguish same-block work from an older deferred revision first processed by a later fanout turn. Temporal detection is always `Deferred` because its occurrence is materialized by Actor control after the pre-external cutoff.
- `Changed variables`: Earliest Fresh Step-0 eligibility and the minimum bounded control topology needed to enforce its causal generation only. Fresh grant size is a declared candidate parameter, never Actor-authored priority.
- `Confounders`: Trigger sources occupy different block phases; comparing unlike publication/extrinsic ordering is invalid. Fresh cohort materialization improvements must not be attributed to T0. Direct package tests that call `execute_cycle` without `on_initialize` do not establish either timing profile: timing conformance requires the complete block hook order, while lower-level queue tests need an explicit physical-cutoff fixture rather than an implicit runtime profile. The I4 baseline is final for candidate construction, but the large formatted worktree still requires its normal repository checkpoint before a commit identity can replace the exact tree identity.

## Statistical / Variance Notes

W1 and the initial W2 profile are exact deterministic hook assertions rather than statistical samples. W1 populations 10 and 100 preserve three committed Actors per service block, with T0 shifting the identical sequence one block earlier. W2 first opens 10 and 100 eight-Step Running Actors, injects one new Fresh Actor per block through `ceil(population/3)` blocks, and requires every retained cursor to advance under both profiles. The in-cap 1,000-Actor profile partitions 666 Running and 334 reusable Fresh Actors, sustains one ingress/block for 668 blocks, proves every pool member is Idle before reuse, and requires every Running cursor to advance. W7 closes the candidate causal-firewall gate in the dedicated runtime profile. An ordinary external transfer starts Actor A in source block N; A's authored transfer creates an AddressEvent for Actor B, but internal protocol provenance is `Deferred`, so B remains pending at cycle nonce 0 through N and starts only at N+1. No same-block Actor cascade is admitted. W6 fixes economic visibility at the declared Actor Drain boundary. Starting from 10 units, a 30-unit post-Trigger/pre-Drain mint is visible to T0 opening, which transfers 39 while preserving one unit; a 40-unit post-Drain mutation cannot retroactively change that receipt. T+1 opens in the following block and therefore snapshots the later total, transferring 79. This is an intentional ordering difference, not nondeterminism: both profiles consume exact state at their configured opening phase. W5 executes one control-only StopCycle plus canonical Burn, Transfer, Swap, and AddLiquidity attempts from one shared ExternalPhase cause. StopCycle remains a control result with no invented Action fee, all four effect families emit their ordinary Action-fee evidence, and each T0 start/attempt is exactly one block earlier than its T+1 counterpart without Task-family timing rank. W4 isolates continuation after a shared ExternalPhase herd: twelve eight-Step Actors preserve an exact four-block gap between all adjacent Action attempts under both profiles, while T0 shifts the complete 96-attempt sequence one block earlier (completion block 32 versus 33). This establishes that the candidate changes Fresh eligibility without changing Q1 continuation cadence in the bounded profile. The 1,000-Actor runtime W3 profile repeats the exact maximum-tail mix at production configuration scale. Separate default/candidate runs each drain all first starts exactly once in subscriber/FIFO order in under eight seconds on the recorded host. A guarded generator was implemented but never run and was physically removed after rejection. It requires the exact environment consent `DEOS_EXP_0018_GENERATE_SNAPSHOT=GENERATE_MAXIMUM_W2`, is ignored by default, reports progress every 500 setup blocks, fails closed after 20,000 blocks, verifies the complete partition before commit, and atomically writes only the matching profile path. The recorded generator/loader commands are intentionally obsolete and no snapshot artifact was produced. The removed draft bundle carried exact Running and Idle-Fresh Actor-id inventories and profile-scoped paths; none became retained evidence. Smoke round trips use separate `roundtrip-*` directories and cannot overwrite retained expensive artifacts. The snapshot proof atomically publishes a deterministic profile-scoped bundle path, reloads it from disk, rejects trailing bytes, recomputes the raw-storage digest, decodes trie root/state version from the manifest, restores externalities, and removes the test artifact. Its reusable partition verifier checks exact Running/Idle-Fresh cardinality, active/identity/System-sovereign counts, FIFO occupancy, total live wakeup entries, profile authority, every retained certificate commitment, every Running RunState, and every Idle-Fresh lifecycle/latch/queue/wakeup condition. The snapshot proof also SCALE-round-trips a versioned manifest containing timing, configured-bounds commitment, encoded state version/root, payload digest, Actor/identity/System-sovereign counts, Running/Idle-Fresh partition counts, and queue occupancy. After restore it rejects profile/config drift by comparing runtime timing and current authority, verifies the retained Actor certificate carries the same configured-bounds commitment, checks exact topology counts, and requires the Fresh fixture to remain unlatched. The project runtime has a profile-independent round-trip proof for the artifact substrate: it commits a seeded Actor-state overlay, SCALE-encodes and decodes the raw trie payload, binds a nonzero BLAKE2 payload digest, reconstructs `TestExternalities` from the exact trie root/state version, and observes the same Actor identity/count under both default and candidate builds. Do not compare Trigger sources with different economic ordering or omit continuously runnable Running pressure.

## Result

The candidate lowered best-effort first-service latency by one block without changing FIFO order, Q1 continuation gaps, Task-family rank, or the actor-produced-cause firewall in the measured profiles. It did not guarantee immediate service for a herd, and maximum-population setup-through-service proved unsuitable as a bounded comparative harness. The selected fixed-timing implementation captures one block-tagged prepass cutoff and makes readiness observed in N eligible no earlier than N+1. Final evidence binds Actors Weight `6a1d68bc`, benchmark Wasm `12e502e2`, production Wasm `674022f0`, metadata `1b74078e`, and cleanup tree `9fe556b4`.

## Interpretation

The one-block best-case gain does not justify a consensus timing branch near release because the branch cannot provide a herd-level execution guarantee. A protocol-fixed eligibility floor is simpler, auditable, class-neutral, and compatible with strict FIFO and available-Weight execution. Initial cohortability remains independent of Contract length, and every surviving multi-Step Actor continues under ordinary Q1 service.

## Decision

Rejected. Production retains one protocol-fixed next-block eligibility floor; N+1 is not an execution SLA. `FreshStepZeroTiming`, `SameBlockExternal`, the candidate feature, disabled candidate tests, and snapshot generator/loader infrastructure are physically removed. Useful fixed-timing runtime herd tests remain as explicit ignored stress profiles. Reopening requires measured failure of a required economic contract and a bounded alternative that preserves FIFO, Q1, non-recursion, and Weight honesty.

## Rejected Alternatives and Tradeoffs

## Validity

- `Historical qualification under EXP-0019`: Current Category A proposal, but no candidate may be constructed against the pre-deletion User lifecycle baseline. This records that earlier checkpoint; current disposition is the metadata Status and Decision above.
- `Establishes`: The bounded Fresh timing question, Q1/non-priority invariants, workload matrix, metrics, causal-firewall gate, artifact plan, exact permissive C0 identity, and the missing T+1 baseline prerequisite.
- `Does not establish`: T0 safety, benefit, throughput, or acceptance; exact Fresh/Running grant; live-chain latency; or any Q2/Q4 claim.
- `Known limitations`: No live-chain latency distribution was measured. The rejected candidate artifacts and bounded tests are diagnostic history only; no T0 runtime/configuration surface remains supported. Tree `2dea97b1` establishes only configuration/admission binding and the original separately metered cutoff authority. The T+1 evidence tree `dbf9009b` consumes that authority for `NextBlock` and carries complete regenerated local evidence. Observation, Crossing, and temporal provenance closure is complete in evidence tree `7fc73c25`; the minimal T0 candidate and deterministic hook-profile harness are now concrete, while comparative W1-W7 measurements remain pending.
- `Invalidation triggers`: Q1/FIFO/causality change, Step-0 storage change, resource-policy phase change, Trigger topology change, block policy, toolchain/backend, or production Weight/Wasm drift.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: First-Step latency, inter-Step gap, and completion latency are distinct; shared-cause cohorts admit mixed Contract lengths and normally end after Step 0. The `N+1` floor is eligibility, not a separate successor queue: an ordinary successor must reserve its canonical FIFO position when its source Step commits so later readiness cannot overtake it. A block-bound staging page outside FIFO cannot recover that interleaved order without becoming a second scheduler.
