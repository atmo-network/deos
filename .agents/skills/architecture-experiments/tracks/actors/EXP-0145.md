## Decision Question

Does the configured Actor Control ProofSize cap admit one honestly priced EXP-0143 maximum-Step User fanout page, even if the nominal family contribution and 40% shared materialization envelope are corrected?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` independent two-dimensional Actor Control and materialization reservations; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` bounded ObservationChange fanout progress.
- `Invariant`: Actor Control must reserve a complete fanout page before mutation without borrowing from another resource domain; the independently bounded mandatory Deadline and Service obligations retain their owners.
- `Reopen trigger`: Block resource domain partition or mandatory Prepass reservation algorithm changes with equivalent measured capacity and admission proof.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: Runtime `configs/mod.rs::BlockResourceBudgetValue` uses `ACTOR_CONTROL_RATIO`. In `lib.rs::execute_mandatory_prepass`, `materialization_remaining` is Actor Control limit minus already charged control and reserved drain finalization; `materialization_maximum` takes the component-wise minimum of that remainder and configured shared materialization limit. The actual family grant is no larger.
- `Reason`: EXP-0144 found the 20% startup family-minimum invariant would fail after honest W2 rebinding, but the live shared 40% scheduler can lend between families. That alone does not establish capacity inside the independent Actor Control domain.
- `Hypothesis`: The retained Actor Control ProofSize cap is already smaller than either selected complete page and therefore cannot admit one after honest physical pricing, irrespective of inter-family lending.
- `Materiality`: A ratio or integrity-only correction that does not fix the controlling resource domain would leave admitted subscriber pages permanently unaffordable under the rebinding.

## Derived Metrics

The configured ratio is `17/50`: because `actor_control.proof_size = floor(schedulable.proof_size × 17/50) = 689,673`, the current schedulable ProofSize lies in the exact integer interval `2,028,450..=2,028,452`. On that unchanged schedulable envelope, W2 page alone requires at least 49.8324–49.8325% Actor Control and its selected complete quantum at least 50.2920%, **before** cutoff, Deadline, coordinator, dependency scan and finalization. Even a hypothetical share just above 50.2920% would not guarantee a live turn. If one demanded W2's complete quantum and the retained Crossing minimum (`564,801`) in the **same** control reservation, their sum is `1,584,949` ProofSize, requiring at least 78.1359–78.1360% before further mandatory work; the actual scheduler rotates access and does not promise simultaneous full quanta in each block. These are mathematical necessary floors only, not proposed ratios or whole-block feasibility tests.

## Result

The retained Actor Control ProofSize cap is smaller than even EXP-0143 W1 page by 272,129, and W2 by 321,153, before mandatory cutoff, Deadline, coordinator, finalization, fault/base/probe and Crossing minima are considered.

## Interpretation

Increasing the nominal family contribution, or relaxing only its startup integrity assertion, does not make a complete page affordable: `materialization_maximum` is clipped by Actor Control. Correcting the domain partition requires separately preserving Actor base/effect, mandatory Deadline, independent Service and global `on_idle` headroom. The current stale short-head Weight lets actual execution cross its intended charge, not evidence of safe admission after honest rebinding.

### One-Third Crossing Reassessment

Re-running the retained 48-User same-threshold Crossing runtime regression under the corrected `(1, 3)` Control ratio (`SKIP_WASM_BUILD=1 cargo test --locked -p deos-runtime crossing_prepass_materialization_rate_follows_the_materialization_family_rotation -- --nocapture`) passes. Source identities: runtime test SHA-256 `e114a5cde1fcab28d8a26504e11544226167e38c4f6584a8e2f606e8efe6ad81`, `configs/mod.rs` SHA-256 `5b68eabe6abdda9ba2f029609458be33e12ba99612f46e03e30d7ecfaeef8fca`. Raw native trace `/tmp/deos-crossing-third.log` SHA-256 `dbef26215ab1b996fe0b9bfccb0a7e21f9af5c59020c0f552d7af87b715b1794`. The current cap is `303,433,283,909 / 676,150`; retained generated minima are Crossing `14,826,059,000 / 564,801` and fanout `74,954,145,000 / 334,664`. The first live materialization block admits five Crossing candidates at `51,331,459,000 / 667,633` Control and zero Actor effect; the next admits six at `48,556,139,000 / 615,331`. The 48 candidates complete once over nine blocks, with cursor rotation and a later independent Shared Economic effect when Service begins. This is a reachability/liveness witness under *stale generated coefficients*, not a complete maximum-header Crossing or Deadline/Service proof. In particular `564,801 + 183,080 = 747,881` exceeds the cap by `71,731` on the selected mandatory-owner floor: actual admission below the cap does not certify conservative reservation of that combination. Do not subtract distinct complete Prepass traces to infer a physical Crossing unit; proof keys overlap and the Deadline/context mix changes across blocks. The fresh one-third trace supersedes only the old ratio's runtime-cap observation, not its historical benchmark identity or the full-page W1/W2 rejection.

### Normative Partition Screen

`docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` caps Actor Control at **one third of schedulable** ProofSize and permits family minima in different blocks. The measured baseline used `17/50` (34%), above that normative cap; the current runtime now configures exact `(1, 3)`. The selected W2-plus-Crossing complete Prepass floor `1,768,029` would require schedulable ProofSize at least `5,304,087` at one third, exceeding the entire configured `5,000,000` block by `304,087` even if fixed work were zero; this rejects **same-block co-admission** under that policy, not rotated one-family liveness. The selected W2-only complete Prepass floor `1,203,228` would require schedulable at least `3,609,684`, hence fixed at most `1,390,316`; the current schedulable interval is only `2,028,450..=2,028,452`. These are conditional lower-bound screens from retained other-owner coefficients, not production ratios or proof that safely reducing fixed work is possible. Raising only the family percentage or accepting a Control share above one third cannot implement the current specification.

## Decision

Reject unchanged Actor Control ProofSize partition for an honest W1/W2-covering fanout page. Defer partition changes until complete two-dimensional owner closure; no generated Weight, ratio, Wasm or runtime code is changed.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: Selected W1 and W2 page bounds exceed the current entire Actor Control cap, independent of rotation or Crossing consumption.
- `Does not establish`: A universal page maximum, corrected domain split, admissible complete mandatory Prepass, safe Service budget, or all-family progress on final Wasm.
- `Known limitations`: Two ordinarily admitted headers, selected W1/W2 Wasm and native runtime budget trace at current config; no source change measured here.
- `Invalidation triggers`: Revised domain split, physical page bound or mandatory Prepass domain wiring with evidence that preserves atomicity.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: The limiting cap for a worker may be its outer resource domain, not the named family contribution or shared family limit.
