## Result

## Interpretation

The constructive population bound is host-history-specific `min(active_room, identity_room, sovereign_room)`. Neither `MaxActiveActors` alone nor Waiting key count is a universal Actor-capacity claim, and retained dormant identities/sovereigns remain part of the bound.

## Decision

Accepted for the named retained-genesis mock/reference histories. Additional ordinary System creation is exactly bounded by the minimum remaining active, identity and retained-sovereign capacity; this satisfies EXP-0029 O4 without asserting a global maximum over different lifecycle histories.
