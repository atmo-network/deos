## Decision Question

Which finite set of bounded ObservationCrossing-materialization candidates is admitted for implementation and comparison against the sealed released baseline, in which selection order, with which protected guarantees, new costs, smallest falsifiers, rollback boundaries and finite stopping rule, or is the campaign closed by a sufficient negative result?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §7.6 owns the one-third Actor Control ceiling and forbids further throughput work from taking additional guaranteed capacity; `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §2 owns the component-wise algebra, the materialization fairness/cursor-rotation rule, and the reservation-before-mutation with valid-actual settlement boundary; `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` §§1.1, 2.1 owns the two-axis reporting and the reactive reference profile.
- `Invariant`: One scheduler, strict FIFO, Q1, the causal floor and cutoff, component-wise admission, reservation-before-mutation with valid-actual settlement, cursor-rotation materialization fairness, and the per-block component counters remain unchanged by every candidate.
- `Reopen trigger`: A candidate requires priority, same-block execution, relaxed rollback, additional guaranteed capacity, or a change to any semantic guarantee; or a measured capped-phase yield that is decided by a non-reservation constraint.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: The mandatory prepass rotates the materialization families and sizes each grant from the shared envelope minus later-family minima; the Crossing worker admits branches against generated reservations. On the pinned binding a single placed branch reserves `162,782` ProofSize, a two-candidate batch reserves the same `162,782`, and any admitted batch of `3..=128` reserves the `608,478` endpoint owner; classification probes cost `15,106` each and a fault reserve `1,529` is checked but unspent on success.
- `Reason`: [EXP-0103](./EXP-0103.md) measured an executed `1,3,8` cycle (mean `4`, ceiling `24` per three blocks) and [EXP-0104](./EXP-0104.md) measured that every capped-phase yield is a reservation-fit event against the rotated family grant. The campaign must now admit a finite candidate set with declared materiality and stop conditions, or close with an explicit negative result.
- `Hypothesis`: The dominant recoverable distortion is reservation precision, not missing physical work: the fallback collapses a classified multi-candidate cohort to the scalar single even though the pair owner has the same ProofSize as the single owner, and small admitted counts are charged the 128-candidate endpoint. A pair-owner fallback and a count-parameterized admitted-batch owner can therefore raise lean-phase yields toward the existing counter ceiling without changing semantics, storage, or the resource policy; a demand-aware family-minimum reservation is a separately labelled policy candidate; no physical index-representation change is required because the ceiling is reachable without it.
- `Materiality`: A retained candidate must change the executed admission mode against the sealed witness: at least one previously lean rotation phase must admit its classified scalar-equivalent placed pair in one admission decision where the released binding admits one scalar candidate (for `CB1` this pair is the generated two-candidate placed pair; admitting the full counter-limited classified cohort up to the `MaxCrossingTransitionsPerBlock`-limited maximum is `CB2`'s separate domain), and the executed three-block cycle must rise above the released `12`. All P2 correctness assertions stay green, no settlement may exceed its generated owner, and no other family's guaranteed in-block minimum or settled per-candidate Weight may worsen. This is a comparative decision against the sealed baseline with a declared architectural switch, never a universal percentage, and it does not reopen the retired `10,000`/`100` horizon. A candidate that raises the cycle without switching the admission mode is recorded as a research result.
- `Materiality scope clarification (post-seal, 2026-09-11)`: At the `CB1` materiality boundary the classified cohort means the generated two-candidate placed pair admitted by `CB1`; `CB1` was not required to achieve `CB2`'s full-cohort objective. All original numbers, timestamps, artifact identities and candidate ordering are unchanged.

## Result

No new runtime measurement was taken. The reused evidence decides that the executed yields are reservation-fit events and that the recoverable distortion is reservation precision at small admitted counts. The finite candidate set is admitted as `CB1 → CB2 → CG` with the declared materiality, falsifiers, rollback boundaries and stopping rule. No candidate is selected as an improvement before its own comparison measures it.

## Interpretation

The released binding spends its lean grants on scalar admissions: after the endpoint owner fails to fit, the worker collapses the classified cohort to one candidate even though the pair owner equals the single owner, and any admitted batch above two is charged the `128`-candidate endpoint. CB1 therefore buys admission mode with no new reservation or regeneration; CB2 removes the endpoint over-reservation itself and is the smallest candidate expected to admit a full classified cohort inside the lean grants; CG is a policy-routed alternative that can enlarge grants only when a later family has no due work, which is expected to be redundant once CB2 lands and is kept as a labelled tradeoff on its own specification track.

A threshold-radix representation change would reduce the fixed per-fired-leaf index cost that dominates the single-owner estimate, but it is not admitted because reservation precision already reaches the counter ceiling on the measured geometry at a much smaller blast radius. The funded-User legal-herd fixture and in-worker per-stop receipts stay inert: the admitted candidates' falsifiers are decidable from phase-level yields, reservation containment and the capacity regression.

## Decision

Accepted as the campaign's Crossing candidate-admission research result. Outcome class: research result (bounded admission and limitation — every executed capped-phase yield is a reservation-fit event, and the materialization payoff is bounded by the existing counter ceiling). Decision scope: candidate admission and selection ordering only; no runtime, semantic, resource-policy or architecture change is selected by this record, and each candidate's efficiency-improvement or tradeoff class is sealed only by its own P4 comparison Leaf.

Finite stopping basis: the measured grant algebra bounds every candidate's effect by the cycle ceiling `24`, and the set contains two reservation-precision candidates plus one policy-routed tradeoff candidate. Selection order: `CB1` first as the smallest observable step and cheapest falsifier of the owner-fit model, `CB2` second only if `CB1` leaves the declared materiality unmet, `CG` third only under an explicit resource-policy decision. Stop when the materiality is met, the remaining delta is below materiality, the bottleneck has moved, or the frozen set is exhausted; exhausting it closes the campaign with an explicit no-optimization research result.
