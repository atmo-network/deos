## Decision Question

Does the recorded W6 mixed-arrival timeline attribute service to actual readiness and current lifecycle/generation authority, without treating pre-window, paused or pre-materialization time as runnable service delay?

- `Invariant`: FIFO, Q1, next-block causal floor, current generation authority and one pending latch remain unchanged.
- `Reopen trigger`: A demonstrated violation of readiness/authority semantics requires explicit defect classification rather than a measurement-driven semantic change.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: Frozen C1 on the accepted EXP-0095 production binding.
- `Hypothesis`: W6's finite trace reconciles mixed block/tick readiness and lifecycle interruptions with current-generation service.
- `Materiality`: One pre-eligibility, paused, closed or stale-generation execution falsifies this bounded witness.

The narrowed claim is mixed block/tick readiness with sparse block-window arrivals, overdue temporal coalescence and lifecycle/generation safety—not staggered temporal block arrivals or preserved per-tick execution rates. No fixture parameter changed to manufacture such evidence.

All 12 retained Actors have progressed by the horizon; Actor 20 is intentionally closed, and no pending signal remains at block 17. No Actor has a between-block Running Pipeline in this one-Step matrix, so the Cadenced two-block inter-cycle gaps are not continuously Running `ServiceGapBlocks`. This closes W6's bounded arrival/eligibility timeline while leaving sustained fairness, User/reactive scale acceptance and complete executed-path resource accounting outside its scope.

## Interpretation

This proves neither staggered temporal block arrivals nor per-tick execution rates. Historical “closes” is bounded timeline reporting, not production User/reactive acceptance. Later User Opening qualification remains a separate parent input and cannot be resolved by this trace.

## Decision

Accepted for its bounded current-production arrival/eligibility claim. The EXP-0095 refresh preserves current-generation authority, lifecycle exclusions and block/tick classification while rebinding quantitative Weight and proof values. It does not accept sustained fairness, temporal rates or User/reactive scale targets.
