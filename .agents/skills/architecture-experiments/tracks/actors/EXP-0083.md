## Decision Question

Can the retained empty-workload Control baseline be decomposed exactly into no-due charged owners plus a one-time genesis-clock term without calling either a completed Cycle?

## Governing Specification

- `Owners`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` (Trigger/lifecycle and scheduling); `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` (charged admission owners).
- `Invariant`: Preserve useful-readiness semantics and complete temporal admission; no fictitious progress or refunds.
- `Reopen trigger`: Demonstrated semantic conflict rather than diagnostic accounting correction.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: Frozen C1 coordinates no-due blocks and initializes a deferred Fee Sink genesis clock.
- `Reason`: Empty workload is neither zero coordination cost nor zero-Step Contract completion.
- `Hypothesis`: No-due owners plus the startup term exactly reproduce the bounded baseline.
- `Materiality`: Keep startup, steady no-due and useful progress independently accountable.
