## Decision Question

Does the measured ObservationCrossing target constraint earn a conforming residual change for 0.7.25, or should G8 retain the exact limitation and reject speculative optimization?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` §§1.1–1.3, 2.1–2.3 owns the 10,000-User/100-eligible-block targets; pallet specification §§4.3, 8 owns Crossing semantics and bounded scheduler authority.
- `Invariant`: FIFO, immutable cutoff, Q1, causal floor, generation safety, one scheduler and component-wise admission remain unchanged.
- `Entry rule`: A micro change must improve a complete path by at least 5% or remove a measured owner without semantic/resource/fairness regression.
- `Reopen trigger`: Accepted EXP-0087 target impossibility permits investigation but does not authorize a geometry change.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Measured owner`: Crossing materialization admits four candidates/block on current generated Weight; User membership is capped at 9,000/feed.
- `Old blocking reason`: Before G6, required User/reactive population and sustained fairness evidence were unresolved rather than falsified.
- `Changed assumption`: EXP-0087 now proves two independent target blockers on exact current production bounds.
- `Hypothesis`: No micro residual can close a 25-fold detector gap plus a separate population-cap gap without reopening physical geometry and target policy.
- `Materiality`: Speculative optimization would consume release risk while still failing the governing target.

## Baseline and Candidates

- `C0 Retain`: Preserve current C1 and publish the explicit target constraint.
- `C1 Micro owner reduction`: Reduce one existing Crossing component by at least 5% without changing topology.
- `C2 Geometry reopening`: Change batching/index/proof topology and User/feed admission sufficiently to construct and materialize 10,000 Users in 100 blocks.

C2 is not a residual micro change. It is included only as the semantic classification of what target satisfaction would require.

## Controls

- `Controlled variables`: EXP-0095 binding, target definition, User identity, one-feed Crossing cause and all frozen scheduler invariants.
- `Changed variables`: Candidate disposition only; no implementation is authorized.
- `Confounders`: System throughput, broad ObservationChange fanout, scheduler service after detection and generated I/O versus physical proof.

## Benchmark Class, Workloads, and Criteria

- `Class`: Evidence-selected candidate-admission decision.
- `Workload`: Governing 10,000 prefunded one-Step User ObservationCrossing profile over 100 eligible blocks.
- `Metrics`: Constructible User population, candidates/block, target-window materialization, complete-path RefTime/ProofSize and semantic changes.

- `Rejection`: Less than 100 candidates/block, cap below 10,000, independent-maxima composition, proof discount or new priority/retry authority.

## Measurements

Current baseline admits 9,000 User Crossing members/feed and four candidates/block. The target requires 10,000 and at least 100/block before scheduler service. A 5% micro improvement yields at most 4.2 candidates/block conceptually and does not change the membership cap; even a 100% detector improvement reaches only eight candidates/block. Target closure requires at least 25× current detector admission plus a separate 11.12% User-cap increase.

## Derived Metrics

- `Detector coverage`: `4 * 100 / 10,000 = 4%`.
- `Required detector multiplier`: `100 / 4 = 25×`.
- `User-cap deficit`: `1,000` identities, or `10,000 / 9,000 - 1 ≈ 11.12%`.

These independent blockers are not composed into one Weight estimate.

## Statistical / Variance Notes

Deterministic configured bounds and generated admission; no timing sample or unobserved throughput is inferred.

## Result

C0 is retained: no residual implementation and the exact target constraint advances to production disposition. C1 is rejected because any plausible micro improvement remains target-irrelevant and leaves the independent User cap blocker. C2 is rejected from 0.7.25 because it is a new architecture/policy campaign requiring ≥25× detector admission, higher User/feed capacity and complete G3–G7 evidence regeneration, not a bounded residual.

## Interpretation

The impossibility result earned investigation but not implementation. Preserving C1 is safer and more truthful than a coefficient patch or unbounded late geometry rewrite. C0 does not grant release permission; it makes the unresolved governing-policy choice explicit.

## Decision

Accepted C0 for G8 residual convergence. Implement no reactive geometry change in 0.7.25; carry the precise constraint to Production Acceptance or Explicit Constraint. This is candidate disposition, not governing-policy permission to freeze or release.

## Rejected Alternatives and Tradeoffs

- `C1 / 5% coefficient patch`: Rejected as target-irrelevant and unsupported by physical proof.
- `System proxy`: Rejected by EXP-0087.
- `Broad fanout substitution`: Rejected because the target requires ObservationCrossing.
- `Independent maxima`: Rejected because User cap and detector Weight are separate blockers.
- `C2 late geometry reopening`: Rejected from 0.7.25 because it requires a new bounded architecture campaign and full downstream evidence refresh.

## Validity

Establishes C0 residual disposition and rejects C1/C2 for 0.7.25. Does not change C1, satisfy the target or grant production/release constraint permission.

## Follow-Up / Next Gradient

Request the owning governing-policy decision for the explicit production constraint. Without permission, Geometry Freeze remains blocked.

## Architecture Impact

No implementation change; C1 remains frozen and C0 carries the exact limitation forward.
