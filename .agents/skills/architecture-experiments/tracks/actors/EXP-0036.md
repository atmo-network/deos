## Interpretation

The traversal has its own fixed physical owner because an empty Opening snapshot does not imply zero structural work. Its direct full-Contract benchmark, zero database topology and mutually exclusive selector are sufficient for this bounded question without rerunning nonzero host capture or complete Opening. Raw maximum wall time is not a consensus coefficient, and the generated median owner is not combined with a nonzero capture intercept.

## Decision

Accepted for the reference binding. When `opening_snapshot_entries == 0` in an Opening head, select exactly `opening_snapshot_traversal()` at `1,467,000` RefTime / zero ProofSize; for every nonzero count, select only the accepted nonzero amount capture envelope. Maximum and actual use the same partition, and inclusive direct Opening owners receive no additive traversal. EXP-0065 owns complete final-tree regeneration and artifact identity.

## Rejected Alternatives and Tradeoffs

Reject omitting traversal because the snapshot is empty, reusing a nonzero capture intercept, adding traversal to nonzero capture, and adding it to inclusive direct Opening owners. Retain raw timing tails as observations rather than manually padding the generated fixed coefficient.
