## Decision Question

Does retained `close_actor` ProofSize bound reachable Service-resident closes with simultaneous Tick Trigger cleanup, including deep-index repair?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` close/custody semantics; `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` terminal resource ownership.
- `Invariant`: Close atomically releases exactly one process residence, its independent Trigger authority and bounded Contract state without transferring custody or publishing a successor.
- `Reopen trigger`: A selected close cannot be constructed from ordinary admitted state, loses custody neutrality, or fails exact cleanup.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: `remove_actor_publication_and_finalize` releases Service and Trigger authority before the shared finalizer. `close_dispatch_weight_upper` and `close_cleanup_weight_upper` consume one generated `close_actor` envelope.
- `Reason`: Historical local mixed Deadline closes fit retained Weight but cannot prove saturated Tick-index repair.
- `Hypothesis`: A reachable Service-resident deep Tick close exceeds retained ProofSize while preserving semantics.
- `Materiality`: A single excess blocks accepting dispatch, internal cleanup and Pipeline fee bounds against stale generated Weight.
