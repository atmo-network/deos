## Decision Question

Does every control-flow segment of the Crossing service admission ladder — in particular the retained pair-fallback selection — have an explicit generated Weight owner whose benchmarked scope includes it, or does the retained binding carry an unowned selection segment?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` component-wise admission and reservation-before-mutation; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` actor execution contracts; production-Weight ownership in the architecture-experiments Benchmark Reassessment Protocol; `BACKLOG.md` P5.4.
- `Invariant`: Every admitted execution segment is covered by a generated owner benchmarked over that segment; pure control flow consumes RefTime and cannot claim zero Weight from absent storage access or absent `meter.consume`.
- `Reopen trigger`: A new or extended benchmarked owner whose measured block reaches the selection helpers, or a changed service ladder that moves or removes the segment.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: `service_crossing_transitions_resuming` charges `crossing_worker_base` once per call (benchmark: fault-state and pending-list reads), one probe per unit before classification (`crossing_work_probe` measures `classify_crossing_work_preflight()`; the fire, pair, rearm-pair and skip-pair probes measure classification extensions; `crossing_tail_refill_probe` measures the tail suffix count), then selects the admission branch and consumes the branch owner after execution (`crossing_placed_unit`, `crossing_placed_pair_unit`, `crossing_placed_maximum_unit`, benchmarked over the executed functions), with `record_crossing_worker_fault` as the fault reserve.
- `Reason`: [EXP-0112](./EXP-0112.md) retains the pinned binding under execution-path containment while [EXP-0108](./EXP-0108.md) states the fallback "adds no owner, I/O, or charge of its own". Under the Benchmark Reassessment Protocol that wording needs an explicit ownership check: absent storage access is not a zero-RefTime proof.
- `Hypothesis`: No generated owner's benchmarked scope includes the admission-selection segment (`crossing_plan_weight_for_admission`, `crossing_placed_pair_fallback`, `crossing_single_candidate_plan`, the fault-reserve arithmetic and the reservation checks); it is a real, bounded, storage-free control-flow gap that must be closed before release. A benchmark whose measured block reaches those helpers, or a production budget that charges an owner for them, falsifies the hypothesis.
- `Materiality`: The claim is decided by an exhaustive coverage map of the service loop against the production benchmark set and the test-built production budgets; a single covered selection segment rejects the gap claim and closes the record as a negative result.

**Audit evidence.**

- The selector helpers `crossing_placed_pair_fallback`, `crossing_plan_weight_for_admission`, `crossing_single_candidate_plan` and `crossing_plan_weight` occur in no `#[benchmark]` block; they appear only in production code and unit tests.
- The charge order in `service_crossing_transitions_resuming` consumes probes before classification and the branch owner after execution; no `meter.consume` or `can_consume` owner exists between them for the selection arithmetic added by the retained fallback.
- The test-built production budget confirms the released charging model: `crossing_worker_base + crossing_work_probe + crossing_fire_pair_probe + crossing_placed_unit + record_crossing_worker_fault` (`crossing_pair_downgrades_to_one_at_branch_weight_boundary`) and the tail-refill budget with `crossing_tail_refill_probe + crossing_placed_non_tail_trimmed_unit`; neither includes a selection owner.
- The released ladder already executed the same selection arithmetic minus the pair preference, so the gap predates the campaign and the retained change extends it by one branch.

## Result

No generated owner's benchmarked scope includes the admission-selection segment. Classification, tail refill, branch execution and fault recording are each covered by their generated owners; the selection arithmetic that computes the admitted branch weight, evaluates the retained pair fallback, applies the scalar fallback and checks the reservation has no charged or benchmarked owner.

## Interpretation

The retained binding's execution-path containment stands: the pair still executes exactly the benchmarked pair function, and the selector performs no storage access, meter consumption or mutation. What does not stand is the implied ownership completeness: "no charge of its own" means no new owner or double charge, not zero RefTime. The unowned segment is fixed, storage-free and bounded by the per-block component counters, but it is real execution time that the production meter does not claim. The gap is pre-existing and was extended by the retained branch; it is small in magnitude and bounded in structure, so the correction is a bounded ownership task, not an architecture campaign.

## Decision

Accepted. Outcome class: **research result** (a bounded ownership limitation established). Finite stopping basis: the segment-to-owner map is exhaustive over the service loop's charged sequence, the production benchmark set and the test-built budgets; no further measurement can change the finding without a source or benchmark change. The correction is recorded as `BACKLOG.md` P5.4 and gates the release Weight-soundness claim; EXP-0108's ownership wording is qualified through its Benchmark Evidence Disposition surface.

## Rejected Alternatives and Tradeoffs

| Candidate | Decision | Evidence-backed reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The common probe already covers the selection | Rejected | `crossing_work_probe` measures `classify_crossing_work_preflight()` only; the selection executes after classification and outside that block |
| The branch owner conservatively includes the selection | Rejected | The branch owners measure the executed functions; the selection that chooses them runs before consumption and outside their blocks |
| Treating absent storage access as zero RefTime | Rejected | Pure control flow consumes execution time; the Benchmark Reassessment Protocol requires an explicit conservative owner |
| Opening a full architecture campaign | Rejected | The gap is one fixed control segment; a bounded owner closes it without semantic, storage or policy change |
| Leaving the wording as historical and shipping | Rejected | The protocol admits a qualified wording only with an explicit release gate; a real unowned segment before release is not a wording matter |

## Validity

- `Establishes`: The exact unowned admission-selection segment and the covered status of every other service-loop segment on `614d0dc…` and the pinned benchmark set.
- `Does not establish`: The selector's RefTime magnitude, the correction implementation, or that the retained binding is release-ready; P5.4 and P6.3 remain open.
- `Known limitations`: The audit is source-based at one commit; benchmark scope is read from `#[benchmark]` blocks and the production exclusion list, not from generated artifacts.
- `Invalidation triggers`: A new or extended benchmark covering the selection helpers, a changed service ladder or charge order, or a changed `WeightInfo` set.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: Generated owners cover functions; a service loop's own glue needs an explicit owner whenever it grows beyond a trivial fixed sequence, regardless of storage access.

## Follow-Up / Next Gradient

```text
target → ownership completeness → binding dimension: RefTime ownership (not ProofSize)
→ dominant contributor: the admission-selection arithmetic → owning mechanism: service_crossing_transitions_resuming
→ next hypothesis: one explicit conservative selection owner closes the gap
```

- `Eliminated hypotheses`: Existing probe, worker-base or branch owners already cover the selection; absent storage access implies zero Weight.

- `Stop condition`: Ownership finding decided; BACKLOG P5.4 owns the correction and P6.3 verifies the resulting owner soundness.
