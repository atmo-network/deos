## Decision Question

Can per-Step User state-hold reconciliation reuse the exact `contract_body` component already owned by `ActorStateHolds`, instead of rescanning immutable canonical tail chunks, while preserving hold amounts, lifecycle atomicity, fail-closed ownership, Q1/FIFO semantics, and the measured P12 middle and lifecycle baseline?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` → `Active User State Hold`, `Hot/Cold Physical Contract`, and lifecycle atomicity; `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` → User state-cost custody.
- `Invariant`: Creation and every Contract mutation compute the exact immutable body charge before commit; ordinary Step execution changes no Contract body; the existing User hold record remains the sole monetary owner; missing or wrong-owner records fail closed; close and replacement reconcile atomically; System Actors remain exempt.
- `Reopen trigger`: Any need for a second Contract mirror, duplicated mutable authority, weaker exact-release behavior, owner-funded repair, off-chain correctness, or changed Step/FIFO semantics reopens Specification rather than remaining a physical experiment.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: EXP-0025 C1 reconstructs `contract_body` hold bytes by scanning every canonical `ActorContractTailChunk` during each User Step reconciliation even though the body is immutable and the exact charged component already exists in `ActorStateHolds`.
- `Reason`: EXP-0004 C3 showed that compact current-Step authority could not help while state-hold reconciliation independently retained the canonical tail read. EXP-0026 fixed P12 at three maximum tail chunks and left three-Step middle proof at `6,876.52`.
- `Hypothesis`: Reusing the existing immutable hold component removes only redundant whole-body scans. Three-Step W1 may remain proof-identical because execution still needs its one tail, while a maximum 12-Step middle Step should stop reading unreachable tail chunks and materially reduce reads/proof without schema growth.
- `Materiality`: Primary evidence is exact owner/read removal at maximum P12 middle geometry with no component-wise regression at three-Step W1/P0. RefTime-only gains are reported but do not substitute for removed storage authority.

## Statistical / Variance Notes

All focused models use compiled Wasm, RocksDB, 1,024 MiB cache, 50 steps × 20 repeats, and no removed samples. W2 B0 and C1 use the same dedicated maximum-Contract benchmark source and differ only by the state-hold sourcing mechanism. Their estimated/measured proof, reads, and writes are exactly identical; the `0.0088%` RefTime difference is non-material.

## Result

C1 preserves focused semantics and does not regress W1/P0 materially, but it removes no complete-path storage owner. Maximum W2 remains three tail reads per Actor because `exp0025_execute_ready_single_step` independently requests full Contract reconstruction before state-hold reconciliation. B0 and C1 therefore share exact `9,882` estimated proof slope, `2,456` measured slope, 11 reads, and 4 writes per Actor. Candidate code and benchmark instrumentation are deleted; the five-file source manifest is restored exactly to `3fd62d1f`.

## Interpretation

The state-hold rescan was real but not the sole full-body reader. Execution-plan construction passes `full_contract = true` for the shared Ready/Running/Suspended path, so removing only state-hold scans cannot change the composed proof/read set. Changing that reconstruction is a different mechanism and may affect Opening control context; combining it here would violate the one-variable boundary. W3–W5 correctly stop because the primary W2 owner-removal rejection criterion already fails.

## Decision

Reject C1 at W2. Exact complete-path reads and proof are unchanged, so the cached hold source does not earn its stale-cache risk. Restore B0 and do not open an automatic second successor; any later full-Contract reconstruction experiment must separately own Opening-context and shared-path semantics.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: State-hold scan elision alone preserves focused parity but removes no complete-path tail read or proof owner under current full-Contract execution-plan reconstruction.
- `Does not establish`: Compact Step representation, EXP-0025 acceptance, integrated W1 throughput, or correctness in another host runtime.
- `Known limitations`: W3–W5 stop at the explicit W2 rejection criterion; the candidate is not production code and all instrumentation is deleted.
- `Invalidation triggers`: State-hold schema/rates, Contract mutation semantics, tail representation, P12 bounds, C32 scheduler geometry, benchmark method, or proof model changes.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: An already charged immutable monetary component may be a valid cache only when lifecycle boundaries remain its exact establishment and invalidation owner.

## Follow-Up / Next Gradient

```text
P12 middle 6,876.52 → canonical current/successor tail remains required
→ exact state hold rescans all immutable tails
→ reuse existing contract_body charge without schema growth
→ measure maximum 12-Step read/proof removal
→ accept/reject before any compact Step composition
```

- `Eliminated hypotheses`: The state-hold rescan is the sole maximum-middle full-body tail owner; its removal alone improves complete-path proof or reads.
- `Remaining hypotheses`: Shared full-Contract execution reconstruction is the retained maximum-middle owner, but it is outside this experiment and not admitted as an automatic successor.
- `Stop condition`: Accept or reject C1 after W1–W5, retain exact artifacts, and either permit one later representation composition or restore B0 with no second successor.
