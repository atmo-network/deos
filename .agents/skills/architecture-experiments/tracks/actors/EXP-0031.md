## Measurements

No new measurement. The necessary starting facts and remaining evidence boundary are:

Existing-key append does not perform the same physical work as first publication of an absent deadline. Likewise, exact removal may repair upward, downward or only remove the tail; changed-page count cannot order their full Weight costs. `run_progress`/`run_suspend` fixtures already register references through `write_run_state`, while Cadenced Opening independently removes/rearms Tick references even when its successor is Queue. These baseline call-path distinctions require complete legal-state, no-double-charge and competing-branch evidence here; isolated historical samples are not retained as an envelope.
