## Decision Question

Does the deadline substrate plan destinations without page scans, and do the retained `close_actor` and temporal Deadline owners component-wise bound every reachable close and due-worker geometry?

## Context and Candidates

- `Current architecture`: C32 deadline pages with paged min-heaps per Block/Tick key ([EXP-0161](./EXP-0161.md) O6); one retained generated `close_actor` owner charges direct close, automatic apoptosis and User cleanup fee; the mandatory Prepass services one Block-then-Tick Deadline quantum.
- `Hypothesis`: Header-owned vacancy authority bounds destination planning to two reads; retained close and temporal owners bound reachable two-clock and deep-index geometries.
- `Materiality`: Any selected reachable geometry above a retained owner in either dimension rejects that owner as a complete bound.
- `Controlled / changed variables`: Same benchmark Wasm per comparison (50 steps × 20 repeats, RocksDB reference DB costs); only the reachable geometry changes.

### Benchmark Evidence Disposition

- `Benchmark Evidence Status`: Qualified — selected same-Wasm falsifiers on pre-cut sources; single 50×20 runs without replicate distribution.
- `Reassessment Trigger`: Coordinated regeneration, a changed close/Deadline path, or changed Actor page/header limits.
- `Compared Observation IDs`: Archived EXP-0138–EXP-0142 output hashes.
- `Noise / Stability Evidence`: None; ProofSize and read/write counts are the stable dimensions, RefTime is one-repeat.
- `Current Authority`: O1 selects the shipped vacancy authority. O2–O5 reject the retained owners as complete bounds but supply no coefficient; the largest selected close (Parked W5) is not a universal maximum.

## Result

Vacancy authority cut maximum planning from 314 reads to two; mutation tests then exposed a truncated-page indexing panic and a missing vacancy head treated as full, both fixed by shared header validation, bounded page refusal and checked C32 access without changing the candidate. Every selected Parked, Service-resident and Suspended two-clock close exceeds retained `close_actor` in at least one dimension. The complete T1 Tick worker costs about four times RefTime and nine times ProofSize of the occurrence-only charge.

## Interpretation

Close cost is dominated by deep-index repair on each clock, and both clocks can be deep at once, so the close owner must price the joint two-clock repair rather than one clock. Temporal pricing must include extraction and useful materialization. Removing the dead comparators would lower the Service outer maximum, but only against stale coefficients. The native tests `mandatory_deadline_reservation_composes_both_clock_branch_maxima` and `service_outer_and_mandatory_deadline_branches_fit_their_own_budgets` prove the algebraic two-clock selector and independent budgets, not physical coefficients.

## Decision

O1 Accepted: efficiency improvement; no second alternative admitted. O2–O5: research results rejecting the retained owners; no production Weight, cleanup reserve or fee is rebound from these diagnostics. O6 stays open until complete live Service bounds exist. Stop adding close geometries: continue only with a reachable missing selector or a conservative complete-owner dominator.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: Bounded destination planning; concrete underbounds of retained close and occurrence-only temporal owners.
- `Does not establish`: Universal close or worker maxima, production coefficients, or post-cut geometry.
- `Invalidation triggers`: Changes to deadline page layout, heap geometry, header size limits, close finalizer or Prepass orchestration.
