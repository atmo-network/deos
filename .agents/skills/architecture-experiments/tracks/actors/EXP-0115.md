## Decision Question

Does the retained pair-owner fallback on the reissued production binding change the end-to-end funded User cohort result — Trigger occurrence through `Ready`, one committed Transfer Step and a completed Cycle — relative to the sealed released cohort on the same bounded 48-member geometry, and is the combined result an end-to-end improvement, a materialization-only improvement, or a downstream-regression finding?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `BACKLOG.md` P5.3 owns the declared end-to-end witness, its identity binding and its classification rule; `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` owns the two-axis reporting; `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` owns component-wise admission and reservation-before-mutation; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` owns the one-third Actor Control ceiling and the FIFO/Q1/causal semantics.
- `Invariant`: Strict FIFO, Q1, the causal floor and cutoff, component-wise admission, reservation-before-mutation with valid-actual settlement, and exactly-once materialization remain unchanged; the candidate takes no additional guaranteed in-block capacity.
- `Reopen trigger`: A protected correctness or resource-soundness regression on the retained binding, a released-side identity that cannot be bound to its recorded production artifact, or a changed witness geometry, runtime constant or binding.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: `service_crossing_transitions_resuming` falls back to the generated pair owner when the aggregate batch does not fit. [EXP-0110](./EXP-0110.md) measured the candidate on the sealed released comparison basis (lean phases `1/3 → 2/6`, steady cycle `12 → 16`), [EXP-0112](./EXP-0112.md) bound the retained cohort, and [EXP-0114](./EXP-0114.md) closed the admission-selection ownership gap and reissued the binding.
- `Reason`: P4 selected and P5 converged the candidate, but its materialization-cycle gain had not been shown to move the funded User cohort's end-to-end completion horizon or service latency against the released side under one held-constant workload and measurement identity.
- `Hypothesis`: The pair fallback's lean-phase admissions reach the real service path, so the same 48-member funded User cohort materializes earlier and completes earlier on the retained binding than on the sealed released cohort, with unchanged exactly-once, FIFO/Q1/causality and fault correctness.
- `Materiality`: Declared in `BACKLOG.md` P5.3 before measurement: end-to-end improvement only when the completion horizon or service latency improves with no protected regression; otherwise a materialization-only improvement or the exact downstream-regression owner; closing the retired `10,000`/`100` horizon is not required.
