## Interpretation

The product cannot be honestly represented by three independently admissible maxima or by one static tuple frontier: authored Task algebra restricts reachability, and Task/control owners decide resource admission. The complete-Contract predicate preserves structural expressiveness and exact refusal without raising budgets or weakening Weight. Whole-owner selection also matters numerically: combining publication and removal would invent an unreachable additive path, while the finite transition partition retains both sound containment and genesis fit. Conditional optimization remains unallocated.

## Decision

Accepted for the current reference composition. Structural reachability is filtered by complete component-wise Contract Weight. Direct Waiting profiles remain inclusive; unmatched Wakeup selects publication once; due workers remain inclusive; populated terminal close selects removal whole. The selected pre-G5 genesis maximum fits the guaranteed service floor. Do not hand-edit generated coefficients, widen budgets, combine mutually exclusive Waiting owners or treat `12/24/48` as a Cartesian admitted product. EXP-0065 owns exact regeneration and changed-binding requalification.
