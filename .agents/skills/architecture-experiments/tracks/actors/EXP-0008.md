## Decision Question

Which production `ObservationPageSize` among 64, 128, and 256 minimizes complete Crossing materialization cost while retaining bounded mutation, exact non-tail compaction, and at least 10,000 admitted feed memberships?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` → `3.5 ObservationCrossing`, `15.2.9 Production Bounds`; `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` → Crossing production evidence.
- `Invariant`: Pages preserve generation-checked membership authority, exact tail/non-tail compaction, revision order, scalar fallback, bounded ProofSize, class-neutral FIFO, and current-state Trigger latching.
- `Reopen trigger`: Any candidate requires changing Crossing semantics, FIFO order, the 10,000-member bound, or one-causal-hop timing rather than physical page geometry.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: Production uses 64-entry Observation pages, a four-candidate Crossing cohort, sparse radix leaves, contiguous-prefix snapshots, and exact physical-tail refill for non-tail removal.
- `Reason`: The release requires a generated cohort of at least 128 candidates when the complete branch fits and explicitly requires measured `64/128/256` page geometry.
- `Hypothesis`: A 128- or 256-entry page can reduce page traversal and refill reads enough to support a larger contiguous cohort, but may lose on maximum encoded-value ProofSize and mutation cost.
- `Materiality`: Select only a candidate that admits 10,000 memberships, keeps every complete branch inside Actor Control and the Crossing worker envelope component-wise, and improves the downstream EXP-0009 cohort frontier.

## Benchmark Class, Workloads, and Criteria

- `Benchmark class`: FRAME pallet generated Weight under compiled Wasm plus deterministic package/runtime integration profiles.

| Workload | State/population geometry | Hot/cold assumptions | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W1 Tail fire | Full source tail page; homogeneous fire candidates | Maximum encoded page cold | Compare contiguous fire and page rewrite |
| W2 Non-tail fire | Full source page plus full physical tail refill | Both pages cold | Expose dominant refill ProofSize and writes |
| W3 Rearm/coalesce/close | Full pages across each terminal branch | Branch-required Actor state cold | Prevent optimization for placed-only behavior |
| W4 Scalar/mixed fallback | First mismatch at positions 1, midpoint, and final slot | Required pages cold | Preserve exact fallback and bounded classification |
| W5 Capacity | 10,000 total / 9,000 User memberships | Deterministic prefunded setup outside measurement | Prove admission and sparse-page topology |

- `Metrics`: RefTime, ProofSize, reads, writes, encoded page bytes, candidates admitted per complete branch, pages touched, worker-envelope fit, and runtime Wasm bytes.
- `Statistical method`: Generate Weight with 50 steps and 20 repeats against each matching benchmark Wasm; retain raw model output and review both Weight components. Deterministic correctness profiles use exact state/event roots and no timing-based exclusion.
- `Acceptance criteria`: Select the Pareto candidate that enables the highest conforming EXP-0009 cohort; ties prefer smaller maximum encoded page value and lower lifecycle mutation Weight. Every branch and 10,000-member profile must pass.
- `Rejection criteria`: Any invariant regression, component-wise envelope overflow, maximum-extrinsic failure, capacity loss, stale locator, partial mutation, or candidate that cannot improve the downstream cohort frontier.

## Statistical / Variance Notes

All twelve focused owners used generated Weight with 50 steps and 20 repeats against the matching candidate Wasm. No samples or owners were discarded. The current fixed ProofSize cap of the complete mutation branches masks page-value growth there, while the isolated tail probe exposes the expected 4,561 → 5,585 → 7,633 proof progression.

## Result

P64 is cheapest at common work. P128 pays 10.17–21.35% RefTime and 64 additional maximum-branch writes to make a one-page 128-candidate snapshot representable. P256 pays 14.23–57.34% RefTime and 192 additional writes, while its extra 256-entry frontier is not required by the release target.

## Interpretation

EXP-0009 confirms that P128 is the smallest page supporting the selected C128 tail/preflight frontier. H0 plus the N64 non-tail cap keeps complete ProofSize inside Actor Control without requiring P256 or weakening exact refill.

## Decision

Accept P128 as the Crossing membership baseline with EXP-0009's C128 tail/preflight and N64 non-tail geometry. Full production regeneration exposed that sharing P128 with broad ObservationChange made one fanout page exceed Actor Control; physical ownership is therefore split, retaining P64 broad fanout independently. Full Actors Weight, metadata/ABI, storage version 15, and canonical post-generation Wasm evidence have landed.

## Rejected Alternatives and Tradeoffs

| Candidate | Decision | Evidence-backed reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pages below 64 | Excluded | Cannot improve the required ≥128 contiguous cohort frontier and increases traversal pressure |
| P64 | Rejected for target | Cheapest common work but cannot represent the selected one-page C128 frontier |
| P128 | Accepted | Smallest target-enabling page; complete H0-N64 evidence passes in EXP-0009 |
| P256 | Rejected | Adds page ProofSize and mutation writes without a required frontier |
| Pages above 256 | Deferred | No release requirement or measured pressure justifies the larger maximum encoded value |

## Validity

- `Does not establish`: A winning page size, ≥128 cohort fit, or production throughput.
