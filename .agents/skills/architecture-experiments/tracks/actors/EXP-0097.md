## Decision Question

Does one successful funded User Action map its Action fee and resource effect separately from Trigger and complete Pipeline owners on the qualified production binding?

## Governing Specification

- `Owners`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §§3.4 and 7.3–7.6; `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §§1 and 4.
- `Invariant`: Pipeline authority and pay-as-attempted Action fees remain separate; no owner borrows or duplicates another charge.
- `Reopen trigger`: A semantic fee change must reopen the specification rather than become an accounting correction.

## Context and Hypothesis

EXP-0088 closes bounded Trigger/Pipeline collection with StopCycle, which intentionally invokes no Action. The remaining independently falsifiable claim is successful funded User Action collection.

## Baseline and Candidates

The selected witness is one funded Mutable User Actor with Manual Trigger, one unpredicated native Transfer of one existential deposit, Persistent completion and successful execution. EXP-0088 supplies the independently qualified Pipeline baseline.

## Controls

- `Controlled variables`: EXP-0095 binding, funded User authority, Manual Trigger, complete Pipeline admission and exact Task outcome.
- `Changed variables`: Action invocation and collection only.
- `Confounders`: System fee exemption, failed/uninvoked Tasks, native-only evidence and overlapping maximum/actual owners.

## Benchmark Class, Workloads, and Criteria

- `Class`: Qualified production-path monetary and resource evidence.
- `Workload`: One successful funded User native Transfer after paid Manual Trigger and complete Pipeline admission.
- `Metrics`: Trigger, Pipeline and Action fees; Control/effect Weight; payer/Fee Sink deltas; typed receipts.
- `Acceptance`: Every owner occurs exactly once and monetary/resource equations close without overlap.
- `Rejection`: Missing receipt, duplicated debit, borrowed charge, failed Task or unsupported native-to-Wasm attribution.
