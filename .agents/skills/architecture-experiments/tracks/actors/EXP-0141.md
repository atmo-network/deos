## Decision Question

Can a preloaded occurrence-only Weight price a reachable complete Cadenced Tick Deadline dispatcher including source extraction, authority loading, Trigger collection and B+1 Service handoff?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` temporal readiness and canonical Deadline/Service authority; `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` complete bounded control owner.
- `Invariant`: A due Trigger is consumed and charged exactly once with source preservation on late refusal; its next temporal authority and pending Service publication remain bounded and atomic.
- `Reopen trigger`: The historical witness was not a reachable dispatcher path or its exact source/work provenance is contradicted by a current executable trace.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: The restored mandatory Prepass traverses due Tick and Block Deadline frontiers and charges a complete owner, not a standalone preloaded occurrence helper.
- `Reason`: The historical T1 fixture reached an indexed Cadenced source while the earlier production occurrence benchmarks omitted extraction/loading from their measured block.
- `Hypothesis`: The historical occurrence-only charge underbounds the complete due Tick worker in RefTime and ProofSize.
- `Materiality`: A single reachable underbound requires owner and selector correction; a historical correction does not confer numerical authority on later Prepass source or release Wasm.

## Result

A reachable historical complete due Tick worker measured `10,371,884,000 / 115,628` against actual admitted `2,573,584,000 / 12,979`. The correction made benchmark ownership include source extraction and useful materialization; native refusal tests pass at their recorded source.

## Interpretation

The temporal source/inner/admission owners must not be priced by a preloaded occurrence helper. This finding alone says nothing about final deep-index maxima or current restored Prepass budget; production weights and vectors need coordinated exact-tree generation.

## Decision

Measured historical counterexample; the earlier occurrence-only pricing is rejected at the T1 identity. The current mandatory Prepass binding remains open and no old numeric coefficient is adopted. Outcome class: research result.
