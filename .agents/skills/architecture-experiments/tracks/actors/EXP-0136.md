## Decision Question

Which finite workloads, populations, horizons, outcomes, resource dimensions, censoring rules and pre-result materiality tests must N6.5 and N6.3 use to decide whether the selected current-state/live-residency architecture provides a useful whole-service result without hiding polling, wake, movement, effect or cleanup work?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `BACKLOG.md` N6.1, N6.3 and N6.5; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §2.3; EXP-0135.
- `Invariant`: Workload selection cannot weaken current-state semantics, Q1, deterministic cyclic encounter order, committed-prefix durability, atomic effects, bounded wakeup, generation isolation, custody or component-wise resource admission.
- `Reopen trigger`: A supported reference economic goal or mandatory lifecycle path has no witness, a frozen fixture cannot be realized under accepted semantics, or implementation introduces a materially distinct workload owner not represented here.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: EXP-0135 selects the physical architecture but contains no empirical benefit claim. The shipped runtime still implements the historical contract, so current-lineage measurements cannot yet run.
- `Reason`: Measuring only retained Live Steps would hide negative checks, sleep/Park transfers, wake storms, owner mutation, cleanup debt or changed ordering. A post-result workload or threshold would permit cherry-picking.
- `Hypothesis`: One primary long-run current-balance workflow plus eleven bounded stress/control witnesses can detect the targeted successor-publication removal, moved topology cost, missed service, ordering changes, wake latency, inactive-population dependence and cleanup instability.
- `Materiality`: A useful result requires semantic conformance and either one additional complete primary economic workflow within the fixed horizon under the same component budgets, or a strict whole-service reduction of at least one persistent scheduler key mutation per successful nonterminal interior Step with lower total scheduler write bytes and no regression in completed workflows, p95 completion delay, ProofSize, effect settlement, retained state or terminal debt. RefTime additionally must exceed the matched local noise envelope if used. This is a mechanism-sized boundary, not a universal percentage.

## Benchmark Class, Workloads, and Criteria

- `Benchmark class`: Deterministic model/integration preparation followed by matched native stress, production-Wasm and full-runtime block evidence in N6.5/N6.3.

| Workload | State/population geometry | Hot/cold assumptions | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W1 — primary long Live economic flow | 32 Actors: 8 Fee Sink splitters, 8 Burn Actors, 8 liquidity Actors and 8 representative Users; each has 8 Steps over 64 blocks; every Step uses `Percent` of current Available changed before each Attempt | Process/ring remains resident; balances, quotes and effects are current/cold unless naturally co-read | Primary useful-work, current amount, multi-Step residency and heterogeneous System/User goal |
| W2 — funded adjacent retry | 32 three-Step Actors; Step 2 fails temporarily twice, receives funding/market recovery, then succeeds; 16-block horizon | Retry state sleeps only when later than B+1; authoritative inputs reread | Same-cursor retry, committed prefix, fresh inputs and no second Cycle |
| W3 — recurrence matrix | 8 each level-sensitive, coalesced-change, one-shot temporal and Manual Actors; 32 blocks | Repeated true state, coalesced updates and direct lookup are separate | Recurrence counts, no same-block restart and no busy future-start latch |
| W4 — true/false churn | 64 idle Actors alternate current start truth each block for 64 blocks; half have complete event Park and half deterministic timed review | No cached readiness authority | Park/Pending/Live churn break-even and negative-check ownership |
| W5 — sparse Park | 10,000 admitted identities: 32 Live, 32 Pending, 64 timed review, 9,872 event-Parked; 64 blocks | Cold parked pages; live frontier may warm naturally | Detect population scans, false scalability claims and inactive-set dependence |
| W6 — wake storm | 1,024 parked subscribers across 32 fixed pages; one shared feed receives 4 updates/block for 32 blocks while pending traversal is capacity-limited | Page/revision state cold at first touch | Coalescing, revision acknowledgment, bounded fanout, saturation and wake delay |
| W7 — Sleep and reentry | 1,024 Sleeping Actors across 32 C32 slots and 32 nonempty deadline keys; 32 become due per block for 32 blocks | Deadline page/heap paths cold at first due extraction | Deadline traversal, extraction order, B+1 eligibility and no range scan |
| W8 — owner mutation | 64 Actors; each block mutates eight head/interior/tail/singleton members across Live, Pending, Sleep and Park; 16 blocks | Reverse handles are authoritative, not prewarmed by setup | Arbitrary unlink, round integrity, replacement isolation and movement refusal |
| W9 — heavy effects under demand | 96 Actors: 32 Transfer, 16 Burn, 16 Router swap, 16 liquidity donation and 16 staking operations; 32 blocks plus matched valid User/Router demand | Effect storage remains independent of scheduler locality | Whole-service contention, effect settlement and capacity-first stop |
| W10 — cleanup under demand | Retire 256 maximum-manifest generations at block 1 while W1 continues for 64 blocks; admit replacement generations for 32 identities | Cleanup namespaces cold; no hidden idle-only budget | Debt arrival/service, retained bytes, time to recovery and new-generation isolation |
| W11 — resource-blocked head | 32 Live Actors with the first complete transition just above remaining per-pass resources and followers below it; repeat at RefTime-only and ProofSize-only boundaries | No affinity/cache bypass | Head priority, refusal retention, component separation and censoring honesty |
| W12 — bridge goals | One Fee Sink 10% allocation Cycle, one foreign-fee Burn flow, one liquidity provision flow and one representative User three-Step transfer/retry goal; 32-Actor replicated cohort where supported | Each engine uses its own legal scheduler; effect initial/final state is matched | H/L economic-goal bridge without claiming semantic or order equivalence |

- `Metrics`: Successful effects and exact postconditions; completed Cycles/workflows; committed Step sequence; detection, first-service and completion block; p50/p95/max delay; service gaps; censored Live/Pending/Sleep/Park/cleanup work; notifications, checks, false checks and retries; ring visits and header/guard operations; persistent-key reads/writes and encoded bytes by owner; ProofSize and RefTime; requested/outstanding/settled Control/effect Weight; retained bytes/holds; cleanup arrivals, service, debt and recovery horizon; ordering digest and custody delta.
- `Statistical method`: Deterministic counts and chronology run once per exact artifact and must reproduce bit-for-bit. Native wall-clock comparisons use predeclared A/B/A/B order, one warmup pair and at least five retained measured pairs; report median, p95, range and baseline drift with no post-hoc outlier removal. Stop RefTime interpretation as Inconclusive when candidate delta does not exceed the observed matched baseline envelope. Production-Wasm confirmation uses the exact final Wasm and generated Weight; it is not averaged into native timing.
- `Acceptance criteria`: Every required workload preserves its exact semantic/economic postcondition, Q1/order contract, custody and generation rules with zero unexplained loss or duplicate effect. W1 must satisfy the materiality rule. W4–W10 must show bounded maintenance with no growing unserved debt over the declared drain horizon. W5 may claim only work independent of parked population that its operation counters actually establish. H/L bridge results must list semantic and order differences beside outcomes.
- `Rejection criteria`: Any safety/correctness failure; a moved cost that erases W1 materiality; worse completed workflows, p95 completion delay, ProofSize, retained state or debt without an explicitly approved tradeoff; cleanup arrivals exceeding service without bounded recovery; dropped/coalesced work violating the current contract; baseline/candidate identity mismatch; RefTime inside noise presented as a win; or a result selected from an undeclared workload.

### Demand, horizon and censoring contract

| Mode | Fixed rule |
| --- | --- |
| Actor-only | All applicable component budgets are available to Actors; records the uncongested service frontier |
| Mixed valid demand | Alternate blocks contain ProofSize-heavy valid remarks and RefTime-heavy valid Router/User calls up to the first component refusal; Actors retain their declared service allocation |
| Maintenance burst | Cleanup and wake arrivals occur at the declared block before ordinary service; no setup work is charged as runtime work, but every resulting transition is counted |
| Primary horizon | 64 linked blocks for W1, W4, W5 and W10; shorter matrices use the exact table horizon plus 32 drain blocks when work remains |
| Censoring | At horizon end, classify every intended workflow as complete, Live, Pending, Sleeping, Parked, Disabled, Retired/cleanup or faulted; report remaining cursor/deadline and reason. Never drop unfinished work from denominators |
| Fairness/order | Compare actual service to each engine's declared order. CS1/CS0 require identical cyclic encounter/Q1 digests; H/L reports the old ticket-FIFO digest separately and cannot fail merely for an approved order difference |
| Capacity | Use exact final component budgets and generated transition owners. A supported complete transition must fit a full-block policy; otherwise classify an admission/support defect, not ordinary censoring |

## Result

A finite twelve-workload matrix now binds representative System and User goals, current multi-Step inputs, retry, recurrence, long residency, Park churn, sparse idle populations, wake storms, Sleep/reentry, mutation, heavy effects, contention and cleanup. Populations range from 32 useful Actors to 10,000 admitted identities; horizons and drain rules are explicit.

The primary useful result cannot be inferred from a local queue coefficient. It requires either one additional complete W1 workflow under the same budget or the removal of at least one persistent scheduler key mutation per successful interior Step together with lower total scheduler write bytes and no regression across the named whole-service dimensions.

## Interpretation

The matrix gives N6.5 a physical-operation accounting surface and N6.3 an end-to-end economic surface. W1 is the materiality owner; W4–W10 prevent a W1 gain from being purchased through polling, index churn or cleanup debt. W12 preserves the old release as an economic-goal bridge only. The matrix does not require every workload to improve and does not assign an arbitrary aggregate score.

## Decision

Accept this matrix as the N6.1 workload and materiality freeze. Outcome class: research result. Finite stopping basis: every BACKLOG-required family has one bounded witness, all comparison identities and outcomes are separated, and candidate acceptance can no longer choose workloads or thresholds after results. N6.1 design exit is closed; implementation of fixtures and all measurements remain with N6.5/N6.3.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: The finite workload set, populations, horizons, demand modes, outcomes, censoring, metrics, baseline separation, materiality and statistical stop rules for N6.5/N6.3.
- `Does not establish`: Runtime fixture existence, any measured benefit, break-even, production Weight, Wasm identity, workload outcome, final candidate acceptance or release readiness.
- `Known limitations`: Final numeric runtime bounds may require population reduction only if the accepted support contract itself is smaller; such a conflict reopens this record rather than silently resizing a run. RefTime remains host-dependent.
- `Invalidation triggers`: Changed supported economic goals, recurrence/Q1/order semantics, resource policy, lifecycle/wake architecture, final bounds below a frozen legal population, or a newly required independent service owner.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: Residency is useful only when complete-path operation removal exceeds movement, current-check and maintenance cost while economic work remains at least equal.
