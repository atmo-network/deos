## Decision Question

Does the accepted Crossing placed pair fallback converge into one retained production execution path with its reach and impact set explicitly classified, and with no rejected implementation, obsolete selector or unused diagnostic machinery remaining in the tree?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §2 owns component-wise admission, reservation-before-mutation with valid-actual settlement, and the materialization family rotation; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §7.6 owns the one-third Actor Control ceiling; `BACKLOG.md` P5.1 owns the convergence exit.
- `Invariant`: One production admission ladder only; the retained change preserves semantics, storage ownership, bounds, no-std, TryRuntime and the public embedding contract.
- `Reopen trigger`: A second competing admission path or selector, a reachable storage, bound or ABI change, or a failed declared build mode.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: [EXP-0110](./EXP-0110.md) sealed the generated two-candidate placed owner as the selected efficiency improvement. The worktree carries it in the production admission ladder: aggregate owner → pair owner → scalar single → refusal, with the pair restricted to `FireCohortPlacedBatch` and more than two classified candidates.
- `Reason`: P5.1 must converge the retained implementation and classify its impact set before P5.2 regenerates production bindings and P5.3 validates the final production cohort.
- `Hypothesis`: The campaign leaves exactly one production execution path; the retained change reaches no storage, bound, TryRuntime or embedding-contract surface; every declared build mode compiles; and every affected test, measurement, consumer and historical claim has a named owner and state.
- `Materiality`: This is a convergence decision, not a benchmark. A leftover competing path or selector, any reachable storage/config/ABI drift, a failed declared build mode, or an unclassified affected surface falsifies it. The claim is decided when the campaign diff audit, the compile-reach matrix, the focused regressions and the impact inventory all pass.

## Result

All four declared build modes succeed on the convergence tree, both pallet ladder witnesses and both runtime witnesses pass on the retained implementation, and the campaign diff audits to one production function, one ladder branch and their tests with no storage, config, Weight, metadata or client change. The only residual campaign artifact was one obsolete doc-comment label, now rewritten for retained behavior. No rejected implementation, obsolete selector or unused diagnostic machinery remains.

## Interpretation

Convergence holds on the audited tree. The retained change's executable reach is exactly one admission branch in the pallet; storage ownership, bounds, TryRuntime hooks and the public embedding contract are untouched, and the declared build matrix covers no-std, try-runtime, benchmark and independent embedding. The impact inventory assigns binding work to P5.2, final-cohort replay to P5.3 and consumer prose to P6.2, so no affected surface is left ownerless. The simplification opportunity here is limited to the witness label; the P3/P4 witnesses remain required regression and reproduction evidence rather than deletable scaffolding.

## Decision

Accepted. This Leaf seals convergence evidence, not a new candidate comparison; the campaign outcome class remains [EXP-0110](./EXP-0110.md)'s efficiency improvement. Finite stopping basis: the P5.1 exit criteria are decided by the diff audit, the compile-reach matrix, the focused regressions and the complete impact inventory, and further convergence measurement cannot change this result without a source or consumer change.
