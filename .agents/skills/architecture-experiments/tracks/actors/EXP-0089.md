## Decision Question

Can one qualified reactive executed path compose detector, deferred fanout and scheduled service charges without omission or duplication?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §§4.2, 4.3, 4.6 owns reactive semantics; `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §§4, 6 owns admission/actual Weight and phase composition. BACKLOG owns open G6 gates.
- `Invariant`: One paid useful latch transition; deferred fanout never executes Actors; scheduler alone owns service.
- `Reopen trigger`: A proposed change to causal or fee semantics belongs to specification.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Reason`: Temporal and System accounting do not establish remaining reactive executed-path composition.
- `Hypothesis`: Qualified reactive resource ownership composes exactly once across detector/fanout/service.
- `Materiality`: Any omitted or duplicated executed owner defeats complete-path accounting.
