## Decision Question

Does rebinding the family minimum to cover EXP-0143 W2 require changing the configured 20% contribution or the integrity invariant, even though the live shared scheduler can lend from Crossing?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` bounded deferred ObservationChange fanout and `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` shared materialization scheduling.
- `Invariant`: A complete charged page must fit both the runtime integrity condition requiring a family minimum within its nominal contribution and the live shared two-dimensional scheduling budget; these are different gates.
- `Reopen trigger`: The integrity minimum assertion or shared materialization scheduler changes.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: `lib.rs::materialization_weight_limit()` adds Crossing and ObservationChange configured 20% contributions into a shared 40% envelope. `service_materialization_families()` reserves minima, rotates first access and lends the rest; the worker does not independently check the 20% contribution. Separately, `lib.rs::integrity_test()` asserts `materialization_family_minimum(1).all_lte(ObservationFanoutWeightLimit)` before runtime operation. The runtime test `observation_fanout_minimum_reserves_probe_page_and_fault_capacity` repeats this assertion.
- `Reason`: EXP-0143 W2 page measures 1,010,826 ProofSize, already 10,826 above the 1,000,000 ProofSize nominal 20% contribution.
- `Hypothesis`: A W2-covering production family minimum violates the current runtime integrity invariant, even though the same comparison alone does not prove a live page refusal in the shared scheduler.
- `Materiality`: Correct binding cannot pass the runtime integrity gate unchanged; confusing configuration-time validity with live per-turn refusal misdirects the remedy and liveness claim.

## Measurements

EXP-0143 owns W2 `1,010,826` ProofSize and `22,125,448,000` RefTime base + 1,352 reads/326 writes. Retained fanout base `1,629` ProofSize, branch probe `3,587`, fault record `4,106`: a selected complete quantum of `1,020,148` ProofSize. This is 20,148 above the **startup integrity bound** on `materialization_family_minimum(1)`; a correct minimum containing W2 must be at least that large. The live combined materialization cap is separately 2,000,000 ProofSize, with later-family minimum reservation and conditional lending, but the prepass first clips it to remaining Actor Control. This arithmetic establishes the integrity failure, not live worker admission; EXP-0145 identifies the tighter outer-domain ProofSize gate.

## Result

With a W2-covering minimum and retained base/probe/fault, the runtime integrity assertion fails by at least 20,148 ProofSize under the unchanged 20% contribution. The **live** family budget instead uses a shared 40% envelope, rotated reservation of later-family minima and lending; this gap does not itself imply worker refusal.

## Interpretation

An honest page rebinding must reconcile the 1,000,000 ProofSize startup invariant, update the family minimum and prove that the outer Actor Control cap and shared envelope admit required quanta in both rotations, while preserving Crossing, Service, mandatory Deadline Prepass and fixed `on_idle` headroom in both dimensions. Earlier statements that a *live worker* necessarily refuses W2 at a separate 20% cap were incorrect and are withdrawn; the physical underbound in EXP-0143 is unaffected.

## Decision

Reject unchanged 20% startup integrity after an honest W2-containing family minimum; invalidate the separate live 20% family-ceiling interpretation. Defer whether to change the configured contribution or justify a different startup invariant until shared-budget/Service headroom closure. No Weight, runtime ratio, metadata or Wasm is adopted.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: The prior live-refusal inference was invalid, but a W2-covering minimum violates the separate startup integrity check with unchanged 20% configuration.
- `Does not establish`: A universal full-page maximum, first/second rotation liveness, corrected family minimum, reserve adequacy, a new ratio or release throughput.
- `Known limitations`: Selected W2 page and retained overhead; no measured Crossing contention or final production Weight.
- `Invalidation triggers`: The startup family-minimum assertion or nominal ratio changes; shared lending changes its live-admission interpretation.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: A configured contribution can bind startup validity even when it does not bound each live turn separately.
