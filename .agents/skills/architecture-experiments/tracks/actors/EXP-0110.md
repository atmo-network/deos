## Decision Question

On the pinned generated Weight and runtime constants, does the retained pair-owner fallback candidate, compared through the fixed 0.7.26 comparison matrix against the sealed released `v0.7.25` cohort, constitute a reproducible useful improvement or approved tradeoff to select for the release line, or must it be rejected as a research result — with the outcome class and the finite stopping basis named?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `BACKLOG.md` P4.3 owns the controlled comparison and its exit; `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` §1 evidence contract, §1.1 two-axis reporting and Pareto exposure, §1.2 required cost metrics, §1.4 outcome classification; `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §2 component-wise admission, reservation-before-mutation with valid-actual settlement and the materialization family rotation; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §7.6 one-third Actor Control ceiling.
- `Invariant`: One scheduler, strict FIFO, Q1, the causal floor and cutoff, component-wise admission, reservation-before-mutation, cursor-rotation materialization fairness, and the per-block component counters remain unchanged; the candidate takes no additional guaranteed in-block capacity; no semantic, ordering, rollback, fee, custody or failure behavior changes.
- `Reopen trigger`: A finding that selection would require changing semantics, guarantees, resource policy or admission authority rather than a physical fallback ladder; or a required crossing-relevant matrix row that cannot conform under the candidate.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: The released binding collapses a classified multi-candidate placed batch to the scalar single when its aggregate owner does not fit the grant; its executed materialization rotation is `1/3/8` and its reproduced lean grants settle one scalar candidate ([EXP-0103](./EXP-0103.md), [EXP-0104](./EXP-0104.md)). The retained candidate reduces the batch to the generated pair owner first and executes `2/6/8` ([EXP-0106](./EXP-0106.md)); its bounded correctness and resource claims are closed with no correction in [EXP-0109](./EXP-0109.md).
- `Reason`: P4.2 decided the candidate's mandatory claims but sealed no performance outcome; P4.3 must now decide selection and outcome class before any release line, binding or public claim consumes the candidate.
- `Hypothesis`: The pair fallback is a reproducible efficiency improvement: lean phases admit their classified scalar-equivalent placed pairs where the released binding admits one scalar candidate, the executed steady cycle rises from `12` to `16` at unchanged ProofSize, settled per-candidate resources fall, and the crossing-relevant required matrix rows stay green on the candidate tree.
- `Materiality`: Declared before the candidate-side run set from the campaign decision: select when (a) the frozen [EXP-0105](./EXP-0105.md) materiality is met against the sealed baseline — at least one previously lean rotation phase admits its classified scalar-equivalent placed pair in one admission decision and the executed steady cycle rises above `12`; (b) every settlement stays at or below its generated owner, with unchanged family minima and no per-candidate Weight worsening; (c) the crossing-relevant required profiles of the [fixed comparison matrix](./experiments.md#fixed-comparison-matrix-0726-p2) conform on the candidate tree; and (d) the new cost is bounded and stated. Reject when any falsifier fails. This is a comparative decision against a sealed baseline, never a universal percentage, and the retired `10,000`/`100` horizon stays closed.

## Result

On the candidate tree the matched witness reproduced every EXP-0106 value exactly: the executed rotation is `1` opening then the steady `2/6/8` cycle, the whole 48-member cohort materializes exactly once and completes inside block 11 against released block 13, control ProofSize is identical on every phase, and the phase RefTime deltas equal exactly one pair premium (steady cursor-zero) and three pair premiums (rotated-first). All five crossing witnesses, the six additional full-Executive campaigns and both detector-scale profiles passed with their exact assertions. No settlement exceeded its generated owner; the single-check boundary, aggregate residue and family minima were unchanged.

## Interpretation

The binding dimension of the candidate is ProofSize, and the pair owner is free in that dimension: it costs the same `162,782` ProofSize as the scalar single, so the fallback switches the admission mode without taking additional guaranteed in-block capacity — the Crossing family minimum already reserved the pair boundary. The measured new cost is a runtime-bound RefTime premium (`775,848,000` per additional candidate), which raises the steady cycle total by `3,103,392,000` while settling each additional candidate below the released per-candidate cost, so both settled per-candidate RefTime and settled per-candidate ProofSize fall. Service improves on the same resource policy: more admitted candidates per decision, a shorter materialization horizon, and no correctness, ordering, rollback, fee, custody or failure change. The measured gain is the executed cycle under the same per-decision guarantees, not a tighter advertised maximum, and the pair path is repeated lean-phase service, not an initialization discount. No member of the measured cohort is censored: all 48 materialize exactly once, and the detector-scale profiles assert their declared convergence and completion assertions. The applicable domain is dense same-threshold funded User ObservationCrossing materialization on the pinned binding with the per-decision cohort retained at the generated pair; the executed cycle remains below the `24`-candidate counter bound, and the retired `10,000`/`100` horizon is not reopened. Because the candidate touches only the admission ladder, lifecycle create/update/close cost, persistent state and repeated-use behavior are unchanged: the lifecycle guard and the non-crossing matrix rows stay reusable, and no repeated-use claim follows from this comparison.

## Decision

Accepted. Decision scope: selection of the retained pair-owner fallback for the Crossing placed-batch fallback ladder in the 0.7.26 line, under the physical architecture only. Outcome class: **efficiency improvement**. Finite stopping basis: the frozen EXP-0105 materiality is met on the matched witness (lean phases admit their classified scalar-equivalent placed pairs; executed steady cycle `16 > 12`), every settlement stays within its generated owner with unchanged minima and no per-candidate Weight worsening, the crossing-relevant required profiles conform on the candidate tree, and the new cost is bounded and stated. The frozen candidate set stops at the first material candidate, so the count-parameterized admitted-batch owner and the demand-aware family-minimum reservation remain unadmitted and outside the release gate; exact-Wasm candidate replay, Weight/binding regeneration and the funded-herd fixture stay with P5.2/P5.3 or a new explicit scope decision. P6.2 reconciles public architecture and Wiki prose from the accepted implementation after P5 convergence.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: the selection and outcome class of the retained pair-owner fallback under the controlled comparison on the pinned generated Weight; the matched phase comparison against the sealed released cohort; conformance of the crossing-relevant required integrated rows on the candidate tree; the stated RefTime-only new cost and the unchanged ProofSize, minima and reservation boundaries.
- `Does not establish`: exact-Wasm candidate execution or production Wasm identity; funded-User legal-herd service; lifecycle or repeated-use improvements; wall-clock or host behavior; market/economic outcomes; total throughput beyond the measured composition.
- `Known limitations`: native full-runtime rather than exact-Wasm; one 48-member fixture carries the local comparison; detector-scale profiles drain with `Weight::MAX` grants; full-Executive campaigns exercise crossing at witness scale; no funded-User crossing cohort at legal herd scale exists, the conditional P2.3 observability item stayed inert because the phase-level yields already decided the candidate, and a funded-herd fixture now requires a new explicit scope decision.
- `Invalidation triggers`: a regenerated Weight binding that moves the pair premium or boundary endpoints; a changed family rotation, minimum set, grant algebra, admission ladder or counter cap; a required integrated row that fails under a later binding; a P5 finding that reopens correctness or containment.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: the shared materialization schedule already reserved the pair boundary for the Crossing family; the fallback activates capacity the family minimum guaranteed, and the RefTime premium is the only new cost.
