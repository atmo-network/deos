## Decision Question

Did ticket-addressed frame-owned Actor Control geometry preserve DEOS semantics and bounded ownership sufficiently to replace the prior scalar Actor Control geometry?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact sections`: Actors specification → sole-owner rule, canonical partitions, Trigger occurrence, Pipeline/Cycle, attempt transaction, placement/temporal readiness, Prepass/cutoff/FIFO service and liveness; resource policy → admission/actual Weight and canonical block phases; performance assurance → evidence contract and ten-thousand Actor targets. Owners: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md`, `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md`, `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md`.
- `Invariant`: Strict class-neutral FIFO, Q1, immutable global ticket chronology/cutoff, one Step per Actor per block, `N -> N+1-or-later`, one active plus one deferred Pipeline, independent committed prefixes, atomic effects, exact Trigger/Pipeline/Action fees and custody neutrality.
- `Reopen trigger`: Only a new experiment proving semantic unsoundness, unenforceable required boundedness, unavoidable duplicate mutable authority, unavoidable FIFO/Q1/causal-floor violation, or physical impossibility of any sound production Weight envelope can reopen C1. A proposal must identify the frozen invariant → exact evidence → why repair inside C1 is impossible → why macrogeometry must change.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Original pressure`: Scalar control exposed the need for canonical physical ownership. The accepted decision and necessary invariants remain here; successor records own unresolved questions.
- `Current architecture`: Retained production source implements C1. Architecture selection is closed; resource/admission/binding/throughput acceptance transfers to successors.
- `Hypothesis decided`: One process-primary plus bounded physical locators/references preserves the semantic machine without a second execution owner.
- `Decision scope`: Physical architecture only. C1 Architecture Freeze removes macrogeometry from active optimization; it is not the later 0.7.25 Geometry Freeze release gate.

## Decision

**C1 PHYSICAL GEOMETRY: FROZEN. Status: Accepted. Decision scope: physical architecture only.**

Established physical basis:

- One canonical mutable process-primary per Active Actor in Unsignaled/Ready/Waiting, bounded `ActorControlLocator` lookup and frame-owned lifecycle/hot/admission authority.
- Ticket-addressed Ready ordering, immutable global chronology, strict FIFO/Q1/causal floor, bounded physical span and no live-cell compaction.
- Waiting primary/reference distinction; at most one Block and one Tick reference in addition to the primary; references own no process state.
- Linked nonempty Waiting pages with bounded unlink/reclaim, shared deadline ordering, mixed-clock fail-closed arbitration and no second scheduler.
- Exact source consumption/successor publication, lifecycle and Trigger semantic equivalence, no production scalar compatibility owner and no second execution algorithm.

Not established: final production Weight, reachable maximum admission, complete Waiting/heap envelope, final Wasm/metadata, W0–W9, 100/100, residual proof optimality or release acceptance.

High coefficients, expensive Contract/heap branches, conservative ProofSize, stale Weight, unreachable fixtures, missed throughput, possible page/bitmap/shared-body/key-locality improvements or simplification preference do not reopen C1. Correctness repairs, dead-code deletion, redundant-read removal, fixture repair, Weight ownership and artifact regeneration may proceed within frozen invariants under successor ownership. No successor implementation begins in this decomposition.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: Physical selection/freeze, bounded ownership model and semantic preservation within retained evidence.
- `Does not establish`: Weight soundness, every maximum Contract fitting, final generated identity, throughput or 0.7.25 acceptance.
- `Known limitations`: G3 audits/corrections and successor resource questions remain mandatory. Old direct-executor results cannot certify ordinary production service.
- `Invalidation triggers`: Numeric evidence expires with relevant source/configuration/artifact change; physical freeze reopens only for the five qualifying failures in Governing Specification.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: Ticket order is physical order; references never acquire process authority; no silent second execution owner.
