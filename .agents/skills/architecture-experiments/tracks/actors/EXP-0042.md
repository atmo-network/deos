### Deciding Public-Close Owner

The retained reference-Wasm evidence partitions the zero-Step live-cursor diagnostic, admitted Manual retry/capture profiles and the joint retry/Crossing profile. The joint profile directly attains O1's smallest falsifier: ordinary admission and a real failed liquidity attempt produce a maximum-Step Suspended User Run; host publications create the deferred Crossing latch and live cursor; public owner close then removes the Contract, three cold tails, Run head/payload, one future Waiting frame, Crossing pages/locators and the ordinary owner's Account/Hold state while preserving custody and resuming the moved guard.

At admitted `(O=4, P=12)`, its generated vector is `742,998,816` pre-database RefTime, `38,096` estimated ProofSize, 34 reads and 32 writes. Under the pinned reference RocksDB charge, the complete RefTime is `4,792,998,816`. This vector dominates the retained zero-Step cursor owner (`429,391,000 / 31,541`, 26 reads / 24 writes) and admitted Manual capture profiles component-wise, so no subtraction or synthetic coordinate envelope is required.

This accepts one inclusive public-close atom over the declared admitted reference state family. Contract/Run deletion, singleton Waiting removal, Crossing cursor repair, lifecycle cleanup and applicable owner-hold release occur once. Observation terminal cleanup remains the mutually exclusive EXP-0040 branch. EXP-0063 owns populated Waiting pages and competing heap-repair alternatives and must reconcile their already-inclusive close traces without charging Contract/Run/lifecycle work twice. EXP-0065 owns exact generation; the diagnostic fragment remains uninstalled.
