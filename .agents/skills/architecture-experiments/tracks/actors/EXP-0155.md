## Decision Question

Would changing only DEOS maximum block ProofSize from `5,000,000` to the proposed `7,000,000`, keeping the 35% Message Queue reservation and exactly one-third Actor Control, admit the selected full-page W2-only Prepass floor `1,203,228` without consuming Shared Economic capacity?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §§1–3; `template/runtime/src/configs/mod.rs::MessageQueueServiceWeight` and `FixedBlockWeightComponentsValue`.
- `Invariant`: Fixed context remains reserved before component-wise one-third Control; Actor effects remain in Shared Economic. A block limit must not exceed the target relay-chain's *verified* configuration.
- `Reopen trigger`: A normative change to the fixed owner, Actor Control share, or provenance of the selected Prepass floor requires independent acceptance rather than treating this candidate as a production setting.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: `MAXIMUM_BLOCK_WEIGHT` is `2,000,000,000,000 / 5,000,000`; Message Queue fixed service reserves 35% of each block component. The retained ProofSize fixed envelope is `2,971,548..=2,971,550`, reconstructed from `floor((5,000,000 - fixed)/3) = 676,150`.
- `Reason`: Comparing a seven-million block with an *unchanged* fixed reserve would falsely claim W2-only feasibility; increasing the block also increases Message Queue's fixed ProofSize.
- `Hypothesis`: Seven million remains insufficient after recomputing the fixed reservation.
- `Materiality`: Reject or retain this specific single-knob candidate with a two-dimensional domain partition; do not propose a new production block ceiling or edit runtime configuration.

## Result

At seven million the 35% Message Queue reservation rises by `700,000` ProofSize, so fixed becomes `3,671,548..=3,671,550`. Exactly one-third Control is only `1,109,483..=1,109,484`, below the selected W2-only complete Prepass floor by at least `93,744`. Separate Shared Economic still receives over `2,218,967` ProofSize, not available to close the Control gap.

## Interpretation

Treating fixed as constant would incorrectly calculate roughly `1,342,817` Control and accept W2. Under the same *conditional* constant-other-owner model, the mathematical minimum block ProofSize for Crossing-plus-mandatory is `5,331,062..=5,331,065`, W2-only `7,432,664..=7,432,667`, and W2-plus-Crossing same-block `10,039,437..=10,039,441`. These are **necessary arithmetic screens for stale floors**, not acceptable block limits, relay PoV guarantees, full worst-case bounds, or reason to bypass permitted family rotation. Neither SDK's `10 MiB` primitive nor a locally chosen block Weight proves the target relay's `max_pov_size`.

## Decision

Rejected: do not raise the DEOS block ProofSize to seven million as a stand-alone solution to the selected W2-only Control shortfall. Outcome class: research result. No runtime, generated Weight, Wasm, metadata, or reserve policy changed. Stop this single-knob candidate rather than tuning a numeric ceiling without target-relay and complete owner evidence.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: Conditional arithmetic rejection of the seven-million W2-only single-knob candidate in both domain ownership and binding ProofSize; selected Crossing-plus-mandatory lower bound alone is affordable at seven million.
- `Does not establish`: Production-safe larger block, full maximum-header Crossing/Deadline liveness, universal Q12 upper, target relay `max_pov_size`, Executive PoV, or shared-user capacity under actual extrinsics.
- `Known limitations`: The current fixed envelope and selected floors are stale; hypothetical capacity holds all non-MQ fixed ProofSize constant; RefTime is an unchanged configured partition, not a physical selector proof.
- `Invalidation triggers`: Configured reserve, fixed owner, generated weights or relay target changes.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: Enlarging the ProofSize ceiling expands the fixed Message Queue reserve as well as Control; increasing block size yields less than one-third of the apparent raw increase for Actors.
