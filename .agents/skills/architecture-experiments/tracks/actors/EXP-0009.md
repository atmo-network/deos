## Decision Question

Does a P128 page with a generated 128-candidate Crossing cohort fit every complete branch inside the component-wise Actor Control and worker envelopes while preserving exact state and 10,000-membership capacity?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` → `3.5 ObservationCrossing`, `15.2.9 Production Bounds`; `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` → Actor Control allocation; `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` → Crossing production evidence.
- `Invariant`: One Trigger instance may materialize one bounded contiguous initial cohort without changing strict FIFO, Q1, N+1 eligibility, class neutrality, useful-transition latching, exact non-tail refill, or fault isolation.
- `Reopen trigger`: Meeting the target requires priority, same-block execution, relaxed rollback, multiple committed Steps, unbounded traversal, or changed Trigger semantics.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: Production P64 admits four homogeneous candidates per generated cohort and falls back to scalar service on mixed authority. EXP-0008 shows P128 is the smallest page capable of one-page 128-candidate snapshots; P256 is materially more expensive for the required frontier.
- `Reason`: The release requires at least 128 generated candidates when the complete branch fits and at least 10,000 admitted feed memberships.
- `Hypothesis`: Existing contiguous snapshot, batch preflight, grouped queue append, and exact tail-refill topology scales parametrically to 128 without semantic redesign; generated slopes expose whether Actor Control, ProofSize, writes, or queue admission binds.
- `Materiality`: C128 must fit every reachable complete branch component-wise and retain positive Actor execution floor. A preflight-only win is insufficient.

## Controls

- `Controlled variables`: Toolchain, compiled-Wasm benchmark method, database/cache, 50 steps, 20 repeats, radix and transition topology, Trigger semantics, actor class mix, contracts, queue state, feed population, worker envelope, and all non-page/cohort runtime constants.
- `Changed variables`: Observation page bound 64 → 128, cohort bound 4 → 128, matching benchmark component range 4 → 128, and for H0 only the physical owner of paired Crossing routing plus Idle activation inputs.
- `Confounders`: Page and cohort bounds move together because P64 cannot represent a 128-entry contiguous page cohort. EXP-0008 independently measured the page-only cost, allowing the cohort increment to be interpreted against P128 page evidence.

- `Metrics`: Parameterized RefTime/ProofSize, reads, writes, maximum branch Weight, Actor Control fit, worker-envelope fit, queue placements, exact roots, faults, Wasm bytes, and completion blocks.
- `Statistical method`: Generate candidate owners at 50 steps/20 repeats against the matching benchmark Wasm and inspect intercept/slope in both dimensions. Deterministic state tests compare exact roots and event/order evidence; no samples are discarded.
- `Acceptance criteria`: C128 fits every complete branch component-wise, preserves positive Actor execution floor and exact semantics, admits 10,000 memberships, and produces sound parameterized Weight through 128.
- `Rejection criteria`: Any envelope overflow, missing actual owner, invalid generated slope, queue/capacity failure, stale locator, partial mutation, semantic regression, or inability to complete the declared maximum profile.

## Statistical / Variance Notes

Five preflight and three complete mutation owners used 50 steps/20 repeats against matching candidate Wasms. Terminal preflight is the maximum preflight at 5,480,760,592 RefTime plus 1,026 database reads and 608,981 ProofSize. Tail and non-tail emptied commits execute 128 candidates. A first trimmed fixture requested 128 moves from a 130-member tail, which creates a third page and correctly fails authority revalidation; the corrected maximum reachable trimmed branch moves 126 from a full 128-entry physical tail and leaves two entries.

## Result

H0 makes all five 128-candidate preflight families and the 128-candidate tail commit fit Actor Control ProofSize. Non-tail remains invalid: exact source-plus-tail refill validation reads 256 `CrossingMemberships` reverse locators, whose `2,549`-byte proof contribution dominates at 653,534. The tail branch reads 128 locators and fits at 346,462. Stale compiled authority falls back to generic classification; complete non-tail and maximum profiles remain pending.

## Interpretation

ProofSize remains binding. H0 validates header-free classification and tail C128; N64 preserves exact source-plus-tail bijection checks at `327,262` ProofSize, 78,428 below Actor Control. The branch-sensitive cap changes only how much contiguous authority one transaction admits: tail remains 128, non-tail remains strict source order at 64, and no locator validation, ordering, semantics, or global allocation is weakened.

## Decision

Accept P128 Crossing pages with H0 C128 tail/preflight and exact N64 non-tail. Reject W10/W14, P256, uniform non-tail C128, and shared P128 broad-fanout ownership. Crossing and broad ObservationChange now own independent P128/P64 page bounds; complete regenerated Actors Weight fits Actor Control, with synchronized metadata/ABI and canonical production Wasm evidence. Exact release-tree attestation remains release work.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: A compile-coherent C128-P128 candidate and predeclared complete evidence contract.
- `Does not establish`: Envelope fit, production selection, or 10,000-member throughput.
- `Known limitations`: Generated complete branches, two maximum release profiles, and first/middle/final mismatch boundaries pass. Production Weight regeneration remains pending. A first Running/Suspended or commitment-mismatched candidate receives one exact generic scalar fallback; a later mismatch truncates before that candidate and commits only the preceding compact prefix.
- `Invalidation triggers`: Page selection, cohort semantics, FIFO batching, latching, queue geometry, resource policy, worker envelope, or benchmark method changes.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: The decision is controlled by both complete grouped mutation and repeated Contract-head proof; page-local routing plus compact Idle placement can amortize the Trigger cause without caching Step semantics.

## Follow-Up / Next Gradient

```text
P128 target-enabling page → C128 W10 ProofSize failure
→ global Actor Control rejects W14 → isolate Contract-head slope
→ H0 page-local paired routing + compact Idle placement
→ exact fallback/rollback → generated envelope → 10,000-member profile
```

- `Eliminated hypotheses`: P64 alone can represent a one-page 128-candidate cohort.
- `Remaining hypotheses`: C128 tail plus N64 exact non-tail service fits Actor Control and preserves throughput better than weakening reverse-locator validation.
- `Stop condition`: Accept C128-P128 with complete generated and exact-root evidence, or reject it with one identified binding owner and a narrower conforming successor.
