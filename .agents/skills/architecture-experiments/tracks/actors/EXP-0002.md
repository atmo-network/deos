## Decision Question

Can the accepted `MaxContractSteps = 32` production profile replace the current 8-Step runtime baseline while maximum create/update/close and current-Step execution remain component-wise dispatchable, semantically identical short Contracts pay no global-ceiling tax, and maximum retained state remains bounded; if not, does controlled 16/8 evidence locate a physical blocker for another conforming candidate or demonstrate a genuine semantic infeasibility that requires explicit Specification reopening?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact sections`: [`template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §1.4 Block-Paced Actor Cycles, §3.1 Complexity, Identity, and Admission, §4.0 Task Resource Boundary, §4.4 Atomicity, §10 Runtime Configuration, and §11 Conformance](../../../../../template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md); [`docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §1 Resource Domain, §2 Resource Algebra, §4 Admission and Actual Weight, and §5 Maximum Step Fit and FIFO Fragmentation](../../../../../docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md); [`docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` §1 Evidence Contract and §4 Contract Complexity Matrix](../../../../../docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md).
- `Backlog contract`: [`BACKLOG.md` No Ceiling Tax, Quantitative Release Contract, and I1 Max Steps](../../../../../BACKLOG.md) fix production at 32 unless Specification is explicitly reopened; 8 and 16 are comparative profiles only.
- `Invariant — No Ceiling Tax`: Raising the global ceiling MUST NOT add cold-body reads, unused Predicate work, full-pipeline orchestration or fees, maximum-pipeline ProofSize, maximum state hold, or unread fragments to an otherwise identical one-, two-, or three-Step Contract.
- `Invariant — lifecycle dispatchability`: Maximum admitted create, activate, update, deactivate, cancel, and close geometry MUST fit the owning runtime dispatch envelope component-wise; maximum close MUST remove all bounded body/run/topology authority atomically without an unbounded prefix scan or orphan.
- `Invariant — component-wise Weight`: RefTime and ProofSize are independent stop conditions. Reads, writes, persistent bytes, state hold, loaded fragments, and Wasm size remain separate evidence and MUST NOT be collapsed into one score.
- `Invariant — semantics parity`: Candidate ceilings change no FIFO order, Actor class treatment, causal delay, one-Step-per-block pacing, Task behavior, fee origin, rollback boundary, semantic Contract identity, run-state ownership, or current-Step outcome.
- `Reopen trigger`: Reopen Specification only for a demonstrated semantic defect or infeasibility that remains after relevant conforming physical candidates are rejected. One P32 implementation exceeding an envelope rejects that physical candidate; it does not by itself invalidate the normative ceiling. P16/B8 may locate a threshold but cannot authorize a lower production ceiling.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: The current baseline binds the admitted semantic Contract to one C6 head with inline Step 0, authority-bound tail chunks of at most four Steps, an admission certificate carrying one resource envelope per Step, and a block-paced executor that loads only the current fragment after Opening. The DEOS runtime still binds `(MaxContractSteps, MaxFundingTrackedAssets, MaxOpeningSnapshotEntries, MaxOpeningPredicateResults) = (8, 10, 16, 32)`.
- `Current runtime bounds`: `MaxBlockWeight = Weight(2,000,000,000,000 RefTime, 5,000,000 ProofSize)`, Normal dispatch is 50%, minimum `on_idle` reserve is 50%, `MaxExecutionsPerBlock = 1,000`, and both Active Actor and queue capacity are 10,000. Actor service applies the accepted dual-meter policy and current-Step resource envelopes.
- `Current implementation gap`: The accepted 0.7.24 specification fixes 32 while production remains at 8 pending this experiment. Preparation found that lifecycle benchmarks still authored one-Step bodies and the Contract-level idle-fit gate still summed a whole cycle despite current-Step execution. The harness now builds maximum C6 geometry for production create/activate/deactivate/update/close owners, asserts exact tail cardinality or complete removal, and admits the component-wise maximum individual current-Step envelope rather than a suffix sum.
- `Reason`: I1 cannot bind 32 from a count constant. It needs controlled production-Wasm and generated Weight evidence that separates short-Contract invariance from maximum lifecycle/state growth and one current-Step fit.
- `Hypothesis`: With only the ceiling tuple changed, P32 remains bounded and dispatchable; short Contract execution and state footprints are invariant across B8/P16/P32 except for an explicitly identified fixed runtime-owned cause; maximum retained geometry grows no faster than the admitted Step/body/snapshot bounds.
- `Materiality`: A semantic mismatch, one added cold-body read/write/fragment for a matched short Contract, any short-Contract persistent-byte/state-hold increase caused only by the global ceiling, any ProofSize increase caused by an unread maximum, or any component-wise dispatchability failure is material regardless of percentage. For otherwise structurally identical generated RefTime models, candidate medians must remain within ±5% of B8; this predeclared bound conservatively exceeds the largest `3.36%` preparation delta between 10/5 diagnostics and 50/20 B8 lifecycle generation. Five 50/20 short-path samples per profile are retained with no outlier removal. Smaller differences remain reportable and cannot excuse a structural tax. For maximum geometry, superlinear growth above the exact bound-derived cardinality plus one fixed header is material.

## Interpretation

The initial matrix made P32 appear capability-dominant because actual short C6 bytes and short FIFO ProofSize/reads/writes were invariant, both candidate RefTime medians satisfied the ±5% equivalence bound, and maximum geometry grew linearly. The first production preflight exposed overlapping maximum-control ProofSize; an atomic maximum-Opening owner corrected that defect, and exact P32 regeneration then fit the conservative control envelope at `Weight(1,559,647,000, 179,078)`. Final generated Weight nevertheless falsified the broader No Ceiling Tax claim: dense observation fanout over identical one-Step subscribers inherited larger maximum encoded admission/funding/run bounds, increasing unit ProofSize `300,510 → 331,998` and reducing service under the unchanged 20% block envelope from three pages to two. The FIFO benchmark had omitted this reactive activation branch. P32 is therefore not a conforming physical candidate even though its maximum lifecycle and control branches fit. P16 remains comparative only; selecting it cannot satisfy the normative 32-Step ceiling. A successor topology must separate compact activation authority from extended execution payloads before fresh measurement.
