## Decision Question

What is the actual reachable domain of Actor state under authored Contract semantics and host bounds?

## Governing Specification

- `Owners`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` (sole ownership, lifecycle/Trigger/Pipeline/Action semantics, placement and scheduler); `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` (component-wise admission/actual settlement, equal-thirds block policy); `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` (evidence and target).
- `Frozen physical input`: [EXP-0025](./EXP-0025.md) C1 Architecture Freeze. No change to FIFO, ticket chronology, Q1, causal floor, primary/reference ontology, source/successor atomicity or sole execution owner.
- `Semantic oracle`: A0 SHA-256 `8746e423168309975e509be91e4eb06fdf0eb0b43369d75f1ee62202a4341678` remains frozen; use only for relevant semantic equivalence, never as a Weight oracle.
- `Scope`:

- Own relationships among Step count, Opening amount surfaces, Opening predicate results, funding-tracked assets, current predicates, Run payload and head/tail fragment geometry.
- Prove mutually exclusive amount-source families, DNF representability and host-specific bounds. Separate reachable maxima, conservative storage-only maxima and resource-admitted state.
- Own fixture reachability and elimination of synthetic state masquerading as Contract-produced state. Reconcile final storage/cardinality assumptions with forward/reverse process locators; EXP-0031 owns Waiting-directory/heap branch geometry.
- Output a mechanically usable domain: host parameters, constraints, task-family qualifications, admissible tuples, constructive witnesses and boundary rejection assertions. Do not change authored semantics to manufacture a convenient maximum.

- `Entry condition`: Satisfied for preparation: synthetic joint maxima and host-specific fixture failures already provide falsifiers; complete domain certification is not yet available.
- `Hypothesis`: Contract-derived constraints can describe the reachable state domain precisely enough to generate legal Weight fixtures without multiplying mutually exclusive storage maxima.
- `Materiality`: Provide executable-use constraints and real lifecycle constructors or validated tuple generation, with TryRuntime/property witnesses for minimal/interior/maximal points and explicit infeasible tuples. For each benchmark axis, prove reachability under its host and semantic Task family; storage-only probes cannot certify production transitions.
- `Dependency boundary`: The formula Opening surfaces + funding-tracked assets ≤ authored amount-source capacity is scoped to the proven amount-source derivation and Task/lifecycle family. Funding ingress history, asset aliasing and host mappings require explicit treatment before extending it.

## Measurements

No new measurement. The necessary starting facts and remaining evidence boundary are:

For the inspected Task families, `derive_funding_tracked_assets` derives LastFunding sources while Opening capture derives the mutually exclusive Opening sources; each current Task has at most two amount sources. The resulting qualified union bound is at most 2 × Steps, not an unqualified law for future Tasks or every lifecycle accumulation. DNF representability uses min(MaxPredicatesPerStep, MaxPreconditionClauses × MaxPredicatesPerClause). Fixtures must distinguish mock funding cap 10 from the reference host and full current fragment length from cursor. These constraints are inputs to certify on the exact baseline source, not proof that all storage maxima are jointly reachable.
