## Decision Question

Does adding non-due population at the identity cap change service or charged Actor Control for the same due frontier?

## Governing Specification

- `Owners`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` (placement and scheduler); `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` (component-wise admission); `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` (evidence).
- `Invariant`: Frozen C1, strict FIFO, Q1, cutoff, causal floor and equal thirds remain unchanged.
- `Reopen trigger`: Evidence of semantic drift requires specification review, not optimization acceptance.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Hypothesis`: Non-due identities do not add charged active-frontier work for this bounded witness.
- `Materiality`: Any nonzero charged Control or service-sequence delta falsifies isolation for this comparison.
