## Decision Question

Does the [P8.4-selected](./experiments.md#p84-single-redesign-freeze-0726-phase-2) class-A qualified drain branch owner remove a material per-occurrence proof share while staying conservative for every reachable path — authorizing the frozen F1 re-run — or must the candidate be rejected under the frozen stop condition?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `BACKLOG.md` P9.1 owns the smallest falsifier and P9.4 the one-campaign stop; the [P8.4 Single-Redesign Freeze](./experiments.md#p84-single-redesign-freeze-0726-phase-2) owns the seven declared fields, thresholds and stop condition; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` owns the temporal drain semantics; `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` owns component-wise admission and reservation-before-mutation.
- `Invariant`: exactly one due occurrence recognized and materialized once; strict min-heap deadline order; FIFO; Q1; the causal floor, cutoff, atomicity, rollback, custody, fee and continuation ownership unchanged; the funded close/fault reservation stays funded.
- `Reopen trigger`: a finding that qualifying the drain charge to the production path requires changing semantics, ordering, admission authority or another protected guarantee.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: `wakeup_cursor_drain_branch_weight` (`scheduler.rs:7499`) composes the Tick branch as `scheduler_wakeup_cursor_worker_partial` (or `wakeup_cursor_worker_remove`) plus `max(at_time_trigger_occurrence, cadenced_trigger_occurrence)`. The partial benchmark measures an unanchored genesis bootstrap and the occurrence owner prices the rearm again, so the composed Retain unit (`4_872_038_000 / 16_410`) contains charge-level repetition: six probe reads, the anchor hot store and a second rearm insert (≥ `1_150_000_000` ps RefTime, ≥ 23.6% per unit, P7.7).
- `Reason`: P8.4 selected the qualified branch owner as the single redesign because the recorded RefTime duplication sits in every F1 drain unit; the proof share of the duplicated operations was the decisive unrecorded premise feeding the F1 refusal-conversion model.
- `Hypothesis`: qualifying the composed Tick unit to the production path (branch consume without the repeated probe or the one-time anchor bootstrap, one rearm insert across the seam, Remove priced on the Tick remove path) lowers the per-occurrence proof by δ ≥ 518 while staying conservative for every reachable path.
- `Materiality`: the frozen P8.4 thresholds — δ ≥ 518 resolves the 200-refusal class, δ ≥ 1_862 adds the 99-class, δ ≥ 3_314 (Retain) and δ ≥ 3_417 (Remove) close all 617, and δ ≥ 4_103 is the recorded unit −25% condition for the +1-Step row.

## Result

- W1 (A): the already-anchored steady retained drain charges `8_790` proof (`2_850` measured) against the composed `16_410` — a δ of `+7_620` proof and `+2_151_656_000` ps (run 1) / `+2_104_513_000` ps (run 2).
- W2 (A′): the same path at the bounded 9_999-key cursor charges `47_296` proof (`11_656` measured) against the composed `16_410` — a δ of `−30_886` proof. The deep rearm insert and its latch-disarm price about 13 cursor heap levels each; the composed owner never priced them.
- W3 (B): the Tick remove path at the bounded 10_000-key cursor charges `82_856` proof (`17_516` measured) against the composed `64_308` — a δ of `−18_548` proof; the composed Remove branch priced a Block-clock sift plus a cheapest-geometry occurrence, not the real Tick remove path.
- Fixture boundary: with the cursor at the full `MaxActiveActors` bound the cadence rearm insert cannot be placed; the drain observed `entries_scanned = 0` with `WakeupWorkerFaultState` set instead of a materialized unit. W2 therefore uses one free cursor slot.

## Interpretation

The duplication premise is confirmed and quantified, but the mechanism direction is falsified. At the composed owners' own fixture geometry the qualified retained unit is 8_790 proof against 16_410: the recorded composition double-charges 7_620 proof (≈ 1.9× the largest frozen threshold) and about 2.1×10⁹ ps RefTime per occurrence, so the P7.7 charge-repetition finding transfers to the proof axis at that geometry.

The conservative production price does not transfer. Both legs that price the reachable deep cursor geometry — the deep retained rearm plus its latch-disarm, and the emptied-bucket sift plus the real occurrence on the Tick clock — exceed their recorded composed units: the honest qualified Retain unit is 47_296 (δ −30_886) and the honest Tick Remove unit is 82_856 (δ −18_548). The recorded 16_410 / 64_308 never priced those legs because their benchmarks ran at a 1-key cursor (`partial`, occurrence) or on the Block service clock (`remove`).

Throughput was therefore never available for reclamation. Holding the protected safety envelope at the recorded price would buy admission with under-reservation on every deep-cursor unit; pricing the deep path raises the Retain gate from 99_799 to 130_685 and the Remove gate from 147_697 to 166_245, so the F1 refusals cannot convert. The candidate fails the frozen stop condition on both rejection clauses: at the conservative price the measured δ is below the first threshold, and worst-case coverage cannot be held at the materiality price.

The result also re-ranks the remaining frozen candidates as evidence: the two other recomposition candidates (occurrence-path separation, consume/rearm split) move the same charge without changing the deep-path cost, while the class-C physical candidates (page-slot compaction, deadline-order representation) and the class-D trade candidate are the only ones that could still change it.

## Decision

Rejected. Outcome class: **research result** — a candidate is rejected and a limitation is established; no performance record is claimed. Finite stopping basis: the frozen P8.4 stop condition — the qualified unit's proof share is material only at the recorded owners' fixture geometry; at the bounded cursor depth the conservative qualified Retain unit (47_296) and Tick Remove unit (82_856) exceed the recorded composed units (16_410 / 64_308), so worst-case coverage cannot be held at any materiality price and the F1 refusal conversion is unavailable. The campaign continues inside the frozen finite set; P9.4 consumes this rejection and decides whether the next frozen candidate earns its own smallest falsifier or the set closes as a research result. No production Weight, composition, execution path or artifact identity changes.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: the deterministic proof identities and read/write sets of the three qualified legs on the benchmark bridge Wasm; the proof share of the composed charge's duplication at the composed owners' fixture geometry (7_620 per Retain unit); the conservative qualified prices at the bounded cursor depth (47_296 Retain, 82_856 Remove); and the rejection of the P8.4 mechanism-1 candidate under the frozen stop condition.
- `Does not establish`: any production Weight replacement; any F1 re-run measurement; that the recorded composed owners hold for every reachable state beyond the geometry measured here; the User fee path, AtTime variants or full-cursor exhaustion cost of the qualified owners; the correctness classification of the full-cursor rearm fault.
- `Known limitations`: System cadenced actors only; the deep retained leg needs one free cursor slot; the deep fixtures install only the accessed cursor pages in the established convention; RefTime magnitude is two-run host-conditioned; the remove leg measures the branch only, with the unchanged close/fault added by the production composition.
- `Invalidation triggers`: a changed drain composition, cursor geometry, page size, anchor state or F1 baseline; a regenerated production binding that moves the composed units; a different benchmark toolchain or fixture convention.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: the recorded drain charge contains real composition duplication, but its materiality is an artifact of the recorded benchmarks' shallow cursor geometry; any future drain-pricing correction must choose between honest deep-cursor reservation and the current under-reservation, and no recomposition of the same owners escapes that choice.
