## Decision Question

Does every admission the retained placed pair-owner fallback can select settle within the generated Weight owner of the path it actually executes — the pair at exactly two candidates through the benchmarked pair path, the scalar single at one, and the aggregate batch unchanged for `3..=128` — with no new Weight owner, no new storage or I/O ownership, no omitted or double charge, and actual settlement at or below the reserved owner?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §2 owns component-wise admission, reservation-before-mutation with valid-actual settlement, and the materialization family rotation; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §7.6 owns the one-third Actor Control ceiling; `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` §§1.1, 2.1 owns the two-axis reporting and the sealed regression-baseline rule.
- `Invariant`: Every admitted branch settles within its generated owner; generated owners remain upper bounds for their benchmarked execution paths; no candidate takes additional guaranteed in-block capacity; RefTime and ProofSize remain independent; no charge owner or storage owner is added by the fallback.
- `Reopen trigger`: A regenerated Actors Weight binding, a changed execution-path dispatch for an admitted count, a fallback admission that settles above its owner, or a generated-model domain mismatch.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: The generated pair owner `crossing_placed_pair_unit` is benchmarked by executing `crossing_placed_batch_work_unit(2)`, the two-candidate atomic placed-batch path. The aggregate owner `crossing_placed_maximum_unit` is benchmarked at the `128`-candidate endpoint and is charged for any admitted batch of `3..=128`. The scalar placed owner is benchmarked on the single-candidate path.
- `Reason`: [EXP-0106](./EXP-0106.md) measured the candidate's executed yields but left the bounded resource-containment claim to P4.2. Arithmetic containment alone does not qualify an execution model: the fallback's admitted pair must provably execute the function its owner was generated from, not merely fit inside its numbers.
- `Hypothesis`: The fallback selects the generated pair owner only at exactly two candidates, and the executed pair branch always runs `do_crossing_atomic_placed_batch_unit(2)` — the same function the pair owner benchmarks — so containment holds by execution-path identity in addition to the measured settlement boundaries.
- `Materiality`: A fallback-selected pair that executes a different commit path (non-tail refill or maximum batch), settles above `crossing_placed_pair_unit`, consumes meter or mutates state during fallback selection, or any admission outside the generated owner's benchmarked domain falsifies the claim.

## Result

The fallback pair executes exactly the function that generated its owner: both the benchmark and the production dispatch reach `do_crossing_atomic_placed_batch_unit(2)`, which cannot take the non-tail branch at two candidates and commits through `commit_placed_pair_authority` on the tail page. The fallback selector adds no storage access, meter consumption, or charge. Admitted counts map one-to-one onto the existing generated owners with no new owner and no Weight regeneration, and every reused executed settlement stays at or below its reserved owner with per-block ProofSize identical to the released cohort.

## Interpretation

Containment for the retained candidate is established by execution-path identity, not by arithmetic coincidence: the pair owner is benchmarked from the same function the fallback executes, and the fallback cannot reach the more expensive non-tail refill or maximum-batch commits because `preflight_current_placed_batch_authority` rejects `max_candidates <= 2` into the pair authority before any commit selection. The binding dimension remains ProofSize, which the pair path spends identically to the released scalar and within the Crossing family minimum that already reserved the pair boundary. The RefTime premium is the candidate's only new cost and is amortized over two admitted candidates. No benchmarked branch changed, so the owning benchmark contract requires no regeneration; regression residuals remain classified by the performance-assurance sealed regression-baseline rule rather than by any new per-sample consensus requirement.

## Decision

Accepted. Decision scope: the bounded resource-containment claim only. Every fallback admission settles within the generated owner of its executed path, no Weight or storage owner is added, and no candidate correction is required within P4.2 scope. This record seals no efficiency-improvement or tradeoff outcome; the candidate's selection disposition and outcome class remain with the P4.3 controlled comparison owner.
