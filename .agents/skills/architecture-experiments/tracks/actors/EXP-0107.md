## Decision Question

Does the retained placed pair-owner fallback change only the admitted-branch count for a classified multi-candidate placed batch while preserving ladder ordering (aggregate → pair → single → refusal), scoping to `FireCohortPlacedBatch`, transactional rollback, the `InsufficientFee` scalar retry, latch/ticket/custody semantics, per-candidate fee ownership, and mutation-free refusal on every reachable state?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §2 owns component-wise admission, reservation-before-mutation with valid-actual settlement, and the materialization family rotation; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §7.6 owns the one-third Actor Control ceiling; `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` §§1.1, 2.1 owns the two-axis reporting and the reactive reference profile.
- `Invariant`: One scheduler, strict FIFO, Q1, the causal floor and cutoff, component-wise admission, reservation-before-mutation with valid-actual settlement, cursor-rotation materialization fairness, and the per-block component counters remain unchanged; the candidate takes no additional guaranteed in-block capacity and alters no fee, latch, ticket, custody, or failure semantic.
- `Reopen trigger`: A needed priority, same-block execution, relaxed rollback, additional guaranteed capacity, or any semantic-guarantee change; or a reachable classification state where the fallback ladder violates its ordering or leaves mutation under refusal.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: The released binding reduces a classified multi-candidate placed batch whose aggregate owner misses its grant directly to the scalar single. The retained candidate inserts the generated two-candidate placed owner between the aggregate owner and the scalar single, and executes through the existing atomic placed-batch path.
- `Reason`: [EXP-0105](./EXP-0105.md) admitted the pair-owner fallback first in the finite candidate set; [EXP-0106](./EXP-0106.md) implemented and measured it. P4.2 must decide the bounded correctness claims of the retained candidate before the controlled comparison.
- `Hypothesis`: The candidate's only changed variable is the admitted-branch count and its owner for a classified multi-candidate placed batch; every other transition, charge, latch, ticket, custody, rollback and refusal behavior remains exactly the released path, so ladder ordering and semantic parity hold on every reachable state.
- `Materiality`: A single reachable ladder decision that violates the declared ordering or scoping, a refusal that mutates economic state, a pair admission that leads to a partial commit, or any fee/latch/custody drift falsifies the claim. The claim is decided when every ladder band is witnessed against exact owner identity and the whole Crossing correctness suite stays green on the pinned binding.

## Statistical / Variance Notes

Deterministic FRAME execution and generated Weight: no sampling, distribution, or outlier treatment applies. Each band is one exact-grant observation; the fixture classification and band arithmetic are deterministic, and the reused full-runtime rotation repeats its steady cycle identically.

## Result

All four ladder bands executed as declared on the retained four-candidate placed batch: the aggregate band admitted `4` candidates and recorded the generated maximum owner; the pair band admitted `2` and recorded the generated pair owner (the aggregate owner necessarily missed because the grant is below its reservation); the single band admitted `1` and recorded the scalar placed owner after the pair missed on its generated RefTime premium; and the refusal band admitted `0`, recorded no branch, and left the storage root unchanged while charging only the classification path. No fault was recorded in any band, and the complete Crossing suite stayed green.

## Interpretation

The candidate's changed variable is exactly the admitted-branch count and owner selection for a classified multi-candidate placed batch. Ladder ordering is strict (aggregate, then pair, then scalar single, then bounded refusal), scoping is limited to `FireCohortPlacedBatch`, and every admitted branch continues through the existing transactional execution paths, so rollback, fee charging, latch/ticket outcomes, and custody are reached through unchanged code. The refusal band confirms the terminal fallback is non-mutating: below the scalar reservation the frontier stops after bounded classification work. No semantic boundary of the released cohort moved.

## Decision

Accepted. Decision scope: the bounded ladder-correctness claim only. Every ladder band is witnessed against exact owner identity on the pinned binding, the semantic invariants are preserved, and no candidate correction is required within P4.2 scope. This record seals no efficiency-improvement or tradeoff outcome; the candidate's selection disposition and outcome class remain with the P4.3 controlled comparison owner.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: Ladder ordering, scoping, exact owner identity, mutation-free refusal, and semantic-path continuity for the retained fallback on the pinned generated binding and the W1 fixture topology.
- `Does not establish`: Exact-Wasm replay of the multi-candidate prepass, production-shaped integrated behavior, long-horizon herd behavior, or any performance improvement.
- `Known limitations`: One pallet fixture family with a four-candidate cohort; the full-runtime executed rotation is reused from [EXP-0106](./EXP-0106.md) under the campaign reuse rule; per-stop receipts are deliberately not instrumented.
- `Invalidation triggers`: A regenerated Actors Weight, a changed ladder or plan set, a changed classification authority, a changed component counter set, or a fixture topology that observes a different executed yield.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: The inserted pair level is reachable exactly in the owner-fit miss that previously collapsed to the scalar; the ladder's only new state is the admitted count.
