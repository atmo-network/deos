## Decision Question

What deterministic residence policy maps every Step continuation, delayed retry, idle check, and Cycle completion onto exactly one current obligation without same-block restart, duplicate Cycles, needless one-block wake topology, or probabilistic eligibility?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `BACKLOG.md` N3.2; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §2.3 current-state residence and rounds.
- `Invariant`: One Actor generation has exactly one service obligation; admission or reentry in block B is first eligible in B + 1, and no hint creates a second Cycle while one is open or already owed.
- `Reopen trigger`: A supported continuation or recurrence cannot preserve Q1, retry state, deterministic eligibility, and one-obligation ownership under the selected Live/Sleep threshold.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: The target contract already distinguishes Live, Sleeping, Parked, and Pending and requires B + 1 reentry, while shipped code still uses successor tickets and legacy wake paths.
- `Reason`: N3.3–N3.8 need one return policy before selecting Park certificates, carrier bytes, round traversal, and atomic transfer behavior.
- `Hypothesis`: Keeping every obligation due in the next block resident and moving only later-known deadlines to Sleep is the smallest deterministic policy; completion performs one fresh current-state decision instead of recreating occurrence history.
- `Materiality`: Select logical residence and eligibility only. Exact ring/sleep encoding, deadline buckets, fees, saturation, and lost-wakeup ordering remain downstream.

### Selected Return Matrix

Let `B` be the block committing the return and `E = B + 1` the earliest next ordinary turn.

| Return | Process authority | Residence after commit | First check/attempt | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Successful nonterminal Step | Running at next cursor | Live, same membership | `E` | Retain residence; advance round cursor; no successor allocation |
| Retry due `<= E` | Retry at same cursor/attempt count | Live, same membership | `E` | Q1 already forbids earlier retry; deadline adds no distinct wait |
| Retry due `> E` | Retry at same cursor/attempt count | Sleep at exact due boundary | Due block, then no earlier than its round admission floor | Transfer once; preserve committed prefix and retry state |
| Idle level-sensitive completion still true | Idle | Live, same membership | `E` current activation check | Completion does not admit a second Cycle in B |
| Idle completion false with complete invalidation plan | Idle | Park | On generation-bound invalidation | No Park -> immediate Live churn |
| Idle completion false/unknown with future review | Idle | Sleep at exact review deadline | Due review | Incomplete notification domains cannot park indefinitely |
| Cadenced completion | Idle | Sleep at first canonical future cadence deadline | Due check | Missed periods coalesce; no catch-up |
| AtTime completion | Terminal/inert under authored lifecycle rule | No recurrence obligation | Never | One-shot source is consumed |
| Hint while Running/Retry/Live/Sleep/Pending | Unchanged | Unchanged/coalesced | Existing authority only | No cursor, retry, fee, or next-Cycle reset |
| Due wake or Park invalidation | Unchanged until acknowledged check | Pending or atomically admitted Live | At least source block + 1 for effect | Destination authority is secured before source release |

`Live` means resident service authority, not immediate eligibility. A member retained from B remains present but its per-round/Q1 guards make it ineligible until E. Sleep is selected only when authoritative state proves that no useful check or attempt can occur at E. Park is selected only with the complete certificate owned by N3.3; otherwise a deterministic review Sleep is required.

## Result

Every next-block continuation can remain Live because Q1 and round guards already prevent same-block reuse. A later authoritative deadline carries information absent from Live traversal and therefore justifies one Sleep transfer. Completion is not a synthetic wake: it either retains Live for one fresh level check, Sleeps to a known cadence/review, Parks under a complete negative certificate, or terminates one-shot authority.

## Interpretation

The threshold is semantic rather than tuned: `due <= B + 1` remains Live and `due > B + 1` Sleeps. This avoids a topology round trip for the minimum legal wait without turning Live into probabilistic polling. Residence and eligibility remain separate, busy hints remain non-authoritative, and one atomic destination-before-release transfer preserves the sole obligation.

## Decision

Accepted as the sole N3.2 logical policy: retain next-round continuation/retry/level-check work Live; Sleep only to a known deadline strictly later than B + 1; Park only under a complete certificate; use Pending for an owed current check whose final residence is not yet acknowledged. All admissions/reentries are first eligible at B + 1, and hints while busy never create future Cycle authority.

## Rejected Alternatives and Tradeoffs

| Candidate | Decision | Evidence-backed reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sleep every inter-Step continuation | Rejected | Allocates and transfers topology solely to enforce Q1 already owned by round guards |
| Keep all future deadlines Live | Rejected | Charges repeated ineligible traversal and turns long waits into polling |
| Fixed tunable multi-block hot threshold | Rejected | Adds policy and benchmark dependence without a semantic boundary |
| Park after any false/unknown check | Rejected | Incomplete invalidation can hide future work indefinitely |
| Completion creates a queued successor Cycle | Rejected | Reintroduces occurrence history and duplicate busy authority |
| Hint reserves the next Cycle while busy | Rejected | Resets/coalesces future work outside current-state admission and enables accumulation |

## Validity

- `Establishes`: The complete logical return matrix, exact B + 1 short-wait threshold, long-Sleep rule, and one-obligation/coalescence semantics.
- `Does not establish`: Concrete storage encoding, Park dependency completeness, atomic interleaving implementation, generated Weight, or production throughput.
- `Known limitations`: Target policy remains unimplemented; the semantic oracle models representative retries and recurrence but not the future carrier.
- `Invalidation triggers`: A supported return cannot use the matrix, B + 1 guards fail to prevent duplicate service, or concrete carrier evidence proves one obligation cannot be transferred atomically.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: Eligibility can change while residence remains stable; topology should move only when a later authoritative boundary earns it.
