## Decision Question

What is the smallest complete Park Certificate and wake plan for each supported current-state activation source, given the present predicate/read surfaces and the host mutation routes that can change them?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `BACKLOG.md` §1.5 and N3.3; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §2.3 current-state residence.
- `Invariant`: Park is valid only for an idle current generation under an accepted plan revision whose negative conclusion cannot become applicable without either a revision-bearing exact-key invalidation or a retained deterministic review deadline.
- `Reopen trigger`: A supported read surface can become applicable through a mutation route absent from the certificate's invalidation set and review plan.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: Current Step predicates read spendable asset balance, monotonic block number, or typed observation value/validity/age. Legacy Trigger indexes do not prove complete wake coverage for all current spendability mutations.
- `Reason`: N3.5 and N3.7 need a finite logical certificate before selecting index bytes and lost-wakeup ordering.
- `Hypothesis`: Conservative exact dependency sets are sufficient. Observation and authored temporal domains can use exact feed/deadline authority, while generic balance/spendability activation requires timed review unless the host explicitly certifies every relevant mutation route.
- `Materiality`: Select certificate contents, completeness classification, and wake-plan fallback only. Concrete registry encoding, revision width/exhaustion, transfer ordering, fees, and benchmarked resources remain downstream.

### Certificate Contract

A logical certificate contains or derives all of:

1. Exact Actor slot/identity plus current generation.
2. Accepted Contract/start-read plan revision or collision-resistant plan identity.
3. Typed negative result: false over valid reads, unavailable/stale requiring a bounded retry/review, or a permanently passed monotonic boundary.
4. Conservative exact dependency keys and the authoritative revisions covered by the check.
5. Every deterministic time boundary that can invalidate or must refresh the conclusion.
6. One wake disposition: complete revision-bearing event invalidation, exact Sleep/review deadline, or both.

The certificate is not a second process and need not be one public stored struct. Registrations are derived index authority; the one current generation-bound service obligation remains Park until invalidation is durably represented as Pending. Contract replacement, generation replacement, authorized resume, and direct Manual intent resolve by exact Actor lookup and never reuse an old certificate.

### Dependency and Wake Matrix

| Current activation domain | Negative dependencies | Complete event-driven Park condition | Required fallback / disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Balance/spendability | Exact sovereign account + asset; reducible/spendable balance semantics including holds, locks/freezes, provider/ED, protected fee reserve, issuance/burn and adapter policy that affect Available | Host certifies revision-bearing notification for every mutation that can change the exact spendable result, in either direction | Generic host contract is not presently complete; use one deterministic timed review or reject event-driven balance activation |
| Typed observation comparison | Exact feed revision, current value/state and accepted validity/age rule | Every authoritative publication/state revision reaches bounded feed subscribers; the plan also carries any age/validity boundary required by its semantics | Unknown, corrupt, future-dated, uninitialized, or otherwise invalid data cannot certify indefinite Park; retain typed recovery/review |
| Monotonic block comparison | Current block and threshold | `BlockNumberAbove` false Sleeps to `threshold + 1`; `BlockNumberBelow` false after its strict boundary cannot become true under the admitted monotonic clock | Plan/control mutation remains the direct invalidator; clock-domain change reopens certification |
| Cadenced / AtTime start | Exact canonical next deadline and consumed/recurrence state | Not Park: authoritative future eligibility is already known | Sleep to exact deadline; AtTime terminates recurrence after its one Cycle; cadence coalesces missed periods |
| Manual | Current Actor generation, authority and lifecycle | Authorized direct intent itself creates/coalesces Pending for an idle parked Actor | Unauthorized or busy intent is typed reject/no-op; paused/disabled state uses only its control path |
| Plan/config/generation | Actor generation and accepted plan revision; runtime policy revisions used by reads | Exact control mutation atomically invalidates or replaces old registrations and obligation | Old-generation references are stale and cannot wake replacements; runtime/config surfaces without exact invalidation force timed review or profile rejection |

DNF evaluation may initially register the union of all exact dependencies in the bounded accepted plan. A smaller falsity witness is an optional optimization only if it preserves complete invalidation after every relevant mutation and plan change.

### Wake and Acknowledgment Rule

```text
read watched revisions → evaluate complete current plan
→ install/validate exact registrations and review deadline
→ acknowledge only the revisions covered by that evaluation
→ commit Park if still current

later/during-change revision > acknowledged revision
→ retain one generation-bound Pending obligation
→ current check no earlier than B + 1 for effects
```

N3.7 must realize this as one atomic or monotone protocol. A callback/feed cursor may advance only after stale-generation proof or durable Pending acknowledgment. Revision exhaustion must fail closed into a bounded recovery/review state; it cannot wrap into apparent equality.

## Result

Exact feed and temporal domains can carry complete keyed/deadline authority. Generic balance transfer notification is insufficient for current Available because locks, holds, freezes, providers, protected minima, burns, fees, and adapter policy can alter spendability; without an explicit host-wide mutation contract, balance-negative activation must use timed review. Generation and plan revision are mandatory invalidators for every form.

## Interpretation

Park means certified absence of useful idle work, not unknown state, retry delay, pause, or reclamation. The first implementation may watch the bounded union of all exact dependencies instead of solving DNF falsity. Event and timed forms share one obligation and one eventual Pending boundary, so fallback review does not create a second scheduler.

## Decision

Accepted as the sole N3.3 logical certificate: bind Actor generation, accepted plan revision, typed negative reason, conservative exact dependency keys/revisions, and all relevant time boundaries. Use event-driven Park only for a proved closed mutation domain; otherwise retain one deterministic timed-review Sleep obligation. Observation/feed and authored temporal paths may be exact; generic balance/spendability remains timed-review by default until the host contract certifies every affecting mutation route.

## Rejected Alternatives and Tradeoffs

| Candidate | Decision | Evidence-backed reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asset-transfer notification proves Available | Rejected | Holds, locks/freezes, providers, fee reserve, burns and adapter policy can change spendability independently |
| Coarse BALANCE/OBSERVATION bits | Rejected | Cannot identify the bounded invalidation owner or avoid unrelated fanout |
| Unknown/stale means indefinite Park | Rejected | Absence of a valid read is not negative completeness |
| Enumerate minimal DNF falsity witnesses now | Rejected | Conservative union is bounded and complete; solver complexity has no demonstrated need |
| Park open retries or paused Actors | Rejected | Retry owns an open Cycle; pause owns separate authorization and recovery semantics |
| Separate polling scheduler | Rejected | Timed review is one wake plan in the same Sleep/Pending service design |

## Validity

- `Establishes`: Required certificate identity, finite dependency classes, complete-event admission rule, conservative-union policy, and timed-review fallback.
- `Does not establish`: Storage encoding, revision width, review interval policy, lost-wakeup implementation, generated Weight, or production throughput.
- `Known limitations`: Balance/spendability completeness is deliberately not claimed from current generic adapters; target parking is not implemented.
- `Invalidation triggers`: New predicate/source, a changed host notification guarantee, non-monotonic time, or concrete bounded-state failure.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: A compact negative certificate is primarily an invalidation contract, not a cached predicate result.

## Follow-Up / Next Gradient
