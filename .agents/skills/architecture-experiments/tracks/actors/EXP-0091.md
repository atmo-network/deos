## Decision Question

Can eligibility-aware sustained observations support the required service fairness claim beyond bounded censoring?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` §1.2 owns service metrics; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §2.1 owns invariants. BACKLOG owns open G6 gates.
- `Invariant`: Class-neutral FIFO, immutable cutoff, Q1 and causal floor remain unchanged.
- `Reopen trigger`: A different fairness promise belongs to specification, not interpretation of censored data.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Reason`: Bounded outcomes and censored waits do not prove the required sustained service scope.
- `Hypothesis`: Correctly classified eligibility and observation windows can decide service sufficiency without treating inter-cycle waits as Running bounds.
- `Materiality`: Unsupported fairness scope prevents the parent service decision.

## Baseline and Candidates

The required fairness scope is the 10,000-User ObservationCrossing target over 100 eligible blocks. Accepted bounded candidates are EXP-0077's one-cycle 100-due-Actor frontier, EXP-0084's sustained System schedules and EXP-0087's exact current User/reactive reachability constraint.

## Measurements

EXP-0077 services one bounded cohort of 100 due Actors over `[14,17,17,17,17,17,1]` and proves 9,885 non-due identities add zero charged-Control delta. It does not reactivate those Actors, so its final observations are right-censored and establish no sustained `ServiceGapBlocks` distribution.

EXP-0084 observes 100 blocks across full 10,000-identity System Manual, Cadenced, Mixed and Mixed-with-user populations. Those schedules establish sustained System accounting only; they do not instantiate the required prefunded User ObservationCrossing population or its trigger-to-service intervals.

EXP-0087 independently proves the required fairness population is nonconstructible at the 9,000 User/feed cap and that Crossing detection can materialize at most 400 of 10,000 candidates within the 100-block window. Therefore no current observation window can support the governing fairness claim.

## Derived Metrics

No finite required-population `ServiceGapBlocks` bound is accepted. The 2,250-block minimum detector horizon for the reachable 9,000-User population is a materialization lower bound, not a scheduler service-gap estimate.

## Statistical / Variance Notes

EXP-0077's terminal one-cycle tail is explicitly right-censored for sustained fairness. System schedule distributions are population-mismatched. No p95/p99/maximum or unobserved-tail bound is inferred for the required User cohort.

## Result

Current evidence is insufficient for the required sustained User/reactive fairness scope, and the governing population/window is independently unreachable.

## Interpretation

The correct result is an explicit unsupported fairness claim, not a smaller inferred bound. Bounded FIFO progress and sustained System service remain valid within their own evidence scopes.

## Decision

Accepted negative result. Do not claim required-population sustained fairness for current production; carry the constraint into EXP-0066/G8.

## Rejected Alternatives and Tradeoffs

- `Final remainder as bound`: Rejected because EXP-0077's one-cycle tail is censored.
- `System schedule proxy`: Rejected because Actor type, trigger and funding population differ.
- `Detector horizon as service gap`: Rejected because materialization precedes scheduler eligibility.

## Validity

Establishes evidence insufficiency for the governing scope. Does not invalidate bounded FIFO/Q1 results or sustained System ledgers. Target, population, detector capacity or new eligibility-aware User evidence requires review.

## Follow-Up / Next Gradient

Compose EXP-0066 with the explicit target and fairness constraints; do not invent a replacement target.

## Architecture Impact

None; no service-priority policy is introduced.
