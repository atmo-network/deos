## Decision Question

Does the temporal ledger identify the actual component-wise charged selector separately from the complete pre-mutation admission envelope?

## Governing Specification

- `Owners`: `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` (component-wise admission and settlement); `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` (temporal readiness, fees, lifecycle and cutoff).
- `Invariant`: Complete admission before mutation; useful readiness, failure-preserving future readiness and fee ownership remain unchanged.
- `Reopen trigger`: Any proposed semantic or safety relaxation requires specification reopening, not coefficient adjustment.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Hypothesis`: Correct selectors distinguish the charged temporal maximum from reserved close/fault contingencies and expose bounded current-topology pressure without implying removability.
- `Materiality`: Exact equality with production selectors; even a one-byte diagnostic mismatch fails the ledger.
- `Scope`: The semantic-classification extraction is source-derived cross-owner context, not additional research or acceptance claims owned by this Leaf.

| Cost class | Evidence / implementation owner | Supported boundary |

| Transient rearm | `process_due_temporal_occurrence_loaded` schedules the next Cadenced deadline before fee/activation handling; successful latching removes temporal detection | A concrete transient-work hypothesis, not proven removable overhead; underfunding/collection failure retains future readiness and must not lose it |
| Repeated activation preparation | The temporal owner preflights terminal outcomes before charging, then `request_activation_inner` reloads and preflights before commit | Repeated preparation is visible in source, but intervening state/fee changes preclude assuming the first plan remains reusable; no isolated cost saving is established |
| Shared/root proof | Reproduced storage-prefix partition and independent committed-root witness | Complete raw-node coverage, retaining cross-owner sharing; neither phase-exclusive cost nor invocation counts |

The Cadenced rearm hypothesis is constrained by `tests/scheduling.rs::cadenced_latch_disables_detection_until_pipeline_opening`, `underfunded_cadenced_occurrence_advances_without_fee_readiness_or_apoptosis` and `cadenced_collection_failure_advances_deadline_without_readiness`. The generated `7 / 7` Cadenced-versus-AtTime difference does not isolate the cost of transient publication/deletion or repeated preflight. A future G8 comparison must preserve these branches, source/destination authority and complete admission; this inspection does not allocate a candidate or waive G7.

The temporal selector ledger covers generated per-unit I/O and admission, not executed selector frequencies or database invocation counts for every W0–W9 block. Dividing root/key pairs by Steps would not fill that gap. Likewise, a fixture-wide next-attempt maximum gives a conservative headroom bound, not automatically the exact live-head deficit. These distinctions keep full resource-ledger closure open despite complete raw proof coverage.

## Result

Charged retained/removed ProofSize is 16,410/64,308; admission is 99,799/147,697. Cadenced owns RefTime and associated generated I/O, AtTime owns maximum temporal ProofSize. The one-byte discrepancies were diagnostic, not runtime undercharging.

## Interpretation

Complete admission conservatively includes contingencies absent from successful charge. Current-topology costs and inspected repeated preparation are not universal minima or isolated removable costs. Shared/root proof cannot supply invocation counts or phase-local stop deficits.

## Decision

Accepted for the bounded current temporal-selector accounting claim. The generated-owner regression exactly distinguishes charged branch Weight from complete admission contingencies on EXP-0095. It does not convert generated I/O into physical operations or establish removable cost.

## Rejected Alternatives and Tradeoffs

- `Rejected diagnostic`: Cadenced-only sums understate charged/admitted ProofSize by one byte where the component-wise AtTime maximum applies.
- `Rejected inference`: No runtime undercharge, raw-proof discount, arbitrary per-Step shared allocation or automatic contingency removal.
- `Unproven alternatives`: Transient rearm removal and reusable preflight need independent reachable-path evidence preserving underfunding, collection failure, authority and admission; G7/G8 remain governing gates.
