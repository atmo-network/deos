- `Smallest falsifier`: Replay one legal maximum independent-source Opening and adjacent parameter points against selected maximum/actual envelopes, independently exhausting RefTime and ProofSize. Then cross solvent/insolvent User Suspended retry with scarce effect capacity; a wrong terminal outcome or avoidable cold payload read falsifies the corresponding admission claim.
- `Evidence class`: Call-path ownership audit, legal native witnesses and production-Wasm FRAME branch evidence.

| Admission family | Required partitions | Observable |

| Suspended | Temporary/Funding retry, cursor/error-policy/attempt boundaries | Action liability and terminal precedence before resource refusal |

Publish the exact 12/24/48 product contract and sound rejection behavior, including any structurally legal but resource-inadmissible subset. Bind every numeric statement to source/Weight/config and production-Wasm evidence.
- `Rejection criteria`: Underweight, discontinuous cheap maximum, omitted liability/native read, changed terminal precedence, reserved Action allowance masking System base undercount, skipped fitting FIFO head or budget increase concealed as optimization.
