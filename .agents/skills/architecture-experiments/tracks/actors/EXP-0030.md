### Receipt Inclusive Boundary Audit

The preparation gap was a missing demonstrated owner, not a proven numeric underbound: incidental margin in old coefficients proves neither attribution nor a bound. The staged candidate measures the exact receipt operation independently and exposes Invoked versus NotInvoked to the Control selector, preserving nonzero fee collection as a separate fact. Reject folding System receipts into a User collection allowance or charging the receipt to Task effects. User holds and trigger-specific rearm remain distinct unresolved rows.

The source audit also finds that `scheduler_inner_opening_complete_max` uses true Opening predicates and StopCycle through the general path, so its measured atom already includes the receipt. The V4 blanket additive allowance therefore overlapped that direct owner. V5 excludes CompleteMax from receipt-free maxima, adds receipt to the remaining envelope, then takes the component-wise maximum with inclusive CompleteMax. Its actual selector marks CompleteMax and conservative maximum fallback as inclusive; all other audited direct and composed paths keep receipt separate. Passing accounting tests alone does not prove disjoint physical work. Running Complete with predicates instead expects NotInvoked, while its unpredicated specialization omits the receipt. Do not subtract an independently measured constant from an old aggregate coefficient to fabricate a disjoint bound.

## Interpretation

The evidence distinguishes three owners without subtraction: the invocation receipt is Control work, nonzero User collection is separate Control work owned by EXP-0062, and Task effects remain effect work. General-path System invocation selects the same receipt owner despite fee exemption; direct CompleteMax already includes it and must not add it twice. This decides ownership and the scoped generated primitive, not the final complete production Weight identity.

## Decision

Accepted for the admitted reference domain. Select the isolated generated receipt owner for receipt-free general paths, retain the inclusive CompleteMax owner without a second addition, and charge no receipt for `NotInvoked`. Exact full-file regeneration and final benchmark/production Wasm binding remain EXP-0065 rather than an open obligation of this Leaf.
