## Decision Question

Does a matched active native-conversion recipient witness falsify the retained two-leg `task_split_transfer(2)` coefficient, and can it certify the current production Fee Sink effect?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `docs/actors.integration.en.md` Fee Sink allocation and certified ingress; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §6.1.
- `Invariant`: Every invoked SplitTransfer leg's host conversion, certified ingress and balance-dependency publication belong to its one Task-effect reservation; production TrustedSet Fee Sink uses two Native legs.
- `Reopen trigger`: A matched reachable native-conversion profile exceeds the retained coefficient in either dimension, or a complete selector proves it dominates the active bridge and all other admitted recipient states.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: `RuntimeNativeSecurityModeProvider` selects TrustedSet in production and LP-backed selection under `runtime-benchmarks`. `TmctolAssetOps::bridge_native_staking_ingress()` itself has no security-mode branch. The production Fee Sink integration test demonstrates its active bridge.
- `Reason`: Existing `task_transfer`, `task_mint` and ordinary `task_split_transfer` benchmarks do not exercise the slash/local-asset mint plus active liquidity Actor ingress. Source route B has no complete independent maximum for this composite.
- `Hypothesis`: The retained two-leg generated coefficient is falsified by one selected full active-conversion effect profile.
- `Materiality`: Exceedance of either independent Weight dimension rejects sufficiency for that profile; non-exceedance does not accept a universal upper or a production-mode ABI/Weight binding.

## Result

C successfully transfers two Native legs, increases liquidity Actor's local asset-0 balance and latches its canonical pending signal after actual certified ingress. Its selected RefTime including DB and estimated ProofSize are below the stale two-leg projection and the matched different-topology N result.

## Interpretation

The full active bridge is measurable on benchmark Wasm because its adapter branch has no security-mode condition. C's measured source is not the Fee Sink and its first recipient is the staking pool, so the benchmark-only LP-backed reward branch is not invoked: C probes the same pool/bridge Task branch as the TrustedSet two-leg plan, not the complete production Step. The normal-mode Fee Sink integration test proves both successful reachability and a separate late mint-failure rollback when the staking asset is frozen. C does not measure that failure; its selected observation neither makes the retained coefficient sound nor makes a new coefficient necessary on its own.

## Decision

Status Inconclusive: one active native-conversion witness does not falsify the retained two-leg projection; the comparator's different recipients and the unbounded aggregate selector forbid acceptance. Outcome class: research result. Stop this isolated diagnostic; do not add a native recipient matrix or rebind production Weight from it.
