## Decision Question

Can a due Cadenced occurrence disable its detector, latch once and defer through the canonical process wakeup when Ready publication is capacity-blocked, without creating an authority state that its own loader rejects?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` sections 7.3 and 8.6.
- `Invariant`: One useful Trigger latch disables family detection while pending; capacity backpressure preserves one canonical process placement and the containing transaction rolls back every partial transition on failure.
- `Reopen trigger`: Evidence that detector invalidation before activation loses a due occurrence, duplicates authority, changes fee finality or cannot roll back to the original Trigger wakeup.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: Due Cadenced processing scheduled the next detector wakeup, requested activation, then removed the detector after successful latching.
- `Reason`: The required maximum mixed breaker profile recorded a sticky wakeup invariant fault and retained overdue Tick(11) without latching.
- `Hypothesis`: Removing the next detector while source authority is still loadable, immediately before activation inside the existing outer transaction, prevents transient dual Trigger/process wakeup authority and retains rollback safety.
- `Materiality`: The maximum mixed profile must latch all three trigger families without execution or loss under the breaker.

## Statistical / Variance Notes

The outcome is deterministic. Wall-clock duration varied with compilation and is not a decision metric. The second passing run removed all temporary diagnostic output, preventing instrumentation from serving as hidden support.

## Result

Baseline diagnostic output proved that Ready capacity, not active-identity capacity, caused activation to defer while the next Cadenced detector remained live. The resulting Trigger plus process-wakeup intermediate could not be loaded for post-activation detector removal and the whole occurrence rolled back into a sticky worker fault. Candidate A passed the exact maximum profile twice.

## Interpretation

The retained Tick(11) pointer was not lawful retry evidence because `WakeupWorkerFault::Invariant` disabled further worker progress. Moving detector removal before activation avoids an unloadable intermediate owner. Because both actions remain inside the wakeup-drain storage transaction, any later fee or placement failure restores the original due detector and state.

## Decision

Accepted. Select Candidate A. Keep queue capacity, cadence arithmetic, fees, public context and C1 unchanged; regenerate the production artifact under EXP-0095.
