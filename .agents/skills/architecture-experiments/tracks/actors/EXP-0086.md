## Decision Question

Does the bounded W5 lifecycle ledger reconstruct complete charged Control/effect owners and generated I/O without double-counting provisional entry, cleanup or continuation work?

## Governing Specification

- `Owners`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` (lifecycle, custody, Pipeline/Action fees and committed prefixes); `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` (component-wise accounting); `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` (evidence classification).
- `Invariant`: Frozen C1 FIFO/Q1, causal floor, committed-Step durability, atomic Task effects, complete-owner settlement and custody-neutral close.
- `Reopen trigger`: Evidenced semantic contradiction, not a generated-I/O or cost difference.

- `Current architecture`: Production `StepControlWeight` selects lifecycle owners from bounded pre-state geometry and phase.
- `Hypothesis`: Their frequencies reconstruct the native author's full and Prepass Control and independent Task effects on the header-matched W5 states.
- `Materiality`: Any omitted or double-counted term falsifies the accounting claim; no optimization threshold or extra lifecycle profile is introduced.

- `Failure means uninvoked`: Rejected by both failed exact-output attempts' effect accounting.
