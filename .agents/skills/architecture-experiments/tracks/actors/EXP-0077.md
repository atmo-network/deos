## Decision Question

Does bounded valid User demand preserve the same due Actor service and accounting under separate resource turns?

## Governing Specification

- `Owners`: `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` (equal-thirds, component-wise admission); `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` (FIFO/Q1); `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` (demand evidence).
- `Invariant`: Valid signed demand, full Executive/finalization, unchanged C1 and causal floor.
- `Reopen trigger`: Resource-policy or semantic drift requires specification review.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Hypothesis`: User ProofSize saturation and the measured RefTime-heavy fallback do not change the bounded Actor service prefix.
- `Materiality`: Any service or Actor-accounting difference in the matched state falsifies this witness.

This closes W9's observer-order refresh, not a standalone RefTime-saturation or complete callable-surface impossibility claim.

An initial native W9 probe reused a one-unit Transfer into an unfunded recipient and failed `Token(BelowMinimum)`. It was discarded; the retained W9 fixture transfers one existential deposit. No W9 evidence from the invalid probe is retained.

## Interpretation

## Decision
