## Decision Question

Can the bounded Manual cohort's ordinary Control stops be reconstructed from actual eligible live heads and phase-local budgets rather than finalized headroom alone?

## Governing Specification

- `Owners`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` (placement and scheduler); `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` (component-wise admission/actual settlement); `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` (evidence).
- `Invariant`: Preserve FIFO, cutoff, Q1, causal floor and complete admission.
- `Reopen trigger`: Evidence of a semantic conflict, not a cost miss.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: Frozen C1 uses one live FIFO head and separate Prepass/idle meters.
- `Reason`: Finalized headroom alone cannot identify a historical stop.
- `Hypothesis`: Actual eligible Manual heads and source-bound phase budgets reconstruct the ordinary Control rejection in the retained nine-block cohort.
- `Materiality`: Distinguish capacity failure from service ineligibility without extrapolation.
