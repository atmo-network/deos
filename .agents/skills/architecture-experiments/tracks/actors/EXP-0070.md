### W1/W2 Service Waits and Right-Censored Horizons

`OneStepServiceTrace` records each workload Actor's known materialized signal and first/last Step block. Initial Manual signals come from the shared fixture's successful block-1 setup; subsequent signals come from workload-filtered `TriggerOccurrenceProcessed` events. Every observed Step consumes known readiness after the next-block causal floor. Successful one-Step completion and absence of lifecycle interruptions constrain this model; it is not a generic Running/retry eligibility model. Terminal pending latches are checked against all 9,985 workload hot records outside measured execution. No consensus state, Weight or production artifact changes.

The observer distinguishes creation-to-first-Step latency, materialized-signal-to-Step latency, unfinished signal waits and raw inter-Step gaps. A Cadenced signal timestamp is its actual materialization block, not its nominal due tick. Completed-sample percentiles exclude censored Actors and cannot describe the whole population. A pending signal first observed in the final block has zero subsequent eligible blocks, not a violated service opportunity.
