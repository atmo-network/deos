## Decision Question

Does the converged candidate tree bind one final production cohort — Actors Weight, production Wasm, metadata, descriptors and client evidence — with every affected consumer agreeing, and is a fresh canonical Weight regeneration on the current host admissible as the replacement production binding?

## Governing Specification

- `Owners`: `BACKLOG.md` P5.2 exit; `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` component-wise admission and reservation-before-mutation; `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` binding and assurance contracts; pallet specification and production-Weight/runtime validation ownership.
- `Invariant`: One source/configuration cohort; generated bindings derive from that source; no stale or placeholder production Weight; the pinned released comparison identity is not silently replaced by unrelated coefficient drift.
- `Reopen trigger`: Any retained-code change, a new generated Weight owner or method, a consumer-identity mismatch, or a regression on the recorded cohort identities.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: [EXP-0111](./EXP-0111.md) converged the accepted pair fallback as the only production admission path and classified the Production Weight file as "unchanged by the campaign; the pair owner was already generated — P5.2 verifies or regenerates after the final cohort".
- `Reason`: P5.2 owns the binding decision and the production artifact cohort; the retained candiate adds no `#[pallet::storage]` item, no bound and no Weight trait or file entry.
- `Hypothesis`: The pinned `9ce37ade…` binding already covers the retained ladder because the pair owner is present and unchanged, while a fresh full-pallet generation on the current host will be dominated by host measurement conditions rather than by the candidate; both outcomes are decidable by comparing generations and deterministic consumer identities.

## Statistical / Variance Notes

The three generations of the same pinned command produced three different coefficient sets while raw measured proof bytes stayed identical for all 160 methods and the raw minimum execution time of `scheduler_paged_mixed_scan` stayed identical (`34_851_000` ps) across generations. The variance is therefore in the bencher's wall-clock fits, not in the recorded storage work. Host state observed at analysis: load average `5.58 / 6.53 / 5.74`, `schedutil` governor, 4,291 MHz, with multiple competing sessions (three `pi` processes, Chrome, Steam, Telegram, Zed) active. A single-extrinsic probe with `--json-file` did not complete within 900 s and published no artifact. Cause attribution to host load is a hypothesis: the evidenced claim is environment-dominated instability — universal unrelated coefficient movement over unchanged raw work — while which host factor dominated remains causal inference, not established fact. Inadmissibility of candidate B is established by the result distribution itself: universal unrelated coefficient movement plus a 74× fitted intercept over an unchanged raw minimum cannot be evidence about a one-branch candidate.

## Result

The pinned Actors Weight `9ce37ade…` remains the production binding. It is complete, current for the retained ladder and carries the pair owner the fallback consumes; the campaign adds no generated method or owner. Two full fresh generations under the pinned command produced environment-dominated coefficient sets — 159 of 160 unrelated methods moved, including implausible fits — and were rejected rather than adopted. The production Wasm was rebuilt on the candidate tree (`c0f5ea18…`), metadata re-exported byte-identical (`75868ea7…`), ABI, bounds, cost, fee and ingress artifacts verified unchanged, observation evidence refreshed for the new Wasm identity only, and the full client suite is green. The generated method set is complete, but ownership completeness is qualified separately: [EXP-0113](./EXP-0113.md) finds no benchmarked owner for the admission-selection control flow, so the binding remains qualified until BACKLOG P5.4 closes that gap.

## Interpretation

A binding refresh is admissible only when its deltas can be attributed to the retained change. Here they cannot: raw storage work is unchanged, the pair owner already exists, and the observed coefficient movement is universal and unstable across identical commands. Adopting candidate B would silently rewrite resource-policy coefficients for 159 methods untouched by the candidate, invalidate the pinned comparison matrix and the EXP-0103/EXP-0104 reservation algebra, and change runtime admission behavior without a measured decision. Retaining the pinned binding keeps the released comparison identity intact while binding the candidate code through the rebuilt production Wasm and the refreshed observation evidence. A future production Weight reassessment may be opened when new evidence questions the retained owner's soundness or when a changed benchmarked path requires a new owner; it uses matched or repeated same-host evidence with explicit noise qualification, and no dedicated quiet host is required. One ownership-bound reopening is already recorded: the admission-selection control flow lacks a benchmarked owner ([EXP-0113](./EXP-0113.md)), so the binding is qualified until BACKLOG P5.4 closes that gap.

## Decision

Accepted. The retained candidate tree binds cohort A: pinned Actors Weight `9ce37ade…`, production Wasm `c0f5ea18…`, metadata `75868ea7…`, unchanged consumer artifacts, observation evidence `e26f3849…` and a green client suite. Finite stopping basis: the exit is decided by method-set completeness, raw-proof identity, the admissibility analysis of the regeneration attempts and the consumer checks; further generation attempts on the same host add variance, not decision information.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: The retained binding is complete and applicable to the retained ladder; the candidate-tree production Wasm and refreshed observation evidence are bound; metadata and all other affected consumers are unchanged; fresh full-pallet generation under the pinned command is inadmissible on the current host.
- `Does not establish`: The final release-tree gate (P6.3), the final production-cohort performance validation (P5.3), a replacement-generation result under any host, or any change to the campaign's measured service claims.
- `Known limitations`: The host cause of the generation variance is inferred, not instrumented; run C's artifact was superseded and only excerpt values were retained; the generation logs were not retained.
- `Invalidation triggers`: A retained-source or benchmark-owner change, a consumer-identity mismatch, or a qualified same-host reassessment that materially differs from the pinned binding.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: Production Weight generation on a shared interactive host is not reproducible at the coefficient level; admissibility must be decided from raw-work identity and attribution rather than from a fresh file's mere existence.
