## Decision Question

Do EXP-0120, EXP-0125 and EXP-0135 already close every necessary 0.7.27 physical choice adequately, and what concrete implementation falsifier alone may admit one alternate or bounded refinement for each selected owner?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `BACKLOG.md` N6.2; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §2.3; EXP-0120, EXP-0125 and EXP-0135.
- `Invariant`: One current Actor generation has one canonical process and exactly one residence or terminal cleanup authority; every operation is bounded, generation-safe and resource-owned without changing current-state, Q1, ordering, custody or effect semantics.
- `Reopen trigger`: Concrete implementation or executable-model evidence satisfies a ledger falsifier and the selected mechanism cannot be corrected inside its stated finite refinement allowance.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: EXP-0120 selects stable canonical process placement, EXP-0125 selects the concrete Live/Pending and deadline carriers, and EXP-0135 composes all physical owners. No current-lineage runtime exists yet, so no implementation falsifier or empirical cost evidence exists.
- `Reason`: N6.2 requires a finite alternative gate before implementation pressure can turn routine refinement into an unbounded prototype campaign. Adequate selected mechanisms should stand unless evidence exposes a missing operation, bound or authority.
- `Hypothesis`: The selected stable process, actor-keyed intrusive ring, exact revisioned Park registrations, retained C32 deadline pages/paged heaps and sealed exact-handle reclamation close all necessary choices. Each admits only local representation refinement unless its named structural falsifier is observed.
- `Materiality`: Close the candidate set at one implementation. A second candidate is admitted only by one concrete ledger falsifier; cost alone transfers to N6.5/N6.3 and cannot reopen architecture. At most one alternate per falsified row and one implementation correction of the selected row may be evaluated before disposition.

### 2026-09-15 Reassessment Amendment

The historical decision and its five-owner baseline remain valid: stable process placement, the actor-keyed intrusive Live/Pending ring, exact revisioned Park registrations, retained C32 deadline pages/paged heaps, and sealed exact-handle reclamation are the sole current implementation baseline. Structural sufficiency, demonstrated release performance, and permanent carrier immunity are distinct claims.

Two independent routes now govern bounded reassessment:

| Route | Admission evidence | Required response | Finite boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S — structural/soundness | A legal supported path violates boundedness, singular authority, wake completeness, saturation/rollback, generation isolation, or complete resource ownership | Correct the selected mechanism or replace it; release cannot retain the defect | Safety correction is mandatory and is never waived by an exhausted research allowance |
| P — performance | A qualified implemented vertical slice misses a criterion declared before decision measurement or violates a protected regression; its complete operation ledger includes wake, negative checks, movement and maintenance; one physical owner is causally material; one concrete same-semantics alternative and smallest falsifier are fixed | Open one focused comparison against the exact selected baseline without weakening semantics or resource policy | At most one performance-driven alternative campaign for the causally selected bottleneck, with at most one bounded refinement fixed before measurement |

A large number, provisional Weight, stale coefficient, diagnostic-only path, or missing end-to-end work cannot open P. A representative early V1/V2 vertical slice may open it before final N7 evidence, but its result remains scoped and cannot become a release claim without the final matched workload and production binding. If the baseline meets its declared criteria, stop without seeking a record. If the bounded alternative fails, record the result and return the unresolved release decision to the task owner; do not move automatically to another bottleneck.

This amendment changes current evidence authority, not the 2026-09-14 historical decision. No alternative is presently admitted because no qualified P evidence or S falsifier has been recorded.

## Result

All necessary N6.2 choices already have one selected bounded mechanism in EXP-0120/EXP-0125/EXP-0135. The audit found no unowned residence, predecessor/population/range scan requirement, uncovered supported wake/deadline class or cleanup discovery requirement in the selected design. Zero alternate implementations are admitted before realization.

## Interpretation

N6.2 is a sufficiency gate, not a demand for comparative prototypes. Local field, page and phase refinements may correct the selected implementation without changing its ownership model. A structural falsifier first receives one correction attempt; only a surviving falsifier earns one alternate in a separate Leaf. Performance measurements may reject release materiality or establish a tradeoff, but cost alone competes against the frozen baseline through N6.5/N6.3 rather than retroactively making the design structurally insufficient.

## Decision

Accept Candidate A as the sole implementation baseline and close the N6.2 design-selection exit. Outcome class: research result. Finite stopping basis: five necessary choices are selected, each has one smallest falsifier and bounded refinement allowance, no falsifier is currently observed, and no second prototype can add decision evidence before implementation exists.
