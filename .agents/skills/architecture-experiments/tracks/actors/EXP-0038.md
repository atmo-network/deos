## Interpretation

Trigger family and terminal disposition are required selector dimensions for temporal zero-Step Opening. Cadenced rearm, AtTime one-shot consumption and authored cleanup are mutually exclusive complete owners. Their inclusive accounting prevents a second Trigger charge and prevents duplicate collection, hold, rearm or cleanup additions. ## Decision

Accepted for the reference temporal binding. Select exactly one complete owner from the family × terminal matrix: AtTime persistence `121,875,000 / 7,624` plus 9 reads / 5 writes; AtTime authored close `214,206,000 / 11,144` plus 16 reads / 15 writes; Cadenced persistence `238,931,000 / 10,744` plus 21 reads / 16 writes; or Cadenced authored close `314,360,000 / 11,476` plus 26 reads / 23 writes.
