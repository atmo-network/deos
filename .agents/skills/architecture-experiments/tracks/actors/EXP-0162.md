## Decision Question

Which finite workload matrix, horizons, censoring rules and materiality boundary must every current-state Actors measurement use, fixed before any result?

## Context and Candidates

- `Current architecture`: [EXP-0161](./EXP-0161.md) physical baseline.
- `Hypothesis`: Twelve bounded workloads cover every required goal, moved cost and unfinished outcome, so candidate acceptance cannot pick workloads or thresholds after results.
- `Materiality`: A primary useful result requires one additional complete W1 workflow under the same budget, or removal of at least one persistent scheduler key mutation per successful interior Step with lower total scheduler write bytes and no regression across the named dimensions. A local queue coefficient never establishes it.
- `Controlled / changed variables`: Semantics, component budgets and generated owners are fixed per artifact; only the engine under test changes. Equal-semantics comparisons and historical bridges are reported separately.

## Workloads and Criteria

- `Evidence class`: Native stress and exact-Wasm full-runtime block profiles through `scripts/actors-assurance.sh`.
- `Acceptance criteria`: Every workload preserves its semantic/economic postcondition, Q1 and order contract, custody and generation rules with zero unexplained loss or duplicate effect. W1 meets the materiality rule. W4–W10 show bounded maintenance with no growing unserved debt over the declared drain horizon. W5 claims only population independence its counters establish. Bridge results list semantic and order differences beside outcomes.
- `Rejection criteria`: Any safety failure; moved cost that erases W1 materiality; worse completed workflows, p95 delay, ProofSize, retained state or debt without an approved tradeoff; dropped work violating the contract; identity mismatch; RefTime inside noise presented as a win; a result from an undeclared workload.

| Workload | Geometry | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| W1 — long Live economic flow | 32 Actors (8 Fee Sink splitters, 8 Burn, 8 liquidity, 8 Users), 8 Steps each over 64 blocks, every Step `Percent` of current Available | Useful work, current amount, multi-Step residency, heterogeneous goals |

## Measurements

Historical subset realizations on pre-cut sources established: one funded User Transfer with conserved Trigger/Pipeline/Action fees; ordered System Cycles across 0/2/4-predicate and 1/2/3-Step cells; committed-prefix retry and continuation; three terminal closure classes; Manual saturation serving 6,700 of 9,985 Actors with 3,285 honestly censored; equal charged due-service with 9,885 additional non-due identities; and W7 with 1,024 Sleepers, one ordered return per block, 63 exact B+1 retries and overdue debt decreasing by one per drain block (`992 → 960`) with recovery censored.

- `Benchmark Evidence Status`: Historical — subset observations bind pre-cut sources, the interim Weight and superseded Prepass orchestration.
- `Reassessment Trigger`: Coordinated production binding under `Actors/Final Weight And Measurements`.
- `Compared Observation IDs`: None current.
- `Noise / Stability Evidence`: None current.

## Result

The matrix binds representative System and User goals, multi-Step current inputs, retry, recurrence, long residency, Park churn, sparse populations, revision storms, Sleep/reentry, mutation, heavy effects, contention and close, with populations from 32 to 10,000 identities and explicit horizons.

## Interpretation

Useful benefit must be shown by complete workflows or eliminated persistent mutations, not local coefficients. Historical W6 parked Actors through a Manual observation-only Opening and historical W8 exercised Crossing replacement; neither mechanism exists, so those rows are redefined above and must be re-witnessed.

## Decision

Accepted as the workload and materiality freeze. Outcome class: research result. Finite stopping basis: every required family has one bounded witness and acceptance can no longer choose workloads or thresholds after results.

### Pull-Model Amendment (2026-09-26)

W3's coalesced-change cohort uses AddressEvent coalescing instead of ObservationChange; W4, W5 and W6 use parked-balance episodes and revisions instead of observation-gated Park and shared-feed fanout; W10 measures synchronous bounded close instead of asynchronous cleanup debt. Populations, horizons and criteria are unchanged.
