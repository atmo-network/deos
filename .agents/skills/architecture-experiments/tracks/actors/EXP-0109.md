## Decision Question

Are the mandatory bounded correctness and resource claims of the retained placed pair-owner fallback candidate decided — with no candidate correction required within P4.2 scope — so the candidate advances unchanged to the controlled P4.3 comparison, or must an invalid candidate be corrected or rejected before comparison?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `BACKLOG.md` P4.2 owns the phase exit (mandatory candidate claims decided; invalid candidates corrected within scope or rejected); `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §2 owns component-wise admission and reservation-before-mutation with valid-actual settlement; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §7.6 owns the one-third Actor Control ceiling; `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` §§1.1, 2.1 owns the two-axis reporting and the sealed regression-baseline rule.
- `Invariant`: The candidate's semantic contract, resource policy, host/artifact identity and measurement method are unchanged; this record composes child decisions only, introduces no candidate, no new measurement, and no semantic change.
- `Reopen trigger`: A child decision is invalidated, a new independently decidable candidate claim is discovered, or the P4.3 comparison cannot proceed on the composed identity.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: The retained pair fallback is implemented at `179eee31…` and its matched measurement is owned by [EXP-0106](./EXP-0106.md); the bounded correctness claim is owned by [EXP-0107](./EXP-0107.md) and the resource-containment claim by [EXP-0108](./EXP-0108.md).
- `Reason`: P4.2 requires each independently decidable claim to have its Leaf and the required results to be composed through a finite Synthesis map before the controlled comparison. Without this closure, P4.3 would measure a candidate whose correctness and resource soundness are still open.
- `Hypothesis`: The candidate's mandatory claim set is finite and fully decided: existing matched implementation/measurement, ladder correctness parity, and generated-owner containment; no claim requires correction within scope and none remains conditional.
- `Materiality`: The closure is decided when every mandatory claim has an applicable decision on one binding identity, no claim is left open or conditional for P4.3, and no correction is outstanding. An open, contradicted, or identity-inconsistent claim fails the phase exit.

## Result

All three mandatory child claims are decided on one composed identity: the candidate's matched implementation and measurement exist and meet the declared materiality ([EXP-0106](./EXP-0106.md)); the admission ladder preserves ordering, scoping, transactional execution, scalar retry, fee/latch/custody semantics, and mutation-free refusal ([EXP-0107](./EXP-0107.md)); and every fallback admission settles within the generated owner of its executed path with no new Weight, storage, or charge owner ([EXP-0108](./EXP-0108.md)). No child decision was taken on a different binding identity, no child requires correction, and no mandatory claim remains open or conditional.

## Interpretation

The candidate's claim set is finite and closed for P4.2: its bounded physical choice is an admission-ladder level that reuses existing generated owners and existing atomic execution paths, so correctness parity and containment are decided without changing the semantic contract or the resource policy. Performance outcome classification is deliberately not decided here: regression residuals remain classified by the owning benchmark and performance-assurance contract, and the candidate's efficiency-improvement or tradeoff class is sealed only by the P4.3 controlled comparison. A count-parameterized admitted-batch owner remains unadmitted and outside this closure, as [EXP-0105](./EXP-0105.md) requires.

## Decision

Accepted. Decision scope: bounded candidate claim closure only — the P4.2 exit is met with every mandatory correctness and resource claim decided in favor of the retained candidate, which advances unchanged to the controlled P4.3 comparison. This acceptance seals no efficiency-improvement, tradeoff, selection, production or release outcome, and no outcome class is named here; the candidate's selection disposition, outcome class and finite stopping basis belong to the P4.3 comparison owner and its downstream P6 disposition.
