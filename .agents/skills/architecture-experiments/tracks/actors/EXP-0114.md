## Decision Question

Which explicit conservative generated owner closes the admission-selection Weight-ownership gap established by [EXP-0113](./EXP-0113.md), is charged exactly once per unit before the arithmetic with fail-closed affordability, and under which reissued production binding identity?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` component-wise admission and reservation-before-mutation; architecture-experiments Benchmark Reassessment Protocol `Production Weight Authority` Case B; `BACKLOG.md` P5.4; pallet specification and package architecture Weight-ownership contracts.
- `Invariant`: Every executed admission segment is covered by a generated owner benchmarked over that segment; pure control flow cannot claim zero RefTime from absent storage access or absent `meter.consume`.
- `Reopen trigger`: A changed service ladder, extraction boundary or charge order, a new or removed `#[benchmark]` scope, or a changed `WeightInfo` set.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: `service_crossing_transitions_resuming` charged `crossing_worker_base`, staged classification probes and tail-refill probe, then selected the admission branch and consumed the branch owner after execution; the selection arithmetic had no owner (EXP-0113).
- `Reason`: EXP-0113 qualified the retained binding's ownership completeness: the fixed, storage-free segment (plan-weight lookup, fault-reserve arithmetic, pair-fallback preference, scalar fallback, component and reservation checks) executes real RefTime that the production meter did not claim.
- `Hypothesis`: Extracting the segment into `select_crossing_admission`, benchmarking it as one dedicated `crossing_selection_probe` over its worst-case path and charging that owner once per unit before the arithmetic closes the gap with no admission-semantics change and no additional owner, storage access or charge inside the segment.
- `Materiality`: The claim is decided by the owner existing with a benchmarked scope that reaches every selection helper, by its charge being consumed exactly once per unit that reaches the selection stage, and by a generated value retained under the Benchmark Reassessment Protocol; a semantic difference in the extracted ladder, an uncovered helper, or a zero/duplicate charge rejects the correction.

## Statistical / Variance Notes

The five W1 fits span 1,397,000–1,467,000 ps (a 5.0% band) while the unchanged storage-reading control moved −7.3%…+10.0% across four sessions. The selection segment's replicate band is therefore narrower than the host's local RefTime noise envelope, and the retained value is the upper end of the replicated fits (1,467,000), never below the observed band. Raw outliers (up to 22,000,000 ps) are host interference on a shared workstation; they are reported and not used to inflate the coefficient. ProofSize, database shape and the charge count are deterministic and independent of RefTime.

- `Noise / Stability Evidence`: Both selector cohorts span 1,397,000–1,467,000 ps with zero ProofSize and database work. Original control drift was −7.3%…+10.0%; corrected-source control drift is −20.1%…+12.3% against pinned `80,668,000`, with unchanged 15,106 estimated ProofSize and 11 reads.

## Result

The ownership gap is closed. `crossing_selection_probe` is the only owner of the admission-selection view. Five focused generations produce a fit band of 1,397,000–1,467,000 ps at zero ProofSize; the pinned production binding now carries the conservative upper end 1,467,000 ps, and the service loop consumes it exactly once per unit before the arithmetic with a fail-closed affordability guard. The package and runtime suites, the generated binding and the client evidence agree on the reissued identity; metadata and every per-owner derived identity are unchanged.

## Interpretation

The unowned segment was real, fixed, storage-free control work whose charged magnitude had been below the benchmark's raw sample resolution before the correction: the trait's unmeasured placeholder (50,000,000 ps) over-stated it by ~34×, while the measured value is about 1.5M ps per unit and never consumes ProofSize. The change therefore does not redistribute capacity or alter any documented Two-Dimensional reservation; it makes the existing charge model honest at a bounded cost of ~0.2% of the branch owner per unit. The conservative-value rule matters more than the exact fit: the protocol's uncertainty is host noise, not mechanism, and the retained upper end covers the observed band without inflating the coefficient.

## Decision

Accepted. Outcome class: **research result** — a bounded ownership limitation is corrected by one measured owner; the added RefTime is a resource-soundness correction, not an optimization claim. Finite stopping basis: the benchmarked scope reaches every selection helper on the worst-case path, the charge is once-per-unit and fail-closed, the retained value is the upper end of the replicated fits, and no further measurement can change the decision without a source, benchmark or toolchain change. No new optimization is activated.
