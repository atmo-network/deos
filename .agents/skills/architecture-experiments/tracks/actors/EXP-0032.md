### Predicate Visit Cardinality

Source audit on `c875973` separates the hint from physical reads. `types/contract.rs::Precondition::evaluation_units` returns `p + o`; `execution.rs::evaluate_precondition_with` visits every predicate, and `evaluate_step_precondition` invokes `evaluate_atomic_predicate` only for Current timing.
