## Decision Question

Among N=12, 24 and 32 maximum inspected subscriber positions per turn on an unchanged 64-slot page, which selected full-header quantum has credible headroom for a one-family Prepass under the normative one-third Actor Control cap, and can two 12-position reservations coexist in one block under retained other-owner estimates?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §§2–3; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` ObservationChange fanout, causal cohort and progress preservation.
- `Invariant`: A turn is bounded in both Weight components, retains the authoritative feed/page/position and one paid readiness transition per subscriber, and does not impose a twelve-subscriber *block* limit. Actor Control may not exceed one third of schedulable Weight; Actor Task effects belong to Shared Economic.
- `Reopen trigger`: Bounded turns cannot preserve causal order, latching, rollback, fairness or funded service without changing the normative contract.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: The production fanout caller still consumes a full page per turn; the diagnostic core accepts an explicit maximum position count while sharing its exact state transition with that caller. The runtime now configures exact `(1, 3)` Actor Control and retains a 64-slot subscriber page.
- `Reason`: EXP-0143's selected full-header 64-subscriber page needs 1,010,826 ProofSize alone; EXP-0145 proves that the prior outer Control cap is smaller. Smaller resumable turns may make one-page progress feasible without shrinking the configured block or silently transferring work to economic/user domains.
- `Hypothesis`: N=12 gives credible component-wise room for two turns in one selected Prepass while N=24/32 test whether larger units can use capacity more efficiently without forfeiting that objective. The selected second turn is a conservative reservation screen, not a completed block replay.
- `Materiality`: One full-header turn plus retained mandatory Control owners must fit the exact one-third cap; two 12-turn reservations must also fit by a conservative sum. Neither conditional result is a production Weight or liveness proof.

## Result

All three selected bounded first turns fit the current one-third cap when substituted into retained mandatory and per-turn owners. Q32 leaves only 21,813 ProofSize of selected headroom, Q24 133,724 and Q12 303,517. A conservative two-Q12 reservation leaves 113,964. The diagnostic worker regression runs two admitted turns on a mock 16-slot page and leaves the second page cursor unchanged when a third turn lacks ProofSize. The later singleton-feed matched-Wasm result executes 24 maximum-header positions through two actual metered Q12 turns with `349,803` estimated ProofSize and `33,316,740,000` ps including RocksDB; the selected Control-plus-mandatory subtotal is `532,883 / 59,405,875,000` with margins `143,267 / 244,027,408,909`. It is one selected topology, not a branch maximum. In the original three-feed topology, the fair cursor sends the second turn to a different feed, so the singleton result cannot be subtracted from or directly ranked against the original Q12/Q24/Q32 first-turn rows. Test source `observations.rs` SHA-256 `385b9c00ccb4a38c8e31e997d2b54dca9c7adc116d1eef7fdd548b9bb00e37aa`, shared worker `reactions.rs` SHA-256 `5697ff546a944b865518463597149fac331ceffa0cb169dc0c6ab46ec5ff0d04`. The runtime regression (`actors_integration_tests.rs` SHA-256 `8420ffa701e991120091d0d4c1cac2c8567f1c052ebbebef4ded4a6acb2ff671`) confirms one retained full-page ordinary unit fits after mandatory Control, but two such units exceed the one-third ProofSize cap even before fault reserves. The family-coordinator mock now proves the structural 12+4 first-block progression and rotated second-block remainder under a candidate price and current one-third cap; it does not execute the mandatory Prepass or 64-slot runtime. After the coordinator source change, matched-Wasm worker measurement was reissued at `33,305,426,000 / 349,803 + 511 reads / 124 writes`. The stricter isolated continuation on a later matched Wasm estimates `17,255,620,000 / 184,475 + 261 reads / 66 writes`; subtracting the existing generated worker base/probe leaves `17,135,924,000 / 179,259`, below the *diagnostic* ordinary upper by `2,864,076,000 / 5,741`. The same Wasm reissues the combined two-turn profile at `33,253,464,000 / 349,803`; all earlier results remain historical, not silently replaced. The ordinary production caller still processes a full page in one turn; its old full-page Weight cannot prove repeated Q12 admission.

## Interpretation

ProofSize, not RefTime, binds the selected screen. Q32 serves more positions in one first turn than two Q12 turns under their conservative reserved total (32 versus 24), but leaves little space for other Control owners; Q12 permits a second turn under the selected equation. Neither establishes best full-block throughput, fairness across dirty feeds, mandatory Deadline/Service coexistence or universal branch weight. The existing Crossing minimum `564,801` plus mandatory `183,080` also exceeds the same one-third cap, a distinct owner that cannot be repaired by the fanout choice alone.

## Decision

Inconclusive for production selection: Q12 is retained as the smallest candidate for same-block repeated-turn validation; Q24 and Q32 pass only selected first-turn screens and remain comparators, not adopted policies. Outcome class: research result. Stop these three first-turn fixtures; qualify full-family scheduling, repeated-page physical cost and independent Crossing/Service owners before production Weight/Wasm.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: The exact fanout core admits one selected 12/24/32-position turn on a full maximum-header page; the shared metered worker repeats a diagnostic 12+4 sequence on a mock 16-slot page within one block while retaining causal cursor, next-block Service eligibility and Control-only ledger. The two-family scheduler structurally advances that work through one family-first and one Crossing-first block on the mock. Matched-Wasm worker evidence separately observes two turns on one 64-slot singleton maximum-header page. Retained-owner substitutions fit the current one-third cap for the three first turns and conservative two-Q12 sum.
- `Does not establish`: Universal branch maximum, startup family-minimum fit, 64-slot materialization-family same-block co-admission with competing Crossing, complete Prepass, Service, Actor-base or production Weight/Wasm.
- `Known limitations`: Mock page 16; benchmark-runtime page 64; actual mock mandatory Control is `3,332,734,000 / 1,015,061`, above the DEOS one-third ProofSize cap, so its prior candidate family rotation is a structural test with runtime-owner substitution, not a complete mock Prepass. Candidate `185,000` ProofSize / `20,000,000,000` ps per Q12 ordinary branch is an experimental admission price, not a measured universal upper. The selected isolated continuation does not cover refusal, rollback, tombstone saturation, latest-revision restart, or another maximum-header host geometry. The singleton repeated-page result excludes the original neighboring dirty feeds; fair rotation delays same-feed continuation in that topology. No full Prepass replay, certified failure/rollback or refreshed worst-case branch/Deadline/close coefficients.
- `Invalidation triggers`: Source/config/storage/SDK or runtime Wasm identity changes.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: The scheduler must bound and account for a *turn* separately from stored page size.
