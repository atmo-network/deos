## Decision Question

Can the current inherited `crossing_skip_pair_probe` fallback of `1,000,000,000 / 300,000` contain one exact selected read-only skip-pair classification on a legal full 128-member Crossing page in both dimensions? The corrected two-member-source diagnostic in EXP-0157 fits its RefTime default by only `5,904,000` ps; this is one earned page-width falsifier, not a matrix or a production coefficient.

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §§2–4; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` Crossing deterministic source authority.
- `Invariant`: Classify the two actually executed adjacent skip candidates on the bounded canonical page under independent RefTime and ProofSize admission, without borrowing Shared Economic capacity.
- `Reopen trigger`: Full-page skip-pair classification cannot be bounded inside the mandatory-plus-Crossing one-third Actor Control reservation without a conforming change.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: The full `T::CrossingPageSize` (128 in reference runtime) may contain a homogeneous adjacent skip pair plus unrelated members. Classification reads its page, validates the adjacent two members and charges the `crossing_skip_pair_probe` trait fallback. EXP-0157's corrected-source two-member probe was `994,096,000 / 11,729` including 31 RocksDB reads, leaving only `5,904,000` ps of selected RefTime margin.
- `Reason`: The same bounded physical page becomes larger while the paid probe method and two inspected Users remain unchanged. A page-width read/decode can exceed the narrow existing margin.
- `Hypothesis`: The full-page selected probe exceeds the retained fallback in at least RefTime. ProofSize is independent; a measured page is not actual relay PoV.
- `Materiality`: Any selected excess falsifies the existing charge for the exact reachable path and changes the finite production owner decision.

- `Changed variables`: Fill their existing source page to `T::CrossingPageSize` with inert System Crossing members after the two User candidates; the fill and postcondition are outside the timed block.

## Result

The corrected pair classifier on one 128-member legal source page exceeds the charged probe's RefTime default by `70,294,000` ps. Frame measured proof rises to `16,673` while its generated estimate remains `11,729`; both are below the current `300,000` ProofSize reservation.

## Interpretation

This is a selected **physical RefTime underbound**, not a complete upper or a proof that the sole cause of the cross-Wasm difference is page width. A future generated coefficient must cover maximum legal page/header and the control preflight/failure selectors in both dimensions; the measured-versus-estimated proof gap requires explicit review rather than promotion of `11,729`.

## Decision

Reject the current pair-probe fallback as a conservative RefTime charge for the selected full-page path. Outcome class: research result; stop after one maximum-page witness. Retain the old default in production only as an explicitly underbound gate pending complete owner/mandatory composition; do not change production Weight/Wasm or the block policy independently.
