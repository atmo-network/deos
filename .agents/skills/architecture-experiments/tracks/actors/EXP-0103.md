## Decision Question

Does the executed per-block ObservationCrossing materialization rate on the pinned released binding bind on the Crossing candidate branch cost, or on the shared materialization family grant inside the Actor Control envelope?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §7.6 owns the one-third Actor Control ceiling and forbids further throughput work from taking additional guaranteed capacity; `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §2 owns the family minimum/cursor fairness rule; `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` §§1.1, 2.1 owns the two-axis reporting and the reactive reference profile.
- `Invariant`: One scheduler, strict FIFO, Q1, the causal floor, bounded per-block worker work, and component-wise admission remain unchanged.
- `Reopen trigger`: A measured executed rate that is invariant to the family rotation position, or one that exceeds the per-block candidate counter cap, reopens this attribution rather than selecting a candidate.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: The mandatory prepass reserves one shared materialization envelope, lends the remainder to the first served family, reserves every later family minimum, and may serve the rotation cursor family once more with trailing budget. The Crossing worker then admits candidates until its own per-block component counters or its granted meter stops it.
- `Reason`: The released capability model derives four Crossing candidates per block from the dedicated `CrossingWorkerWeightLimit` pair arithmetic (EXP-0098). That is a derived capacity envelope; the executed per-block rate under the real prepass grant was never measured, so the campaign could not bound what a materialization-efficiency candidate can buy.
- `Hypothesis`: The executed rate binds on the shared family grant inside the Actor Control envelope, not on the per-candidate Crossing branch envelope, so per-candidate cost reduction alone cannot raise the mean rate.
- `Materiality`: A decision-relevant difference in the attributed owner or in the reachable per-block rate; closing the retired `10,000`/`100` horizon is not required.

## Derived Metrics

- `Executed cycle`: phases yield 1, 3 and 8 candidates; cycle total 12 in exactly three blocks.
- `Executed mean`: `(1 + 3 + 8) / 3 = 4` candidates/block, equal to the released derived coordinate.
- `Executed ceiling`: 8 candidates/block = `MaxCrossingTransitionsPerBlock`, reached under the richest grant.
- `Reactive coverage bound`: `4 * 100 / 10,000 = 4%` on the mean and `8 * 100 / 10,000 = 8%` on the ceiling, both independent of the separate 9,000-User membership cap.

## Statistical / Variance Notes

Deterministic FRAME execution and generated Weight: no warmup, sample, percentile, or outlier treatment applies. Determinism is evidenced by four identical `(1, 3, 8)` cycles in the retained 48-member witness and by the same cycle amplitude at 12 and 24 members. No wall-clock or host claim is made.

## Result

On the pinned binding the mandatory prepass materializes Crossing candidates at a deterministic rate that follows the materialization family rotation: 1, 3 and 8 candidates across the three cursor phases, repeating every three blocks, exactly once per candidate, and never above `MaxCrossingTransitionsPerBlock = 8`. The Actor Control ProofSize envelope (676,150) is far below the shared materialization limit (2,200,000), and the three family minimum quanta (667,364) leave only 1,244 ProofSize of the 668,608 materialization budget, so the reservation set is effective and the served order decides each family's grant.

## Interpretation

The bound is the shared-envelope schedule over the Actor Control ProofSize domain, not the Crossing candidate branch envelope: a single grant admits at most three single-candidate branches or one eight-candidate admitted cohort, and the Crossing branch weight is never the last consumed resource in a capped block. Per-candidate cost reduction therefore raises the rate only toward the fixed per-block counter ceiling, and it cannot raise it past it. Candidate A is rejected as the executed owner because the executed mean and ceiling both exceed the dedicated pair-arithmetic coordinate; candidate B is rejected as the executed owner because the rate changes with a grant the branch envelope does not influence. The result does not measure which internal decision stops the Crossing worker inside a grant; that attribution is not needed for the campaign payoff bound and is transferred to P2.3 if a selected candidate requires it.

## Decision

Accepted as a research result for the executed ObservationCrossing materialization rate. The finite stopping basis is that the attributed owner and the reachable rate are decided by the deterministic retained witness; further measurement of this claim is not required. Decision scope: executed baseline capacity and owner attribution only; no physical architecture, semantic, or resource-policy change is selected. The campaign's materialization-efficiency payoff is bounded by the per-block counter ceiling of 8 candidates, so any candidate claiming a materialization-rate improvement must state how it approaches that ceiling and must not claim closure of the retired `10,000`/`100` horizon.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: The executed per-block Crossing materialization rate, its rotation alignment, its counter ceiling, and the Actor Control/family-minimum root envelope on the pinned binding.
- `Does not establish`: Production-Wasm replay of the multi-candidate prepass path, per-family actual Weight attribution inside one grant, the internal Crossing worker stop reason per grant, Transfer-service throughput after materialization, or any membership-cap change.
- `Known limitations`: Native full-runtime profile rather than exact-Wasm replay; the fixture prints public pallet storage for diagnosis but asserts only on events, configuration limits, and generated Weight.
- `Invalidation triggers`: Actor Control ratio, fixed envelope, family limits or minimum quanta, Crossing worker caps, `MaxCrossingTransitionsPerBlock`, rotation policy, generated Weight, or the prepass family algebra.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: Materialization capacity is a scheduled grant; candidate-level cost is a secondary lever behind the per-block grant and the counter ceiling.

## Follow-Up / Next Gradient

```text
target → measured gap → binding dimension → dominant contributor
→ owning mechanism → next hypothesis → smallest falsifier
```

- `Measured gap`: executed mean 4 candidates/block versus the retired 100/block horizon.
- `Binding dimension`: ProofSize inside the one-third Actor Control envelope.
- `Dominant contributor`: shared materialization family rotation with minimum-quantum reservations.
- `Owning mechanism`: `service_materialization_families` / `materialization_family_budget` over the Crossing admission loop.
- `Next hypothesis`: a crossing materialization candidate can raise the mean toward the 8-candidate counter ceiling only by reducing per-candidate ProofSize or branch count per admitted cohort.
- `Smallest falsifier`: a matched candidate comparison showing a higher executed mean with unchanged or lower per-candidate ProofSize and unchanged semantics.
- `Eliminated hypotheses`: dedicated-envelope and branch-cost executed ownership; cohort discovery.
- `Remaining hypotheses`: batch/cohort admission amortization, per-candidate index-write reduction, grant-schedule changes as a labelled policy tradeoff.
- `Stop condition`: the attribution claim is decided; candidate admission belongs to the campaign's finite candidate set.
