## Decision Question

Does the admitted pair-owner fallback reach the generated two-candidate placed owner through the real Crossing execution owner, and does it meet the frozen materiality against the sealed released cohort: a previously lean rotation phase admitting its classified scalar-equivalent placed pair (the generated two-candidate placed owner) in one admission decision where the released binding admits one scalar candidate, and the executed three-block cycle rising above `12`?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §2 owns component-wise admission, reservation-before-mutation with valid-actual settlement, and the materialization family rotation; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` §7.6 owns the one-third Actor Control ceiling; `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` §§1.1, 2.1 owns the two-axis reporting and the reactive reference profile.
- `Invariant`: One scheduler, strict FIFO, Q1, the causal floor and cutoff, component-wise admission, reservation-before-mutation with valid-actual settlement, cursor-rotation materialization fairness, and the per-block component counters remain unchanged; the candidate takes no additional guaranteed in-block capacity.
- `Reopen trigger`: A needed priority, same-block execution, relaxed rollback, additional guaranteed capacity, or semantic-guarantee change; a per-decision admitted cohort above the generated pair owner without a new generated owner; or a fallback that cannot revalidate its authority transactionally.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: The released binding reduces a classified multi-candidate placed batch to the scalar single when its aggregate owner does not fit the grant. The generated pair owner carries the same `162,782` ProofSize as the scalar single and a `775,848,000` runtime-bound RefTime premium.
- `Reason`: [EXP-0105](./EXP-0105.md) admitted the pair-owner fallback first in the finite candidate set with declared materiality: the admission-mode switch in a previously lean phase plus an executed cycle above the released `12`.
- `Hypothesis`: The pair owner is affordable wherever the scalar single is affordable because both reserve the same ProofSize; reducing the classified batch to the pair before the scalar single therefore doubles the lean-phase admission count per decision without a new Weight owner, a storage change, or additional guaranteed capacity.
- `Materiality`: The frozen [EXP-0105](./EXP-0105.md) materiality: one previously lean rotation phase admits its classified scalar-equivalent placed pair in one admission decision where the released binding admits one scalar candidate, the executed steady three-block cycle rises above `12`, all correctness assertions stay green, no settlement exceeds its generated owner, and no other family in-block minimum or settled per-candidate Weight worsens. Scope note: the retained per-decision cohort is the scalar-equivalent generated pair (`2` candidates), not the counter-limited maximum (`8`).

## Result

The candidate pair owner executed through `service_crossing_transitions_resuming` in the real mandatory prepass. The previously lean rotation phases switched from the scalar single to the generated pair: the steady cursor-zero phase settled `2` candidates and the rotated-first phase settled `6`, against released `1` and `3`, at identical Actor Control ProofSize (`312,497 / 652,800 / 665,647 / 565,437`). The rotated-last phase remained the `8`-candidate aggregate batch. The steady three-block cycle rose from the released `12` to `16`; the whole 48-member cohort materialized exactly once and stopped at block 11 instead of block 13. Every settlement stayed at or below its generated owner: each lean-phase admission settled `crossing_placed_pair_unit` and the rotated-first Actor Control RefTime equals the released phase plus exactly three runtime-bound pair premiums. The single-check boundaries, the aggregate residue, the family minima, and the component counter ceiling were unchanged.

## Interpretation

The binding dimension of the candidate is ProofSize: the generated pair has the same `162,782` ProofSize as the scalar single with a bounded positive RefTime premium, so the fallback switches the admission mode without taking additional guaranteed in-block capacity — the Crossing family minimum already reserved the pair boundary (`196,066` = base + common probe + pair probe + pair owner + fault). The RefTime premium (`775,848,000` runtime-bound) is the measured new cost and is amortized across the two admitted candidates, halving settled per-candidate RefTime. The materiality declared in [EXP-0105](./EXP-0105.md) is met on the measured mode-switch and cycle criteria: lean phases now admit the scalar-equivalent two-candidate placed pair from the classified cohort in one decision where the released binding admitted one scalar candidate, the steady cycle is `16 > 12`, every correctness assertion in the Crossing set stayed green, no settlement exceeded its generated owner, and no other family minimum or settled per-candidate Weight worsened. Explicit scope: the retained per-decision cohort is the pair (`2`), not the `MaxCrossingTransitionsPerBlock` per-decision maximum (`8`); reaching the counter-limited cohort per decision remains the separately admitted count-parameterized candidate's domain, which this record does not implement, and no universal throughput or latency claim is made.
