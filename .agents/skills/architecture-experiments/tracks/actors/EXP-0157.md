## Decision Question

Do matched-Wasm *selected* skip-pair probe and unit diagnostics reveal an owner where current `WeightInfo` default reservations (`300,000` and `250,000` ProofSize) are materially above the selected generated estimates, and would substituting those estimates alone make the current complete mandatory-plus-Crossing screen fit one-third Actor Control? This does not approve replacing either default without full physical selector coverage.

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §§1–3; `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` Crossing materialization and B+1 causality.
- `Invariant`: One complete admitted Crossing quantum and mandatory Deadline/Service headroom use only Actor Control, at most exactly one third of schedulable RefTime and ProofSize. No inferred fee charge or generated owner is accepted from a selected fixture.
- `Reopen trigger`: A needed resource share above one third, changed phase/rollback semantics, or missing authoritative fixed/context reservation reopens architecture, not these benchmarks.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: The two skip-pair methods are extra-only benchmarks absent from the runtime generated Weight implementation; trait defaults supply `1,000,000,000 / 300,000` for `crossing_skip_pair_probe` and `750,000,000 / 250,000` for `crossing_skip_pair_unit`. Component-wise maxima make both dominate Crossing family minimum ProofSize: `1,543 + 11,729 + 300,000 + 250,000 + 1,529 = 564,801`. Together with retained mandatory `183,080`, the conditional floor exceeds current Control `676,150` by `71,731`. `crossing_same_tail_page_places_two_candidates_in_one_admitted_cohort` confirms that `SkipPostInstallationPair` occurs after a later canonical transition (the test positions its cursor).
- `Reason`: Unbound fallback defaults may be the independent Crossing startup gate rather than the placed-pair body or fanout page. They cannot be removed merely because they look large.
- `Hypothesis`: Both selected estimates are smaller than the defaults in both dimensions, and a conditional mandatory-plus-family screen fits the current Control if, *only for arithmetic*, those selected estimates replace the two defaults. The unit RefTime result falsifies the first conjunct.
- `Materiality`: Establish whether full selector qualification of these two named owners is a worthwhile next resource gradient; do not generate production Weight or reduce admission from a fixture.

## Result

The first attempt failed before measurement because both extra fixtures aligned a nonexistent frame primary for canonical Unsignaled Actors. After removing those stale setup calls, both package tests passed and the two corrected extra benchmarks ran sequentially on Wasm `90607251…`. Selected probe estimate `981,943,000 / 11,729` is below its default `1,000,000,000 / 300,000`; selected pair unit estimate `1,135,571,000 / 11,729` exceeds its charged default RefTime `750,000,000` while remaining below its ProofSize `250,000`. No production Weight or Wasm was changed.

## Interpretation

The ProofSize default pair drives the retained family minimum, but the unit default is not a conservative *two-dimensional* upper even for this selected fixture. Hypothetically replacing only those two fallback estimates lowers the family-minimum ProofSize to `206,530` (not `189,312`: the unbound `crossing_skip_unit` default `180,000` then dominates), and retained mandatory plus one-family screen fits current one-third Control. This is necessary arithmetic, not a complete Prepass, certified pair/skip bound, or permission to replace defaults. The generated proof estimates for P/U share keys and are not an atomic combined PoV measurement.

## Decision

Rejected as a safe two-dimensional default-reservation hypothesis; outcome class: research result. A selected reachable skip-pair unit RefTime falsifies its charged default, so keep the complete owner open. Do not hand-edit a new production coefficient or lower the ProofSize reserve from two selected extra fixtures; qualify the complete live skip pair and scalar/failure selectors, then coordinate production Weight/Wasm and mandatory/Service admission. Stop this selected P/U fixture.
