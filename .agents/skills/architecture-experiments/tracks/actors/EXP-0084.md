## Decision Question

Do source-bound charged-owner equations reconstruct the retained System schedule cohorts' complete Control and generated I/O, keeping temporal work, successful Steps, baseline and no-progress work distinct?

## Governing Specification

- `Owners`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` (Trigger latching, scheduler and lifecycle ownership); `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` (component-wise admission and settlement); `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` (production evidence).
- `Invariant`: Frozen C1 class-neutral FIFO, ticket chronology/cutoff, Q1, causal floor, complete-owner accounting and separate Task effects.
- `Reopen trigger`: Evidenced semantic conflict, not cost or stale evidence alone.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: Native full-block authors and restricted public-worker replays reconstruct generated charges against retained production phase totals and headers.
- `Hypothesis`: The schedule-specific owner frequencies exactly explain each retained cohort without allocating shared work to individual Actors or equating generated I/O with physical calls.
- `Materiality`: A mismatching owner equation, occurrence order, phase total or required header falsifies scoped accounting; no new schedule selector or optimization is proposed.
