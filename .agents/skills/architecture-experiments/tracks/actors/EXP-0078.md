## Decision Question

Does temporal materialization share the mandatory-Prepass Control budget with service while the captured cutoff defers newly materialized Actors?

## Governing Specification

- `Owners`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` (temporal readiness, scheduler, cutoff); `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` (Control accounting); `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` (evidence).
- `Invariant`: FIFO, Q1, causal floor, equal thirds and committed Steps remain unchanged.
- `Reopen trigger`: Any proposal changing phase authority or causal order must reopen the owning specification, not masquerade as optimization.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Hypothesis`: Temporal occurrence materialization competes with service in the same mandatory Prepass; new readiness cannot cross the cutoff in that block.
- `Materiality`: Exact placement and disjointness are required, independent of any cost-saving proposal.
