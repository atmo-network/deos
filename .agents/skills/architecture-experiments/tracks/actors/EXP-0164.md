## Decision Question

Do the retained generated Trigger owners bound a legal maximum-header User Actor — maximum Steps, maximum source/asset filters, signed funding and balance-watch header — for one useful occurrence?

## Context and Candidates

- `Current architecture`: `manual_trigger()` and `address_event_trigger_occurrence()` charge signed Manual dispatch and one matched AddressEvent notification; the User Trigger fee uses the same owner.
- `Hypothesis`: The retained short-header owners also bound the maximum legal header.
- `Materiality`: Any selected maximum-header occurrence above the owner in either dimension rejects it as a complete bound.
- `Controlled / changed variables`: Same benchmark Wasm; only header geometry changes. Source publication, later Pipeline and Task effects stay outside the measured owner.

### Benchmark Evidence Disposition

- `Benchmark Evidence Status`: Qualified — selected maximum-header falsifiers, not universal maxima; one run each.
- `Reassessment Trigger`: Coordinated regeneration or a change to header limits, filter sizes or funding/watch layout.
- `Compared Observation IDs`: Archived EXP-0148 and EXP-0149 outputs.
- `Noise / Stability Evidence`: None; the ratios (about 2.1–2.2× RefTime and 2.3–3.5× ProofSize) far exceed plausible noise.
- `Current Authority`: Rejects both retained owners as complete maximum-header bounds; supplies no production coefficient.

## Result

Both maximum-header occurrences exceed their retained owners in RefTime and ProofSize. Manual's generated ProofSize (21,922) is about twice its measured PoV (11,032) because `MaxEncodedLen` accounting charges maximum header size.

## Interpretation

Trigger cost scales with header size (filters, funding and watches), which the short-header benchmarks never exercised. Signed Manual dispatch and the User Trigger fee are therefore underpriced for large headers. The gap between generated and measured PoV is an accounting question for `Actors/0.7.28 PoV Accounting`, not a reason to charge less now.

## Decision

Reject both retained owners as complete maximum-header bounds; production coefficients and fee vectors are rebound only through Detector/Placement closure and coordinated generation. Outcome class: research result. Stop after these two witnesses; do not expand a header × family matrix.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: Concrete underbounds for Manual and AddressEvent at selected maximum headers.
- `Does not establish`: Universal maxima, AtTime/Cadenced owners, or post-cut coefficients.
- `Invalidation triggers`: Header limits, filter or watch layout, or Trigger owner benchmarks change.
