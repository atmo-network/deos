## Interpretation

The traversal has its own fixed physical owner because zero Opening results do not imply zero structural work. Its direct full-Contract benchmark, zero database topology and mutually exclusive selector are sufficient for this bounded question without rerunning nonzero host capture or complete Opening. Raw maximum wall time is not a consensus coefficient, and the generated median owner is not combined with the nonzero capture intercept.

## Decision

Accepted for the reference binding. When `opening_predicate_results == 0` in an Opening head, select exactly `opening_predicate_traversal()` at `1,885,000` RefTime / zero ProofSize; for every nonzero count, select only the accepted nonzero predicate capture envelope. Maximum and actual use the same partition, and inclusive direct Opening owners receive no additive traversal. EXP-0065 owns complete final-tree regeneration and artifact identity.

## Rejected Alternatives and Tradeoffs

Reject omitting traversal because the result vector is empty, reusing a nonzero capture intercept with phantom proof, adding traversal to nonzero capture, and adding it to inclusive direct Opening owners. Retain raw timing tails as observations rather than manually padding the generated fixed coefficient.
