## Decision Question

After the converged Q1 path is fully optimized and measured, does remaining context/persistence overhead still bind strongly enough to justify reopening Specification for a controlled Q2/Q4 Pareto comparison over accepted C6 Chunk4 storage?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact sections`: `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` → `1.4 Block-Paced Actor Cycles`, `4.1 Execution Model`, `4.2 Snapshots and Fresh Opening`, and `5.1 FIFO and Temporal Readiness`; `docs/actors-performance-assurance.specification.en.md` → `Independent Throughput Axes`, `Required Cost, Latency, and Fairness Metrics`, and `Contract Complexity Matrix`.
- `Invariant`: Chunk is physical Contract fragmentation, Batch/Service Quantum is the maximum consecutive Steps in one Actor turn, and Cohort is shared causal origin. Candidates preserve deterministic actor-major FIFO order, User/System neutrality, per-Step atomic effects and fees, bounded work, no Task-family ranking, no task-shape affinity, and no Actor-produced same-block reaction cascade.
- `Reopen trigger`: The authoritative Specification fixes Q1 and next-block continuation. Q2/Q4 require explicit Specification reopening and may re-enter only if converged Q1 still misses its committed-Step target and controlled decomposition proves context/persistence overhead remains binding after accepted reuse, fragment, accounting, and scheduler optimizations. T0 belongs to separate Fresh Step-0 causality research.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: EXP-0016 accepted one inline Step 0 plus authority-bound tail chunks of at most four Steps while retaining Q1 execution. EXP-0013 accepted carrying one validated decoded state and admission certificate through one service transition, reducing the measured marginal cheap-Actor RefTime `167,553,076 → 137,612,859` (`-17.87%`); later loaded-state corrections removed additional redundant reconstruction without changing Q1 semantics.
- `Reason`: Repeated fragment loading, control setup, run persistence, and scheduler placement may dominate cheap multi-Step pipelines. C6's physical locality earns a controlled service-quantum question but does not imply that BatchMax equals ChunkSize.
- `Hypothesis`: Up-to-Q2 or up-to-Q4 actor-major service may amortize Actor-machine cost per committed Step and shorten short-pipeline completion while retaining a useful distinct-Actor breadth frontier. The null result is that Q1 remains preferable because next-block Current semantics, population breadth, proof/write pressure, or head-of-line behavior dominate.
- `Materiality`: A candidate must be Pareto-relevant across Steps/block and Actors/block and materially reduce Actor-machine RefTime or pipeline latency without violating component-wise ProofSize/block limits, deterministic service, accepted temporal semantics, or healthy service gaps. No scalar score may combine the two throughput axes or the two Weight dimensions.

## Benchmark Class, Workloads, and Criteria

- `Benchmark class`: Controlled pallet/production-Wasm branch measurement for marginal costs, deterministic native or integration stress for populations and cohort divergence, and full-runtime block profiles for end-to-end throughput, latency, fairness, head-of-line loss, and resource composition.

| Workload | State/population geometry | Hot/cold assumptions | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W1 control-only | Pipeline lengths `1,2,4,8,16,32`; boundary populations `10,100,1,000,10,000` where feasible | C6 Step 0 hot; required tail chunk cold once per service turn | Isolate scheduler, fragment, RunFrame, fee, and persistence amortization |
| W2 Burn-like | Same representative lengths/populations with deterministic cheap effect | Canonical effect owner; no fabricated priority | Measure automation tax around a cheap useful effect |
| W3 Transfer | Same representative lengths/populations and stable recipients | Real ledger consequences and ingress ownership | Compare machine/action ratio and write pressure |
| W4 Swap | Deterministic bounded liquidity, typed success/failure, current market predicates | Real route/effect Weight; no stale shared quote | Measure market-heavy fit, divergence, and head-of-line capacity |
| W5 liquidity | Bounded add/remove-liquidity fixture | Real reserve/accounting writes | Measure heavy action without ranking by cost |
| W6 mixed population | `40%` one-Step, `25%` two-Step, `20%` four-Step, `10%` eight-Step, `5%` sixteen/thirty-two-Step boundary sample | Deterministic activation order | Locate Steps/block versus Actors/block Pareto frontier under heterogeneous demand |
| W7 divergent pipelines | Controlled Current false, temporary failure, funding unavailable, early completion, and retry at later cursors | Divergence seed and branch counts fixed | Measure early yield, service gap, cohort half-life, and wasted capacity |
| W8 shared-cause cohorts | One Trigger revision activates `10,100,1,000,10,000` short Actors | Cohort exists only through shared causal origin | Test whether Q2/Q4 expands the cohort-friendly short-Contract class without dynamic re-cohorting |
| W9 causal timing | T+1 baseline; T0 only if separately admitted | Exact trigger block, cutoff, first service, and Actor-produced causes recorded | Measure trigger-to-first-Step latency and verify causal firewall |

- `Metrics`: CommittedStepsPerBlock; DistinctActorsProgressedPerBlock; ActorMachine RefTime/ProofSize per committed Step; reads/writes per committed Step; useful Action Weight versus Actor-machine Weight; trigger-to-first-Step and trigger-to-completion latency; continuously runnable service-gap distribution; head-of-line unused capacity; completion/outcome counts; cohort materialization/execution cost; cohort half-life/divergence; per-block control/effect usage; Wasm size; exact faults and ordering.
- `Statistical method`: Production FRAME Weight uses 50 steps/20 repeats, generated component review, and no outlier removal. Native/integration profiles use deterministic seeds, declared warmup, at least five retained runs when affordable, median plus range/p95, and no outlier removal unless contamination is recorded before interpretation. Full-runtime profiles retain every measured block rather than averaging away service gaps or head-of-line tails.
- `Acceptance criteria`: Select only a specification-conforming candidate on the useful two-axis Pareto frontier. It must materially improve Steps/block, per-Step machine cost, or completion latency while retaining substantial distinct-Actor breadth and bounded service gaps; fit RefTime and ProofSize independently; preserve exact actor-major order and every Step outcome; and add no Task/class/fee ranking or dynamic re-cohorting. Q1 wins if Q2/Q4 are semantically inadmissible, Pareto-dominated, or add complexity without material frontier gain.
- `Rejection criteria`: Semantic violation; same-block Current reinterpretation without authority; Actor-produced same-block cascade; FIFO reorder; non-atomic Step fees/effects; class or Task priority; component-wise block overrun; cold-body ceiling tax; unacceptable breadth/service-gap regression; hidden divergence; or a tiny throughput gain that does not justify consensus complexity.

No quantum samples exist. EXP-0013 and EXP-0016 establish causal motivation and accepted storage/reuse baselines only; neither measures Q2/Q4 service. The full matrix may use representative boundary points instead of every Cartesian combination when cost is unreasonable, but every omission must preserve pipeline, population, action, divergence, and cohort boundaries sufficient to decide the frontier.

## Result

No quantum measurement result. The converged design direction retains Q1 and narrows active work to making the one-Step boundary cheap, load-elastic continuation liveness, Fresh Step-0 latency, and shared-cause Fresh cohorts. Q2/Q4 are preserved as reopenable provenance rather than active candidates.

## Interpretation

The C6 chunk size and Q1 service quantum remain independent. EXP-0013 shows repeated reconstruction is expensive, but the preferred next act is to delete that boundary cost rather than hide it behind a larger quantum. Q1 retains maximum scheduler granularity, population breadth, next-block Current semantics, and load degradation through service gap. Cohorts remain shared-cause Fresh materialization only and dissolve after Step 0 unless the one-Step Actor completes.

## Decision

Proposed and explicitly dormant. No physical baseline changes; Q1/T+1 remains authoritative. Do not transition to Prepared unless the recorded Q1 failure/decomposition reopen condition is met. Fresh T0/T+1 work transfers to EXP-0018 rather than reviving this quantum matrix.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: The exact research boundary, semantic gate, controlled candidate axes, required workloads/metrics, baseline lineage, and artifact plan for a future Q1/Q2/Q4 comparison.
- `Does not establish`: That Q2/Q4 are valid, faster, fair, dispatchable, or preferable; that Chunk4 implies Q4; that same-block T0 is safe; or that 100 Steps/block equals 100 Actors/block.
- `Known limitations`: I4 production evidence is not regenerated; no quantum harness or sample exists; the deferred matrix has no numeric comparison and supports no Q2/Q4 performance claim.
- `Invalidation triggers`: Service/fairness/causality specification changes beyond the declared candidates; C6 replacement; resource-policy or block-Weight changes; executor/fee/RunFrame topology changes; toolchain/backend drift; or an I4 baseline that removes the hypothesized amortizable owner.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: Keep Chunk, Service Quantum, and Cohort independently tunable and report Steps/block and Actors/block independently even if Q1 remains final.

## Follow-Up / Next Gradient

```text
100 cheap committed Steps/block target → no Q2/Q4 evidence
→ unknown RefTime/ProofSize/breadth frontier → repeated fragment/control/run persistence
→ Actor service quantum → bounded up-to-Q hypothesis
→ Specification reopening, then smallest Q1/Q2/Q4 short-pipeline falsifier
```

- `Eliminated hypotheses`: Chunk4 alone justifies Q4; Task similarity justifies cohort affinity; one scalar throughput score can select the design.
- `Remaining hypotheses`: Q1 can become a tiny process-state transition; Fresh T0 may reduce first-reaction latency with a one-generation firewall; Q2/Q4 become relevant only if Q1 context/persistence overhead remains binding after convergence.
- `Stop condition`: Stop when one conforming candidate is Pareto-relevant and materially justified across representative cheap/effectful, mixed, divergent, and shared-cause profiles, or when Specification retains Q1/T+1 and thereby rejects Q2/Q4/T0 without candidate construction.
