## Decision Question

Which single physical design carries current-state Actor semantics — process ownership, discovery, residence, rounds, wake acknowledgment, deadline placement, lifecycle and resource ownership — without legacy snapshot or Ready-queue authority?

## Context and Candidates

- `Current architecture`: The released 0.7.26 engine moved whole-cell frames through a ticket FIFO with Opening snapshots, latches, successor tickets and waiting-primary copies, coupling unrelated lifetimes.
- `Hypothesis`: One stable Actor-generation process with generation-bound memberships, one Live/Pending ring and the retained C32 deadline substrate carries every supported state with bounded structural work and no legacy authority.
- `Materiality`: Each choice must remove a named structural cost or hazard (whole-population scan, predecessor discovery, duplicate residence, lost wakeup, unowned cleanup) without semantic change; no throughput benefit is claimed.
- `Controlled / changed variables`: Semantics fixed by the Actors specification; one candidate per decision family, each with one smallest falsifier. Candidate sets were frozen and exhausted before selection.

## Workloads and Criteria

- `Evidence class`: Design-level structural analysis, finite interleaving matrices and native regressions; no production benchmark.
- `Acceptance criteria`: Every supported state has exactly one semantic owner and at most one residence; every transition has a bounded plan; every hazard has an invariant and a finite falsifier.
- `Rejection criteria`: Any required population, predecessor or deadline-range scan; duplicate or zero residence; lost acknowledgment; custody mutation by scheduling.

### Behavior Placement Comparison (2026-10-02)

- `Freeze`: O15 frozen 2026-10-02 before its deciding probe; no O1–O14 choice is reopened. The approved outcome-policy extension introduces previously absent authored policy and monitor state.
- `Candidates`: Inline optional expression in the common head plus optional monitor in the common Run versus one fixed head presence bit and optional Actor-keyed authored-policy/monitor companions. The public canonical Contract and its semantic hash remain identical; full reconstruction joins the companion. Companions remain under Contract/lifecycle ownership and create no queue or service order.
- `Controls`: The existing native mock head is the byte-identical common carrier, not a proposed public ABI. The four-by-four expression is diagnostic geometry, not a selected host limit. The existing ten-metric monitor supplies its codec ceiling. No RefTime, production Weight or exact-Wasm claim is admitted.
- `Materiality`: Reject any policy-width term in shared no-policy head metadata or monitor-width term in the shared no-policy Run capacity. Select companions only if a false presence bit performs no policy/monitor read and a true bit reaches bounded companion keys. Stop at that structural placement decision; host limits, complete lifecycle binding and cost qualification remain backlog work.

### Benchmark Evidence Disposition

- `Benchmark Evidence Status`: Qualified — O14 rests on one matched pre-cut pallet benchmark; every other obligation is structural.
- `Reassessment Trigger`: A structural or soundness falsifier named in O1–O14, or a change to the governing specification.
- `Compared Observation IDs`: None.
- `Noise / Stability Evidence`: None.
- `Current Authority`: Physical design baseline for the Actors track; the O14 comparison decides the design, not a production coefficient. It proves neither Weight soundness nor throughput.

O15 is structural codec/FRAME metadata and native recorded-key evidence, not a benchmark. Its dimensional observations select placement only; changing companion payloads, scope references or clean flags requires final geometry qualification, while changing the no-policy lookup/shared-capacity premise reopens the decision. No RefTime or ProofSize discount follows from these observations.

The owner-selected reference watch ceiling changed from twelve to four on 2026-10-05, then to one for 0.7.27. Multi-recipient Task fanout is removed; individual Transfers share one bounded per-Cycle volume snapshot when exact distribution is required. Four-watch/fanout observations remain historical and require reassessment for the current domain. This is a semantic-domain tradeoff, not an architecture comparison or measured optimization. Earlier twelve-watch resource/profile and fixed-corpus observations remain Historical for their exact configuration; they prove no current maximum or Control excess. The accepted process/residence architecture remains selected. The current `MaxParkedBalanceWatches = 1` typed activation/episode/registration plan and eleven-Task enum exclude the studied single-Actor multi-watch and inline SplitTransfer constructions. Their repeated-history obligation is retired; this does not exclude global sources or recipients across many Actors or adapter-internal effects. Reopening construction work requires a current-domain witness and decision-relevant exceeding lower under `BACKLOG.md`'s Close Resumption. `Actors/Dependency Reclamation Resource Qualification` owns current one-watch physical qualification; no coefficient, full budget fit or baseline is accepted by this decision.

## Result

Every supported current-state transition has one semantic owner, at most one residence and a bounded structural plan. Per-Step Ready dequeue, successor ticket allocation, Ready-page growth, waiting-primary copies and latch rearm are structurally eliminated. Close is synchronous and bounded; no asynchronous cleanup debt exists. Vacancy authority cut maximum deadline destination planning from 314 reads to two; mutation tests then exposed a truncated-page indexing panic and a missing vacancy head treated as full, both fixed by shared header validation, bounded page refusal and checked C32 access without changing the design.

O15's three probes pass. Inline and companion absent-policy values have equal actual size, but inline adds the complete expression maximum to shared head metadata. Inline monitor adds its full optional maximum to the Run capacity reserved before any Run exists.
