## Decision Question

Which single physical design carries current-state Actor semantics — process ownership, discovery, residence, rounds, wake acknowledgment, deadline placement, lifecycle and resource ownership — without legacy snapshot or Ready-queue authority?

## Context and Candidates

- `Current architecture`: The released 0.7.26 engine moved whole-cell frames through a ticket FIFO with Opening snapshots, latches, successor tickets and waiting-primary copies, coupling unrelated lifetimes.
- `Hypothesis`: One stable Actor-generation process with generation-bound memberships, one Live/Pending ring and the retained C32 deadline substrate carries every supported state with bounded structural work and no legacy authority.
- `Materiality`: Each choice must remove a named structural cost or hazard (whole-population scan, predecessor discovery, duplicate residence, lost wakeup, unowned cleanup) without semantic change; no throughput benefit is claimed.
- `Controlled / changed variables`: Semantics fixed by the Actors specification; one candidate per decision family, each with one smallest falsifier. Candidate sets were frozen and exhausted before selection.

## Result

The selected design assigns one semantic owner, at most one residence and a bounded structural plan under its stated invariants. It eliminates per-Step Ready dequeue, successor ticket allocation, Ready-page growth, waiting-primary copies and latch rearm. Synchronous reclamation is the selected topology, not a certificate for complete Close geometry, resource bounds or production serviceability. O14's matched comparison reduces maximum destination planning from 314 reads to two; truncated-page and missing-vacancy-head rejection remain its mutation falsifiers, not proof of complete Deadline callers.

At O15's decision-time probe, three synthetic comparisons pass: absent-policy actual sizes agree, inline policy enlarges shared head metadata, and inline optional monitor enlarges pre-reserved Run capacity. The companion selector excludes absent optional keys and reaches their owners when present. That probe installed no production caller or new hold binding; subsequent realization is classified separately in Validity, without rebinding original source identities.

## Interpretation

The design removes structural hazards, not measured cost: the eliminated work is replaced by ring, reverse-handle and deadline maintenance whose frequency and total cost only the frozen workloads in the performance-assurance specification §6 and generated owners can establish.

For O15, the binding dimension is shared capacity/metadata rather than actual absent head bytes. A fixed discriminator plus optional owners isolates policy-width costs, at the price of policy-bearing key/read/cleanup work. The existing head's actual-size hold must not be misreported as a maximum-policy hold tax. Existing Run capacity does have that tax if an optional monitor is embedded. The comparison does not choose numeric limits, final companion codecs, episode scope or a service coefficient.

## Decision

Accepted as the current Actors physical baseline. Decision scope: physical architecture only. Outcome class: research result, except O14, an efficiency improvement over linear page scan. Finite stopping basis: every necessary choice has one selected mechanism and one smallest falsifier, no falsifier is observed, and no alternative can add evidence before realization.

O15 selects optional Actor-keyed authored-policy and mutable monitor/current-Cycle companions, guarded by a fixed head presence discriminator. Public canonical reconstruction joins the authored companion; policy-free service skips both owners. Keep these owners outside the shared hot/Run capacity and inside existing Contract/lifecycle transactions, with no new queue. Outcome class: explicit tradeoff. Decision scope: placement only; the original comparison does not establish runtime delivery. Stop the two-candidate comparison: the inline maxima violate its frozen criterion and the companion selector passes. Current realization is scoped in Validity; remaining complete lookup/invariant/destruction costs, episode/limit applicability and production qualification belong to BACKLOG P28-08/11/20, not a reopened layout comparison.
