## Decision Question

Which single physical design carries current-state Actor semantics — process ownership, discovery, residence, rounds, wake acknowledgment, deadline placement, lifecycle and resource ownership — without legacy snapshot or Ready-queue authority?

## Context and Candidates

- `Current architecture`: The released 0.7.26 engine moved whole-cell frames through a ticket FIFO with Opening snapshots, latches, successor tickets and waiting-primary copies, coupling unrelated lifetimes.
- `Hypothesis`: One stable Actor-generation process with generation-bound memberships, one Live/Pending ring and the retained C32 deadline substrate carries every supported state with bounded structural work and no legacy authority.
- `Materiality`: Each choice must remove a named structural cost or hazard (whole-population scan, predecessor discovery, duplicate residence, lost wakeup, unowned cleanup) without semantic change; no throughput benefit is claimed.
- `Controlled / changed variables`: Semantics fixed by the Actors specification; one candidate per decision family, each with one smallest falsifier. Candidate sets were frozen and exhausted before selection.

## Workloads and Criteria

- `Evidence class`: Design-level structural analysis, finite interleaving matrices and native regressions; no production benchmark.
- `Acceptance criteria`: Every supported state has exactly one semantic owner and at most one residence; every transition has a bounded plan; every hazard has an invariant and a finite falsifier.
- `Rejection criteria`: Any required population, predecessor or deadline-range scan; duplicate or zero residence; lost acknowledgment; custody mutation by scheduling.

### Behavior Placement Comparison (2026-10-02)

- `Freeze`: O15 frozen 2026-10-02 before its deciding probe; no O1–O14 choice is reopened. The approved outcome-policy extension introduces previously absent authored policy and monitor state.
- `Candidates`: Inline optional expression in the common head plus optional monitor in the common Run versus one fixed head presence bit and optional Actor-keyed authored-policy/monitor companions. The public canonical Contract and its semantic hash remain identical; full reconstruction joins the companion. Companions remain under Contract/lifecycle ownership and create no queue or service order.
- `Controls`: The existing native mock head is the byte-identical common carrier, not a proposed public ABI. The four-by-four expression is diagnostic geometry, not a selected host limit. The existing ten-metric monitor supplies its codec ceiling. No RefTime, production Weight or exact-Wasm claim is admitted.
- `Materiality`: Reject any policy-width term in shared no-policy head metadata or monitor-width term in the shared no-policy Run capacity. Select companions only if a false presence bit performs no policy/monitor read and a true bit reaches bounded companion keys. Stop at that structural placement decision; host limits, complete lifecycle binding and cost qualification remain backlog work.

## Result

Every supported current-state transition has one semantic owner, at most one residence and a bounded structural plan. Per-Step Ready dequeue, successor ticket allocation, Ready-page growth, waiting-primary copies and latch rearm are structurally eliminated. Close is synchronous and bounded; no asynchronous cleanup debt exists. Vacancy authority cut maximum deadline destination planning from 314 reads to two; mutation tests then exposed a truncated-page indexing panic and a missing vacancy head treated as full, both fixed by shared header validation, bounded page refusal and checked C32 access without changing the design.

O15's three probes pass. Inline and companion absent-policy values have equal actual size, but inline adds the complete expression maximum to shared head metadata. Inline monitor adds its full optional maximum to the Run capacity reserved before any Run exists. The companion selector's actual recorded key set excludes both optional owners while absent and reaches exactly those owners while present. These are candidate-layout observations; no production caller or new hold binding is installed.

## Interpretation

The design removes structural hazards, not measured cost: the eliminated work is replaced by ring, reverse-handle and deadline maintenance whose frequency and total cost only the frozen workloads in the performance-assurance specification §6 and generated owners can establish.

For O15, the binding dimension is shared capacity/metadata rather than actual absent head bytes. A fixed discriminator plus optional owners isolates policy-width costs, at the price of policy-bearing key/read/cleanup work. The existing head's actual-size hold must not be misreported as a maximum-policy hold tax. Existing Run capacity does have that tax if an optional monitor is embedded. The comparison does not choose numeric limits, final companion codecs, episode scope or a service coefficient.

## Decision

Accepted as the current Actors physical baseline. Decision scope: physical architecture only. Outcome class: research result, except O14, an efficiency improvement over linear page scan. Finite stopping basis: every necessary choice has one selected mechanism and one smallest falsifier, no falsifier is observed, and no alternative can add evidence before realization.

O15 selects optional Actor-keyed authored-policy and mutable monitor/current-Cycle companions, guarded by a fixed head presence discriminator. Public canonical reconstruction joins the authored companion; policy-free service skips both owners. Keep these owners outside the shared hot/Run capacity and inside existing Contract/lifecycle transactions, with no new queue. Outcome class: explicit tradeoff. Decision scope: placement only, not installed runtime delivery. Stop the two-candidate comparison: the inline maxima violate its frozen criterion and the companion selector passes. Binding, complete lookup/invariant/destruction owners, episode authority, policy limits and resource qualification transfer to `Actors/Behavior Plan And State` and `Actors/Behavior Resource And Client Closure`.

### Pull-Model Amendment (2026-09-26)

The 0.7.27 pull-model cut removed push-reactive observation Triggers, Oracle-to-Actors ingress, shared-feed fanout, Crossing indexes and observation-gated Park. Observations are read only by fresh-only Step predicates at the check or Attempt.

- `O2`: Discovery sources are now Manual and certified AddressEvent lookup, AtTime/Cadenced deadlines, and parked-balance revisions. For parked balance, only a configured asset's checked absolute net movement from the fixed final parking anchor reaching `max(authored minimum, 100 × asset minimum)` qualifies; subthreshold changes stay parked and create no check.
- `O3`: Predicate applicability alone never returns a parked-balance Actor; a qualifying signal creates at most one check while not busy, and completion either Parks with a new anchor or returns under round rules.
- `O4`: The parked-balance episode is the only live Park producer. Its certificate also binds watched assets, the fixed anchor, direction and inclusive threshold; a negative rearm keeps the anchor. Native/fungibles adapters publish coalesced revisions and timed review covers other routes. Unreachable non-episode negative reasons are retired; remaining initial-placement and certification qualification belongs to `Actors/Parked Balance Contract Coherence`.
- `O8`: A negative parked-balance check may acknowledge the covered revision but never replaces the anchor.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: A bounded, contradiction-free physical design for current-state semantics under the governing specification; O15 additionally selects optional behavior-companion placement under its frozen codec/metadata/key-lookup criterion.
- `Does not establish`: Production Weight soundness, fairness under load, throughput, or net benefit over the released engine.
- `Invalidation triggers`: A change to residence, round, acknowledgment, lifecycle or custody semantics; a new discovery source; a reachable scan or duplicate residence. O15 reopens if required behavior semantics force policy/monitor reads while absent or variable policy/monitor width into shared no-policy capacity/metadata.
- `Passive Idle amendment`: An owner-selected semantic change now removes completed unlatched Idle processes from Service, reusing Protocol Disabled or independent window Deadline and preserving temporal/ParkedBalance discovery. The sealed finite witness plus repetition invariant exposed unowned quiet work. This amends residence scope; earlier timing/quiet-state results remain historical. Current functional and diagnostic placement evidence belongs to the package architecture, with complete physical pricing and other refusal backing still open. No throughput improvement or production coefficient is accepted.
- `O15 reassessment`: Authored `on_unfunded` adds one constant byte to the common Contract/head carrier. O15's original absolute sizes and source identities remain historical; its variable-width differences and absent-owner criterion are unchanged. The same diagnostic geometry tests pass on the new carrier. This preserves placement authority only, not old head hold values, metadata identity or physical resource evidence; Gate B owns the new constant cost and final geometry.
- `O15 selector realization`: Rust now replaces the head's one-byte legacy completion enum with fixed `has_close_when`; absent authored policy remains a one-byte option. The opaque-carrier geometry probes still preserve their relative width/key observations. The common discriminator and cold expression/plan owner are now installed. Native Contract-geometry tests cover semantic/admission binding, corruption and destruction; an actual absent-policy full loader records no optional key. The required-only mutable owner and lifecycle episode anchor are now installed too; native geometry tests cover re-certification, replacement, rollback, actual User holds and selected key sets. The internal current-Step transaction now reduces/commits actual observations and selects policy Close; prepared native fixtures cover permitted Action, clean/zero-Step completion and late-refusal rollback. Public policy admission and physical qualification remain open. Original measurements remain bound to their recorded sources; no former physical evidence or policy success is rehabilitated by the field replacement.
