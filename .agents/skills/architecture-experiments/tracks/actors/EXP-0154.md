## Decision Question

Can the real runtime's mandatory Prepass admit more than two 12-position ObservationChange turns within one block at exactly one-third Actor Control, retain the cursor for a later block, and leave Shared Economic untouched for effect-free System Actors, without rebinding production Weight/Wasm?

## Governing Specification

- `Exact section`: `docs/actors-resource-policy.specification.en.md` §§2–3 and `template/pallets/actors/docs/specification.en.md` ObservationChange causality, materialization and B+1 Service.
- `Invariant`: Each paid quantum retains the authoritative feed/page/position; repeated turns in a block require independent RefTime and ProofSize admission. Deadline precedes materialization, the family cursor rotates, and Actor Control cannot borrow from Shared Economic or exceed one third.
- `Reopen trigger`: A candidate needs duplicated scheduler authority, pre-extrinsic execution, lost committed prefixes, or an Actor Control share above one third to make progress.

## Context and Hypothesis

- `Current architecture`: The production Prepass still passes a full 64-slot page and generated full-page Weight to the shared coordinator. EXP-0153 measured a singleton maximum-header Q12 continuation but did not run full Prepass. The generic mock's mandatory owners cost `1,015,061` ProofSize, already above DEOS Control `676,150`, so it cannot certify this runtime phase.
- `Reason`: Worker-level two-turn algebra and family rotation omit actual Deadline settlement, materialization grant, next-block Service and resource-ledger coupling.
- `Hypothesis`: With a diagnostic-only `12`/`20,000,000,000 ps + 185,000 ProofSize` ordinary unit, the real runtime Prepass admits three turns in one block and resumes a partial page the next block under its unchanged one-third budget.
- `Materiality`: A complete-phase positive witness can eliminate a structural impossibility hypothesis for the selected state. It cannot establish a universal production-safe ordinary upper, Crossing coexistence, failure Weight or release feasibility.

- `Metrics`: Actual Prepass RefTime/ProofSize ledger, Control limit, Shared Economic usage, subscribers latched, cursor position, next-block Service eligibility and family rotation.
- `Statistical method`: Deterministic native test, one state transition per block; no wall-clock timing or production PoV measurement. The numbers are generated-Weight accounting for this exact workload, not physical worst-case estimates.
- `Acceptance criteria`: More than two Q12 turns admit in B1 while both Control dimensions fit one third, exactly the processed prefix latches with B+1 eligibility, unprocessed positions retain the cursor, and B2 completes them without charging effect-free System work to Shared Economic.
- `Rejection criteria`: Fewer than three turns, lost/duplicated readiness, Control limit exceeded, Shared Economic borrowed for fanout, or failure to resume under a valid second-block context.

## Result

The benchmark-only entry delegates to the real Prepass implementation, including required context, cutoff, Block/Tick Deadline attempt, dependency scan, materialization coordinator, Service and resource ledger. In B1 it processed positions `0..36`, left `36..40` dirty and booked `646,747` Control ProofSize, with no Shared Economic usage. The family cursor rotated. In B2 it consumed the four remaining subscribers and published eligibility at block 3, with `624,659` Control ProofSize. The production `actor_prepass` inherent was not changed.

## Interpretation

The selected workload refutes the claim that the complete Prepass structure itself imposes a twelve- or twenty-four-subscriber block cap. It does not certify three-turn worst-case throughput: current generated non-fanout owners, ordinary System headers and an optimistic experimental Q12 unit produce the observed grant. The independent Crossing family minimum plus mandatory owners still exceeds the normative cap; their coexistence and rotated service remain open. Runtime-only feature-gated admission is not an authorization to rebind production Weight/Wasm.

## Decision

Inconclusive for production Q12 selection. Retain the diagnostic Prepass seam and the exact two-block witness as a research result; stop this System singleton fixture. Before production rebinding, prove complete component-wise fanout selectors, worst-case mandatory/Crossing/Service compatibility and both-dimensional runtime admission with the chosen certified upper.

## Validity

- `Establishes`: Complete runtime Prepass can process 36 selected subscribers as three twelve-position turns inside one Control third, carry the four-position suffix into the next block, preserve B+1 Service and charge no Shared Economic on this effect-free System fixture.
- `Does not establish`: Production inherent Q12 behavior, universal ordinary/failure upper, maximum-header User Prepass, competing Crossing, guaranteed Service under market Actions, complete PoV or production Wasm validation.
- `Known limitations`: Direct benchmark-only Prepass method bypasses inherent origin admission, although it executes the same internal Prepass body; the runtime-benchmarks feature differs from the production build. These System Contracts are short and effect-free; no user fees, maximum funded/watch headers or saturated page topology.
- `Invalidation triggers`: Host runtime/fixed context, SDK, page/ledger storage, generated Weight, causal eligibility or benchmark-feature composition changes.
- `Residual qualitative insight`: The bound belongs to a paid turn and available Control budget, not a hard twelve-position block cap; when the third turn fits, the real Prepass takes it.
